Recall

Magnetopia Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion hazard.

WhatMagnetopia Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks
The hazardSerious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion hazard
Sold atSold Online At: Magnetopia.comfrom April 2025 through October 2026 for between $20 and $40.
ContactMagnetopia by email at toyrecall78945@outlook.com or online at recall.magnetopia.com for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27076)

What was recalled

This recall involves Magnetopia-branded Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks, which are magnetic cubes sold in sets of 60, 100, 150 and 200. The cubes contain small, powerful magnets that allow the cubes to stick together and create 3D designs. The sets are available in a variety of themed building/landscape designs and colors.
