My 1st Years Table Top Houses Recall (June 2026)

The products present a risk of injuries and choking.

WhatMy 1st Years Table Top Houses
BrandMy 1st Years
The hazardThe products present a risk of injuries and choking because small, sharp parts can easily detach.
Product typeToys - Dolls Building/Settings
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-02 (notice 2605-0156)

What was recalled

Pastel coloured themed wooden houses.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a high risk of injuries because the front roof, screws and hinges can detach during play. The detached screws have sharp points and may cause injuries. The screws are also small parts, and therefore pose a choking hazard to young children, particularly those under 36 months of age who have a natural tendency to explore objects by placing them in their mouths.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The products have been withdrawn from the market and recalled by My 1st Years.

We recommend that consumers stop using this product immediately and keep it out of reach of young children. To register for a refund, please follow the instructions provided at: https://www.my1styears.com/product-recalls-safety-notices

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0156

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
