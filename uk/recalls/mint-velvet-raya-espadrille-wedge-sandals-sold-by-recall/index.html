Recall

Mint Velvet Raya Espadrille Wedge Sandals sold by Next Recall (October 2026)

Risk of injuries as the footbed can become detached from the wedge heel during use.

WhatMint Velvet Raya Espadrille Wedge Sandals sold by Next
BrandMint Velvet
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries as the footbed can become detached from the wedge heel during use.
Product typeClothing, textiles and fashion items - Shoes - General Purpose
Made inUnknown
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-13

What was recalled

Light tan and brown wedge sandals.

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk of injuries as it has been identified that the footbed can become dislodged from the wedge heel. If the footbed unexpectedly became dislodged during use, the user could fall leading to injuries.

The products do not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Next.

Next have contacted consumers who purchased the heels directly. Owners are advised to stop using the product, and follow the instructions sent by Next to arrange for a refund or replacement.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0072 and 2610-0192

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
