NTI and HTP Brand Gas Boilers Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire, Carbon Monoxide (CO) Poisoning and Impact hazard — about 4,726 (In addition, 2,261 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatNTI and HTP Brand Gas Boilers
How manyAbout 4,726 (In addition, 2,261 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe condensate dish inside the boiler can crack during a delayed ignition, allowing unburned gas to build up inside the sealed cabinet. If the gas ignites, the front cover can suddenly detach, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire, carbon monoxide (CO) poisoning, and impact hazards.
Sold atSold At: Independent heating and plumbing contractors and wholesale distributors nationwide from May 2021 through July 2026 for between $3,200 and $7,700.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled boilers immediately and contact Ariston to schedule a free repair by a certified technician. Consumers who must continue using the boiler while awaiting repair should have working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms in their homes.
ContactAriston at 800-688-2575 from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at VCexchanger@ariston.com or online at https://ntiboilers.com/safety-recalls or www.ntiboilers.com and click on “RECALLS” at the top of the page or https://htproducts.com/safety-notice.html or www.htproducts.com and click on “URGENT RECALL” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27051)

What was recalled

This recall involves NTI brand residential gas boilers in the FTVN and TFTN model series, and HTP brand residential gas boilers in the EFTU model series. The NTI FTVN and TFTN series boilers are gray, while the HTP EFTU (Elite Fire Tube Ultra) boilers are black. The boilers are metal, rectangular units and have either NTI or HTP printed on the front.
