HARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stools Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury and Death from Entrapment and Fall hazard — about 47,166 units.

WhatHARPPA Nordi Toddler Tower Stools
How manyAbout 47,166 units
The hazardThe recalled tower stools can collapse or tip over while in use, and a child's torso can fit through the openings on the front and back sides, posing a risk of serious injury and death due to tip over, fall and entrapment hazards.
Sold atOnline at Harppababy.com and Amazon.com from September 2023 through June 2026 for about $130.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled stools immediately and store them away from children. Contact HARPPA for a free repair kit, including shipping. The repair kit consists of protective nets, stabilizing feet and installation instructions.
ContactHARPPA toll-free at 833-471-9321 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT, Monday through Friday, email at recall@harppababy.com, or https://harppababy.com/recall or https://harppababy.com/ and click on "Safety Recall" at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-07-30 (recall no. 26651)

What was recalled

This recall involves HARPPA's Nordi foldable toddler towers. The tower stools are white, black and blue with natural wood color on the stool's step, platform and safety bars. The tower stools measure about 18 inches deep, 18 inches wide and 38.6 inches tall. "MODEL No.: HANS0002" is printed on a label on the underside of the stool's platform.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled stools immediately and store them away from children. Contact HARPPA for a free repair kit, including shipping. The repair kit consists of protective nets, stabilizing feet and installation instructions..
