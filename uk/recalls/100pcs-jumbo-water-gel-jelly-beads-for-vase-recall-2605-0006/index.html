Recall

100pcs Jumbo Water Gel Jelly Beads for Vase, Round Gel Beads, 9-11mm sold via Shein Recall (June 2026)

Serious risk of asphyxiation.

What100pcs Jumbo Water Gel Jelly Beads for Vase, Round Gel Beads, 9-11mm sold via Shein
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation because if the product is swallowed in its hydrated form, it can block a young child’s airway.
Product typeDecorative articles/Homeware/Gardenware – Flower/Plant Pots Replacement Parts/Accessories
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-03 (notice 2605-0006)

What was recalled

100pcs pack of water beads for use in vase, supplied in plastic packaging.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation because if the product is swallowed in its hydrated form, it can block a young child’s airway.

OPSS has published a Product Safety Alert for water beads, which can be accessed at: https://www.gov.uk/product-safety-alerts-reports-recalls/product-safety-alert-water-beads-psa7

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by SHEIN.

We recommend that consumers stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of children. Affected users have been contacted directly by the marketplace.

The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (Shein).

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0006

Notifying Authority: Office for Product Safety and Standards
