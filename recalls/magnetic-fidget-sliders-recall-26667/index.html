Recall

Magnetic Fidget Sliders Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion — about 860 units.

WhatMagnetic Fidget Sliders
How manyAbout 860 units
The hazardThe magnetic fidget sliders violate the mandatory standard for toys because they can liberate loose high-powered magnets posing an ingestion hazard to children. When high-powered magnets are swallowed, the ingested magnets can attract each other or other metal objects and become lodged in the digestive system. This can result in perforations, twisting, and/or blockage of the intestines, blood poisoning and death.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from October 2025 through May 2026 for about $10.
RemedyConsumers should take the fidget sliders away from children, stop using them immediately and contact PIXLABBY for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the fidget sliders and submit a photo of the disposed toys to ah1998zxc@gmail.com to receive a refund.
ContactPIXLABBY by email at ah1998zxc@gmail.com.
Recall date2026-08-06 (recall no. 26667)

What was recalled

This recall involves PIXLABBY's 6-PCS silicone magnetic fidget sliders. The fidget sliders come in various two-tone colors, yellow/red, pink/yellow, orange/purple, blue/orange, green/blue, and red/green. Each slider has a different silicone texture on the top and bottom surfaces. The fidget sliders make a clicking sound as you slide them. The "PIXABBY" logo is on the front of the box.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should take the fidget sliders away from children, stop using them immediately and contact PIXLABBY for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the fidget sliders and submit a photo of the disposed toys to ah1998zxc@gmail.com to receive a refund.. PIXLABBY by email at ah1998zxc@gmail.com.
