Recall

Style Homeware Emerspring Mattresses Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 263 units.

WhatStyle Homeware Emerspring Mattresses
How manyAbout 263 units
The hazardThe recalled mattresses violate the mandatory flammability standard for mattresses, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from January 2026 through May 2026 for $226. · Style Homeware, Inc., of Monrovia, California
RemedyRepair
ContactStyle Homeware email at EmerspringMattressRecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-09-24 (recall no. 26796)

What was recalled

This recall involves Emerspring Mattresses. The mattresses are 12 inches thick and sold in queen size. They have a white top and white sides with a green trim. They were sold compressed in a box. “Prototype ID: EMERS-12” and the manufacture date “6/2025” are printed on a sewn-in white label located on one side of the mattress.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Repair. Style Homeware email at EmerspringMattressRecall@outlook.com.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-09-24. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
