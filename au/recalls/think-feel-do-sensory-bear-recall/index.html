Think, Feel, Do Sensory Bear Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or death by choking.

WhatThink, Feel, Do Sensory Bear
The hazardThere is a risk of serious injury or death by choking if small pellets are released from the toy and a child places them in their mouth. An incident of seam failure has occurred.
Sold atCurijo website, trade show booths.
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale6 May 2021 - 5 December 2025
Recalled byCURIJO PTY LTD
Made inAustralia
ContactCurijo Call: 02 6285 4802 Email: info@curijo.com.au Visit: https://www.curijo.com.au/contact-us/
CategoryStuffed and plush toys · Toys for babies and toddlers
Recall date2026-07-09

What was recalled

Weighted bear used as a sensory tool.

Why it was recalled

The product does not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age.

The seams of the bear may separate and release the small weighted pellets.

What to do

  1. Stop using the bear immediately and place it out of reach of children.
  2. Contact info@curijo.com.au to arrange to return the product and receive a full refund, or if you have any questions about the recall.
