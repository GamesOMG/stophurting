Baby Brezza Enterprises SuperFast Sterilizer Dryers Recall (November 2026)

Overheating and Burn hazard — about 29,000 (In addition, about 4,500 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatBaby Brezza Enterprises SuperFast Sterilizer Dryers
How manyAbout 29,000 (In addition, about 4,500 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled sterilizer dryers can overheat when plugged in and operating, posing a risk of injury from a burn hazard if the surface is handled.
Sold atSold At: TJ Maxx and Marshall’s storesnationwide and online at babybrezza.com, target.com and babylist.com from January 2024 through October 2026 for between $160 and $180.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled sterilizer dryers immediately and contact Baby Brezza Enterprises for a free replacement sterilizer dryer, including shipping at no cost to the consumer. Consumers will be asked to register at https://babybrezza.com/pages/about?view=v3-recalls, complete the recall form, unplug the product, cut the power cord to destroy the sterilizer dryer and upload photos. Send photos of the model number on the label of the dryer the destroyed appliance and the return authorization number to recall@babybrezza.com. After uploading the photos, consumers should dispose of the recalled sterilizer dryers in accordance with local and state ordinances.
ContactBaby Brezza Enterprises toll-free at 888-527-9824 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at recall@babybrezza.com or online at https://babybrezza.com/pages/about?view=v3-recalls and www.babybrezza.com and click "Product Safety Recall" at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27072)

What was recalled

This recall involves the SuperFast Sterilizer Dryer, a countertop appliance that uses a push-button control to sterilize and dry baby bottles, breast pump parts and accessories in ten minutes. The recalled units have model numbers BRZ0083, BRZ0154 and BRZ0156. The model number is printed on the product label located on the bottom of the unit.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled sterilizer dryers immediately and contact Baby Brezza Enterprises for a free replacement sterilizer dryer, including shipping at no cost to the consumer. Consumers will be asked to register at https://babybrezza.com/pages/about?view=v3-recalls, complete the recall form, unplug the product, cut the power cord to destroy the sterilizer dryer and upload photos. Send photos of the model number on the label of the dryer the destroyed appliance and the return authorization number to recall@babybrezza.com. After uploading the photos, consumers should dispose of the recalled sterilizer dryers in accordance with local and state ordinances..
