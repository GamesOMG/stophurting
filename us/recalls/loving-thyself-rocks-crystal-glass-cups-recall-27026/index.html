Recall

Loving Thyself Rocks Crystal Glass Cups Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury from Ingestion and Laceration hazard — about 600 units.

WhatLoving Thyself Rocks Crystal Glass Cups
How manyAbout 600 units
The hazardGlass can break inside the recalled cups while in use, posing a risk of serious injury from ingestion and laceration hazards.
Sold atSold At: Loving Thyselfstore and on-line athttps://www.instagram.com/lovingthyselfrocksandfossils/?hl=en andhttps://www.instagram.com/lovingthyselfrocks/?hl=en and Etsy.com from April 2026 through September 2026 for between $20 and $50.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled crystal cup immediately and register at http://www.lovingthyselfrocks.com/recall to receive a full refund in the form of original payment. Consumer will be asked to fill out an online form and submit a photograph with the word “RECALL” written on the front of the crystal cup in permanent black marker. Consumers will be asked to attest to proper disposal of the recalled cup following the instructions on the firm’s website.
ContactLoving Thyself Rocks LLC by email at lovingthyselfrocks@gmail.com or online at https://lovingthyselfrocks.com/recall or click on https://lovingthyselfrocks.com and go to “Safety Recall” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-15 (recall no. 27026)

What was recalled

This recall affects Crystal Glass Cups containing loose crystal stone chips between two layers of glass. The cups are intended for both decorative use and drinking. Each cup measures approximately 3.5 by 3 inches. The crystal stone chips are sold in various options: amethyst, black obsidian, rose quartz, smoke quartz, fire and clear quartz, moss agate, hematite, black tourmaline, black tourmaline in quartz, charoite, blue apatite, morganite, larimar, red jasper, malachite, garnet, kunzite, fluorite, peridot, aquamarine, watermelon tourmaline, carnelian, lapis lazuli, tigers eye and labradorite.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled crystal cup immediately and register at http://www.lovingthyselfrocks.com/recall to receive a full refund in the form of original payment. Consumer will be asked to fill out an online form and submit a photograph with the word “RECALL” written on the front of the crystal cup in permanent black marker. Consumers will be asked to attest to proper disposal of the recalled cup following the instructions on the firm’s website..
