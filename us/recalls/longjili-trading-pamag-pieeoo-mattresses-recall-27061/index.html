Recall

Longjili Trading Pamag Pieeoo Mattresses Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 50 units.

WhatLongjili Trading Pamag Pieeoo Mattresses
How manyAbout 50 units
The hazardThe recalled mattresses violate the mandatory flammability standard for mattresses, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com in March 2026 for between $80 and $95. · Longjili Trading (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Longjili Trading for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED,” on the top surface of the mattress and send a photo of the marked mattress to Pamagpieeoo@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactLongjili Trading by email at Pamagpieeoo@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27061)

What was recalled

This recall involves Pamag Pieeoo Mattresses. The mattresses are 8 inches thick and were sold in a twin-XL size. They have a white top and white sides with a gray curve pattern. They were sold compressed in a box. The date of manufacture and “Model: BFC8HMBYTX-US” and care instructions are printed on a sewn-in white label located on one side of the mattress.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Longjili Trading for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED,” on the top surface of the mattress and send a photo of the marked mattress to Pamagpieeoo@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. Longjili Trading by email at Pamagpieeoo@outlook.com.
