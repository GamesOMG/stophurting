Recall

Diamond Wipes International MD Wipe Outz Numbing Tattoo Wipes Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Child Poisoning — about 55,250 units.

WhatDiamond Wipes International MD Wipe Outz Numbing Tattoo Wipes
How manyAbout 55,250 units
The hazardThe recalled numbing wipes contain lidocaine, which must be in child-resistant packaging, as required by the Poison Prevention Packaging Act. The packaging of the wipes is not child-resistant, posing a risk of serious injury or death from poisoning if the contents of the wipes are ingested by young children.
Sold atMD Wipe Outz, of Thousand Oaks, California
RemedyConsumers should secure the wipes out of sight and reach of children immediately and contact Diamond Wipes for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the packet and send a photo of the marked packet to recall@diamondwipes.com. Consumers should then dispose of the packets.
ContactDiamond Wipes toll-free at 800-454-1077 from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at recall@diamondwipes.com, or online at https://diamondwipes.com/pages/re-call or https://diamondwipes.com/ and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-08-27 (recall no. 26718)

What was recalled

This recall involves MD Wipe Outz Numbing Tattoo Wipes. The pain-relieving wipes were sold in individually wrapped 3 x 4 inches foil packets in boxes of 5, 10, 25, and 250. The Wipe Outz logo, “NUMB” and “Lidocaine Infused” is printed on the front of the packet.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should secure the wipes out of sight and reach of children immediately and contact Diamond Wipes for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the packet and send a photo of the marked packet to recall@diamondwipes.com. Consumers should then dispose of the packets.. Diamond Wipes toll-free at 800-454-1077 from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at recall@diamondwipes.com, or online at https://diamondwipes.com/pages/re-call or https://diamondwipes.com/ and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
