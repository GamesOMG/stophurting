Fidlock GmbH Hermetic Dry Bags Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion hazard — about 490 units.

WhatFidlock GmbH Hermetic Dry Bags
How manyAbout 490 units
The hazardThe recalled bags’ adhesive closure strips can detach, causing the magnets to fall out, posing a risk of serious injury or death from a magnet ingestion hazard.
Sold atSold At: Sports, water sports and outdoor stores nationwide and online at Fidlock.us and Amazon.com from January 2025 through September 2026 for between $30 and $35.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the bags immediately and contact Fidlock USA to receive a replacement bag or a gift card for the Fidlock USA online shop. Customers who purchased the product from the FIDLOCK online store can receive a full refund in the form of a credit card refund or PayPal refund. Consumers will be asked to go to www.fidlock.us/pages/recall and send a photo of the product. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactFidlock USA toll-free at 888-303-8332 from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday, email at recall_2608@fidlock.com, or online at www.fidlock.us/pages/recall or www.fidlock.us and click on “More Recall Information” for more information.
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27093)

What was recalled

This recall involves Fidlock Hermetic dry bags. The medium-sized, watertight plastic bags are clear and measure about 8 inches tall by 4.6 inches wide. They have a blue closure strip with magnets at the top and a black lanyard. “Fidlock” is printed in white on the front of the strip and lot number 2409140930201 is on the back. “Hermetic” is printed on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the bags immediately and contact Fidlock USA to receive a replacement bag or a gift card for the Fidlock USA online shop. Customers who purchased the product from the FIDLOCK online store can receive a full refund in the form of a credit card refund or PayPal refund. Consumers will be asked to go to www.fidlock.us/pages/recall and send a photo of the product. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. Fidlock USA toll-free at 888-303-8332 from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday, email at recall_2608@fidlock.com, or online at www.fidlock.us/pages/recall or www.fidlock.us and click on “More Recall Information” for more information.
