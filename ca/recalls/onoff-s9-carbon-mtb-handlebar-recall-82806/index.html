ONOFF S9 Carbon MTB handlebar Recall (October 2026)

Fall and injury hazards.

WhatONOFF S9 Carbon MTB handlebar
BrandOnOff
The hazardfall and injury hazards
CategorySports and fitness
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled by​​Manufacturer​ Blue Factory Team, S.L.U. Elche (Alicante), Spain
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-10-16

What was recalled

This recall involves certain Mondraker mountain bicycles equiped with ONOFF S9 Carbon MTB handlebars. The bicycle serial number is located on the underside of the bottom bracket area of the frame.

Why it was recalled

The ONOFF S9 Carbon MTB handlebar can crack or fracture during use. If the handlebar breaks while riding, the rider may lose control of the bicycle, posing a fall hazard that may result in serious injury or death.

As of ​September 23, 2026​, the company has received no reports of breakage or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact an authorized Mondraker dealer to schedule a handlebar replacement. Consumers can verify whether their bicycle is affected through the serial number verification tool provided by Mondraker.

For more information, consumers can contact Mondraker HQ by email at info@ogc.ca or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 52 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from ​December 2024​ to ​June 2026​.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in ​Taiwan​
