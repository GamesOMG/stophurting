Recall

Toyworld168 Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Water Bead Ingestion — about 276 units.

WhatToyworld168 Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys
How manyAbout 276 units
The hazardThe squishy bun toys violate the mandatory safety standard for toys because the water beads inside the dumplings expand larger than permitted, posing a deadly ingestion hazard. If a water bead is ingested, it can pose ingestion, choking and intestinal obstruction hazards inside a child’s body, resulting in severe discomfort, vomiting, dehydration and a risk of death to a child.
Sold atSold At: Various third-party stores nationwide from June 2026 through July 2026 for about $3.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the squishy dumpling toys immediately and contact Toyworld168 for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” in permanent marker on the squeezy dumpling and its tan plastic container, throw the toy away and email a photo of the disposed toy to toyworld16850@gmail.com.
ContactBy email at Toyworld168 at toyworld16850@gmail.com.
Recall date2026-09-17 (recall no. 26786)

What was recalled

This recall involves Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun Toys. The squishy bun toys are a translucent green color, containing glitter and water beads. The toys have a cartoon face and are packaged in a tan plastic container shaped like a bamboo steamer. “Rainbow MYSTERY” and “SUGAR EDITION” are printed on the front of the removable package labeling. There are no markings on the product, other than the cartoon face.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the squishy dumpling toys immediately and contact Toyworld168 for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” in permanent marker on the squeezy dumpling and its tan plastic container, throw the toy away and email a photo of the disposed toy to toyworld16850@gmail.com.. By email at Toyworld168 at toyworld16850@gmail.com.
