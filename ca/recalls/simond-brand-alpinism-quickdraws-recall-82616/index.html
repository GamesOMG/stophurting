Simond brand Alpinism quickdraws Recall (September 2026)

Injury hazard.

WhatSimond brand Alpinism quickdraws
BrandSimond marketed by Decathlon
The hazardinjury hazard
CategorySports and fitness
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byManufacturer Decathlon SE Villeneuve D'Ascq France
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-15

What was recalled

This recall involves Simond brand ultra-light Alpinism quickdraws, blue and purple, 11 cm and 17 cm long, model numbers #8589547 and # 8589548, marketed by Decathlon Canada.

Why it was recalled

There is a defect on the riveting of the finger wire of the quickdraw. This assembly defect can lead to the breakage of the wire finger and create a risk of breakage of the carabiner in the event of a fall.

Do not use your quickdraw without having done the necessary verification by visiting the site: https://simondrecallquickdraw.fandi.fr/en

As of September 8, 2026, the company has not received any reports of incidents or injuries in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact Decathlon Canada for a refund.

For more information, consumers may contact Decathlon Canada Customer Service by phone at 1-844-533-3322 from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. ET Monday through Friday or by email at onlineservice@decathlon.ca.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number sold

The company reported that 1,078 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from January 2022 to July 2026.

Place of origin

Manufactured  in France
