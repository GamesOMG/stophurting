If a page here is wrong

Tell us and we will fix it. Every recall page is built from the official CPSC notice and links to it — if this site disagrees with that notice, this site is wrong. Include the page address and what is incorrect, and the correction gets made.

If you own a recalled product

Please contact the company running the recall, not us. Their phone number, email or website is listed in the Contact row on each recall page, taken straight from the CPSC notice. We are not the manufacturer, the retailer or the CPSC, and we cannot arrange a refund, a repair or a replacement for you.

If you are the company being recalled

We publish the CPSC's public record and link to it. If the notice itself has been updated or withdrawn, point us at the updated notice and we will match it.
