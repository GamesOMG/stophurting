Recall

Stihl Ride-on Lawn Mowers Recall (May 2026)

Risk of fire.

WhatStihl Ride-on Lawn Mowers
BrandStihl
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire because leaks may occur in the fuel system of the EVC 7000 engine.
Product typeMachinery - Lawn Mowers/Rakers (Powered)
Made inAustria
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-29 (notice 2605-0119)

What was recalled

Orange and white ride-on lawn mowers.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire because leaks may occur in the fuel system of the EVC 7000 engine. If fuel escapes through the leaks, it could ignite and catch fire.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Stihl LTD.

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately. To determine if your mower is affected by this recall or for more information, please visit: Recalls - STIHL

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0119

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
