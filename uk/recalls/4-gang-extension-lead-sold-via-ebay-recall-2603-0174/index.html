Recall

4 Gang Extension Lead sold via eBay Recall (June 2026)

Serious risk of electric shock.

What4 Gang Extension Lead sold via eBay
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of electric shock because it is not earthed.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment - Extension/Power Supply Cords
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-12 (notice 2603-0174)

What was recalled

Extension lead with 4 sockets and black switch. Sold in a plastic bag.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of electric shock because it is not earthed. Under fault conditions, this means accessible parts of the product could become live. If the user touches these parts, they could receive an electric shock.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by A&R Connections.

We recommend you cease use of this product immediately and contact A&R Connections via 07727 094756 for next steps.

The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (eBay).

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2603-0173

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
