Glow Crown Headbands with LED battery pack Recall (July 2026)

Children are at risk of choking, serious injury or death.

WhatGlow Crown Headbands with LED battery pack
BrandFizz Kidz
The hazardChildren are at risk of choking, serious injury or death if they swallow or place button or coin batteries inside their bodies. If you suspect a child has swallowed or inserted a button or coin battery: Call Triple Zero (000) if your child is bleeding or having any difficulty breathing. Call the Po…
Sold atFizz Kidz
Where it was soldNationally
On sale30 March 2026 - 27 July 2026
Recalled byFIZZ KIDZ AUSTRALIA PTY LTD
Made inChina
ContactFizz Kidz Phone: 03 9059 8144 Email: kym@fizzkidz.com.au
CategoryButton batteries · Arts and crafts toys
Recall date2026-07-31

What was recalled

LED battery pack used in a children’s craft activity at Fizz Kidz to create glow crown headbands.

Why it was recalled

The battery pack doesn’t comply with the mandatory standard for products containing button/coin batteries.

The battery isn’t secured, and children could access the button/coin battery.

What to do

  1. Stop using the affected product immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Remove the LED battery pack from the crown and place in a secure container or sealed bag. Do not open the battery casing or attempt to remove the batteries.
  3. Return the battery pack to any Fizz Kidz studio or dispose of it properly through your local battery or e-waste recycling facility.
  4. Fizz Kidz will provide a free replacement with a compliant battery pack.
  5. Contact Fizz Kids if you have any questions or need assistance returning the battery pack.
