TU Tote Shopper Bags Recall (September 2026)

The products present a serious chemical risk.

WhatTU Tote Shopper Bags
BrandTU
The hazardThe products present a serious chemical risk because they contain excessive concentrations of phthalates.
Product typeClothing, textiles, fashion items – Rucksacks/Backpacks/Holdalls
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-28 (notice 2609-0173)

What was recalled

Three shopper bags in brown, tan, and black.

Why it was recalled

The products present a serious chemical risk because they contain excessive concentrations of phthalates. Phthalates may harm the health of children, potentially causing damage to the reproductive system. 

The products do not meet the requirements of the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by Argos and Sainsbury’s.

We recommend that you stop using the products immediately and return them to your nearest Sainsbury’s or Argos store for a full refund. For further details, or if you have any questions, please visit the websites below:

  • Sainsburys.co.uk/help or 0800 028 8303

  • Argos Safety Product Recalls or 0800 011 3462

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0173

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
