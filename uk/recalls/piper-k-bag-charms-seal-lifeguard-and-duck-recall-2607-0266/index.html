Recall

Piper K Bag Charms – Seal Lifeguard and Duck in Dungarees sold by TK Maxx Recall (November 2026)

Risk of choking.

WhatPiper K Bag Charms – Seal Lifeguard and Duck in Dungarees sold by TK Maxx
The hazardThe product presents a risk of choking because the metal spring clip can detach, creating a small part.
Product typeDecorative articles/Homeware/Gardenware - Key Rings
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-02 (notice 2607-0266)

What was recalled

Crochet seal on a keyring with a buoyancy aid and crochet duck in blue dungarees.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of choking because the metal spring clip detached at a force below the required minimum, which caused the connecting O-ring to open. If removed from the fabric, the O-ring is a small part which fits wholly within a small parts cylinder. A young child may place the small part in their mouth, where it could obstruct the airway and lead to choking. 

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by TK Maxx.  

We recommend that you stop using the product immediately and keep it away from children. Return the product to any TK Maxx store for a full refund or a replacement. For more information, please access: https://www.tkmaxx.com/uk/en/l/product-recall

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0266

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
