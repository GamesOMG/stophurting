Fotopro Bluetooth smartphone shutter remote Recall (July 2026)

Children are at risk of choking, severe internal burn injuries or death.

WhatFotopro Bluetooth smartphone shutter remote
BrandFotopro
The hazardChildren are at risk of choking, severe internal burn injuries or death if they swallow or place button/coin batteries inside their bodies. CategoryButton batteries · Phones, cameras and accessories
Recall date2026-07-10

What was recalled

Bluetooth remote that connects to smartphones to take photographs.

  • BT-4 model
  • contains button battery

Why it was recalled

The remote doesn’t comply with the mandatory standards for products containing button/coin batteries.

The battery isn’t secured, and children could access the button/coin battery.

The packaging is missing warning information about

  • the dangers of button/coin batteries to children
  • what to do when a person suspects someone has swallowed or placed a button/coin battery inside any part of their body.

What to do

  1. Stop using the remote immediately and keep out of reach of children.
  2. Contact Plaza Cameras Pty Ltd to arrange a full refund.
