Recall

Meat products Recall (July 2026)

Undeclared mustard and soy.

WhatMeat products
BrandThe Butcher Barn
The hazardundeclared mustard and soy
CategoryProcessed
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byRecalling firm: The Butcher Barn
Recall classClass 1
Issued byCanadian Food Inspection Agency
Recall date2026-07-09

What was recalled

Brand Product Size UPC Codes The Butcher Barn Hotdog Variable (~1 lb) Starts with 0 200227 All codes where mustard is not declared on the label The Butcher Barn Mild Beef Jerky Variable (~1/2 lb) Starts with 0 200226 All codes where mustard and soy are not declared on the label The Butcher Barn “Teryaki” Pepperette Variable (~1 lb) Starts with 0 200056 All codes where mustard and soy are not declared on the label

Why it was recalled

The affected products are being recalled from the marketplace because they contain mustard and soy which are not declared on the label.

What to do

  • Check to see if you have recalled products
  • Do not consume recalled products to which you are allergic or sensitive as they may cause a serious or life-threatening reaction
  • Do not serve, use, sell or distribute recalled products
  • Recalled products should be thrown out or returned to the location where they were purchased

Learn more:

  • Learn more about common food allergies
  • Sign up for recall notifications by email and follow us on social media
  • View our detailed explanation of the food safety investigation and recall process
  • Report a food safety or labelling concern

Background

This recall was triggered by inspection activities.

There have been no reported reactions associated with the consumption of these products.
