Recall

Junbright Ltd Vittanly Gaming Chair sold via Amazon and AliExpress Recall (August 2026)

Risk of fire.

WhatJunbright Ltd Vittanly Gaming Chair sold via Amazon and AliExpress
BrandJunbright Ltd
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire because the cover fabric and sheet foam does not meet flammability requirements.
Product typeFurniture - Household/Office Chairs/Stools (Non Powered)
Made inChina
Risk levelMedium
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-11 (notice 2607-0300)

What was recalled

Black and white models of faux leather gaming chair, supplied in black cardboard packaging.

Why it was recalled

The product’s cover fabric and sheet foam failed the flammability requirements of Schedule 5 Part 1 of the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988 (as amended). In the event of exposure to an ignition source, the furniture may ignite and burn more readily, increasing the risk of fire and resulting in serious injuries or fatalities.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Junbright Ltd.

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately, and contact the distributor they purchased from for instructions on return of the product and refund/redress.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2607-0300

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
