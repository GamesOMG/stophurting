SUNS International Residential Elevator Interlock Switches Recall (June 2026)

Fall and Crushing hazard — about About 7,000 units.

WhatSUNS International Residential Elevator Interlock Switches
How manyAbout About 7,000 units
The hazardThe affected interlock switches can become stuck in a retracted position, allowing the exterior door to remain unlocked, posing a risk of death or serious injury if the elevator is called to another floor.
Sold atSUNS International and authorized residential elevator dealers nationwide from September 2024 through November 2025 for about $200.
RemedyConsumers should stop using their residential elevator containing the SUNS interlock switch immediately and contact SUNS or their elevator installer listed below for free professional installation of a replacement interlock switch.

What was recalled

This recall involves all SUNS International SS6291 Solenoid Interlock switches used in residential elevators produced from August 20, 2024, through November 6, 2025, with date codes 2431 through 2543. The SUNS SS6291 interlock switch measures approximately 10 inches by 2 inches and weighs about 2-pounds. "SUNS", the model number and date code are printed on a label on the side of the product.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using their residential elevator containing the SUNS interlock switch immediately and contact SUNS or their elevator installer listed below for free professional installation of a replacement interlock switch..
