Recall

Cooper Lighting Metalux Optimized High Bay LED Light Fixtures Recall (July 2026)

Fire hazard — about About 42,000 units.

WhatCooper Lighting Metalux Optimized High Bay LED Light Fixtures
How manyAbout About 42,000 units
The hazardThe LED's board can overheat or come into contact with the lens or nearby combustible materials, posing a fire hazard.
Sold atCooper Lighting authorized distributors nationwide from April 2020 through May 2025 for between $425 and $950.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled light fixtures immediately and contact Cooper Lighting to schedule a free repair or replacement. Consumers will be asked to complete a form at www.content.cooperlighting.com/ohb-recall to register the light fixtures and schedule a repair or replacement.
ContactCooper Lighting toll-free at 800-954-7228 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at OHBProgram@cooperlighting.com, or online at www.content.cooperlighting.com/ohb-recall or www.cooperlighting.com and click on "Safety and Recall Notices" at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-07-02 (recall no. 26599)

What was recalled

This recall involves Metalux Optimized High Bay (OHB) LED light fixtures used indoors in commercial structures with high ceilings such as warehouses and commercial bays. The fixtures are painted white metal and measure between 2 feet and 4 feet in length and between 1 foot and 1.5 feet in width, with a plastic diffuser lens cover. The catalog number and date code are printed on the product label inside of the fixture channel cover, behind the tray and can be accessed by removing two screws. The date code is in a two-digit mm/dd/yy format representing the month, day and year. Catalog Number Date Code OHB-60SE April 2020 through December 2022 OHBL-60SE October 2022 through May 15, 2025 OHBL-100SE October 2022 through May 15, 2025

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled light fixtures immediately and contact Cooper Lighting to schedule a free repair or replacement. Consumers will be asked to complete a form at www.content.cooperlighting.com/ohb-recall to register the light fixtures and schedule a repair or replacement..
