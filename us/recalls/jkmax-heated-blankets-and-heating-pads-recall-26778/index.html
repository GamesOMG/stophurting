JKMAX Heated Blankets and Heating Pads Recall (September 2026)

Fire and Burn hazard — about 195,000 units.

WhatJKMAX Heated Blankets and Heating Pads
How manyAbout 195,000 units
The hazardThe internal wire heating elements of the blankets and pads can overheat, posing fire and burn hazards to consumers.
Sold atSold Online At: SHEIN.com from June 2025 through May 2026 for between $8 and $12. · Hong Kong Xuheyuan Trading Co., Ltd, dba Sweet Cubby, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled blankets and pads immediately and contact JKMax for a full refund.
ContactJKMAX toll-free at 800-917-5732 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at JkmaxRecall@outlook.com, online at www.jkmaxrecall.com or amazon.com and click on “Recalls and Product Safety Alerts” at the bottom of the page to learn more.
Recall date2026-09-17 (recall no. 26778)

What was recalled

This recall involves all heating pads and heated blankets sold by JKMax including models JKMAX-002, JKMAX-7284B, JKMAX-6284B, JKMAX-10090B, JKMAX-8490B and JKMAX-6284B. The heated blankets and heating pads are blue, gray, brown or red. They are made of polyester fabric and are machine washable. The blankets and pads were sold in multiple sizes, and both have a detachable white remote that can be used to adjust the heat. The model number is located on the back of the remote and is also printed on a white label affixed to the product packaging along with the brand name “JKMAX”.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled blankets and pads immediately and contact JKMax for a full refund.. JKMAX toll-free at 800-917-5732 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at JkmaxRecall@outlook.com, online at www.jkmaxrecall.com or amazon.com and click on “Recalls and Product Safety Alerts” at the bottom of the page to learn more.
