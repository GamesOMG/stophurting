Recall

Harkla Children’s Sensory Swings Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury from Fall hazard — about 25,541 (In addition, about 5,534 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatHarkla Children’s Sensory Swings
How manyAbout 25,541 (In addition, about 5,534 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled swings’ ceiling hook can separate from its plate, posing a risk of serious injury from a fall hazard.
Sold atSold At: Sensory Tool House, Hammer & Jacks, Loaded Sensory and Renew (Faire), and online at Amazon.com and Harkla.co from March 2020 through December 2025 for between $100 and $130.
ContactHarkla toll-free at 844-442-7552 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, by email at recall@harkla.co or online at https://harkla.co/pages/ceiling-hook-recall-2026 or https://harkla.co and click “Recall” at the top of the page.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27060)

What was recalled

This recall involves Harkla’s children’s sensory swings. The compression swings have a blue fabric seat and come with a four-hole hook and ceiling mounting hardware for suspension, with a drop length of 18 to 24 inches. Only swings with a four-hole hook are included in this recall. SKU number LS-COMP-1BLU or LS-COMP-1BLU-SW is printed on the side of the product packaging.
