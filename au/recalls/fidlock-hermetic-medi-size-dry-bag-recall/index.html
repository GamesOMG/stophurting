Fidlock HERMETIC Medi size Dry Bag Recall (September 2026)

Serious internal injuries or death.

WhatFidlock HERMETIC Medi size Dry Bag
BrandFIDLOCK
The hazardRisk of serious internal injuries or death if a child swallows more than one high-powered magnet as they stick together in the child’s intestine or other digestive tissue. Risk of serious injury or death from choking.
Sold atAquastash Solutions Valley Outdoors Mountain Equipment Trek & Travel Mt. Creek Outdoors
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale1 December 2024 - 1 September 2026
Recalled byFidlock GmbH
Made inChina
ContactFidlock GmbH Email info-bike@fidlock.com Visit https://www.fidlock.com/consumer/en/recall
CategoryPhones, cameras and accessories
Recall date2026-09-21

What was recalled

Plastic bag with a watertight and dust tight closure mechanism consisting of sealing strips with embedded permanent magnets that automatically close and seal the bag.

Affected products are Medi size and have petrol blue coloured sealing strips with the lot number 2409140930201, which is located on back of sealing strip.

Why it was recalled

Due to a manufacturing defect, the sealing strips may come away from the bag allowing individual magnets to fall out.

What to do

  1. Check the lot number on the bag. If the bag has the affected lot number on it, immediately stop using the bag and place out of reach of children.
  2. Cut the bag with scissors so that it is unusable and take a photograph of the bag with the lot number visible.
  3. Return the product to the place of purchase for a free replacement or a full refund.
