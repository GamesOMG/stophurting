ARB bull bar mounting brackets for MY26 N90 Hilux Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or property damage (including to other road users).

What was recalled

Brackets for mounting a bull bar to the front of MY26 N90 Hilux vehicles.

Affected bull bars:

  • Summit MKII (part number 3414770)
  • Commercial Bar (part number 3414780)

Why it was recalled

The mounting brackets may become loose during or after use on corrugated roads, causing the bull bar to detach from the vehicle.

What to do

  1. Stop using the vehicle on corrugated roads immediately.
  2. Contact your nearest ARB store to arrange for a free replacement of the bull bar mounting brackets.
  3. For further information or assistance with this recall please contact your nearest ARB store.
