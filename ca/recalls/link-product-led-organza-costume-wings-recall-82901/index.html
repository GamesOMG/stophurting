Link Product LED Organza Costume Wings Recall (November 2026)

Ingestion hazard.

WhatLink Product LED Organza Costume Wings
BrandLink Product Solutions
The hazardingestion hazard
CategoryToys and games
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byImporter Link Product Solutions ON Canada
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-03

What was recalled

This recall involves the Fright Night Organza Wings. The Organza wings with LED lights can be purchased in Black, White, Red, Pink, Fuchsia and Purple and contains 3 x LR44 Batteries.

Why it was recalled

The LED key supplied with the wings contains 3 x LR44 button/coin cell batteries. The button/coin cell batteries may become accessible from the LED box. If swallowed or inserted into the body, button/coin cell batteries can cause serious internal injuries. The identified hazard is associated with the LED box only. The wings itself are not affected.

As of October 27, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product, keep it out of reach of children, dispose of product and contact the company for a partial refund.

For more information, consumers can contact Link Product Solutions by telephone at 416-637-2180 between 9:00am at 5:00pm EST, Monday to Friday or by email at custserv@linkproducts.ca.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 432 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from July 2026 to September 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
