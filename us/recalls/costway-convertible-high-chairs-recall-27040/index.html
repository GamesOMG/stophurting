Recall

Costway Convertible High Chairs Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Fall hazard — about 3,332 units.

WhatCostway Convertible High Chairs
How manyAbout 3,332 units
The hazardThe convertible high chairs violate the mandatory safety standard for high chairs because they do not contain a passive restraint system and a child can slip through the opening between the feeding tray and seat and become entrapped or fall, posing a risk of serious injury or death.
Sold atSold At: Walmart and Target stores nationwide and online at Costway.com from May 2023 through July 2026 for between $84 and $100. · Goplus Corporation, dba Costway, of Fontana, California
RemedyConsumers should immediately stop using the recalled high chairs and contact Costway for a free replacement high chair. Consumers will be asked to provide a receipt or a photo of the product and will receive a prepaid label to return the recalled product to Costway. Upon receipt of the recalled product, Costway will ship a replacement high chair free of charge.
ContactCostway toll-free at 844-242-1885 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at recall@costway.com, or online at or www.costway.com and click on “Recall” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-22 (recall no. 27040)

What was recalled

This recall involves Costway high chairs designed to convert into a table and chair set. They were available in several color combinations, including purple and white, red and white, blue and white, and brown and white. There is no tracking information located on the products.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled high chairs and contact Costway for a free replacement high chair. Consumers will be asked to provide a receipt or a photo of the product and will receive a prepaid label to return the recalled product to Costway. Upon receipt of the recalled product, Costway will ship a replacement high chair free of charge.. Costway toll-free at 844-242-1885 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at recall@costway.com, or online at or www.costway.com and click on “Recall” at the bottom of the page for more information.
