Rota Cota Trade Pro 3 Paint Spray Gun Recall (September 2026)

Serious eye injury and high-pressure skin injury.

WhatRota Cota Trade Pro 3 Paint Spray Gun
BrandRota Cota
The hazardRisk of serious eye injury and high-pressure skin injury if the safety lock unintentionally disengages and the spray gun discharges paint when the trigger is pressed.
Sold atSee traders list.
Where it was soldNationally
On sale23 January 2026 - 28 August 2026
Recalled byDuluxGroup (Australia) Pty Ltd
Made inChina
ContactDuluxGroup (Australia) Pty Ltd Call: 13 23 77 Email: hello@rotacota.com.au Visit: www.rotacota.com.au/contact-us/
CategoryTools and machinery
Recall date2026-09-15

What was recalled

Spray gun supplied with the Rota Cota Trade Pro 3 Paint Sprayer (N1031235-UNIT – RCTA TradePro 3 Paint Sprayer) or separately as a spare part (N1031247-UNIT – RCTA TradePro 3 Spray Gun HB133).

Why it was recalled

The spray gun's safety lock mechanism may move from the locked position to the unlocked position without the user's knowledge if the trigger is repeatedly depressed or the spray gun is shaken, creating a risk of unintentional discharge.

What to do

  1. Immediately stop using the spray gun and disconnect the spray gun from the hose.
  2. Return the spray gun to a Dulux Trade Centre, Paint Spot or Inspirations Store for a free replacement spray gun.
