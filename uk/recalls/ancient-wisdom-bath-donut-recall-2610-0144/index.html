Ancient Wisdom Bath Donut Recall (November 2026)

Serious risk of choking, as due to the characteristic form, appearance, colour and size,….

WhatAncient Wisdom Bath Donut
BrandAncient Wisdom
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of choking, as due to the characteristic form, appearance, colour and size, it may be mistaken for a foodstuff by young children or vulnerable users.
Product typeCosmetics, personal care and grooming – Bath Additives
Made inUnited Kingdom
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-02 (notice 2610-0144)

What was recalled

1 piece/180g bath product in the shape of a donut, packaged in sets of 16 in cardboard packaging.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of choking, as due to the characteristic form, appearance, colour and size, it may be mistaken for a foodstuff by young children or vulnerable users. The product has small parts that can be easily detached. A small child/vulnerable user may put these parts in the mouth and choke.  

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 (the ‘Cosmetics Regulation’) or the Food Imitations (Safety) Regulations 1989.

What to do

The local authority has required that the product is withdrawn from the market and recalled by the wholesaler, AW Aromatics Limited (“Ancient Wisdom”). Owners are advised to stop using the product and keep it away from children. Contact the retailer/supplier for a replacement or refund. 

For more information, please contact the wholesaler AW Aromatics Limited via care@ancientwisdom.biz, WhatsApp:+44 7951 490 or call +44 (0)1142 729 165.

Additional information
