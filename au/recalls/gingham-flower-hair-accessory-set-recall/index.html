Recall

Gingham Flower hair accessory set Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking or asphyxiation.

WhatGingham Flower hair accessory set
BrandCountry Road
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking or asphyxiation if the centre button of the flower clip separates when pulled from the product and a child swallows it.
Sold atCountry Road stores, authorised retailers and online at www.countryroad.com.au and www.countryroad.co.nz
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale4 July 2026 - 16 August 2026
Recalled byCountry Road
Made inChina
ContactCountry Road Email: customerservice@countryroad.com.au Visit: www.countryroad.com.au
CategoryJewellery and fashion accessories
Recall date2026-09-09

What was recalled

Hair accessory set

  • includes hair clips and a hair tie
  • available in pale blue and mineral pink
  • SKU 60319534

Why it was recalled

The centre button of the flower clip can separate from the product when pulled creating a choking hazard.

What to do

  1. Stop using the hair accessory immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Return the products to any Country Road store for a full refund
  3. Contact Country Road via email or the live chat function on the website if you are unable to return the product to store, or if you have sold or gifted it to another person.
