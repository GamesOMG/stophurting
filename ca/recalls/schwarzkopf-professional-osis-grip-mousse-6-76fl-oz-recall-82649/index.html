Schwarzkopf Professional Osis Grip Mousse 6.76fl Oz/200ml Recall (September 2026)

Injury hazard.

WhatSchwarzkopf Professional Osis Grip Mousse 6.76fl Oz/200ml
BrandSchwarzkopf
The hazardinjury hazard
CategoryBeauty and personal care
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled by​​Manufacturer​ Henkel Consumer Brands Canada Corporation Mississauga, ON ​​Canada​
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-21

What was recalled

This recall involves Schwarzkopf Professional Osis Grip Mousse 6.76 fl Oz/200ml. 

Why it was recalled

The recalled product has packaging issues, which can cause the product to leak from the package when under pressure. The leakage and resulting pressure may cause the package to burst, which could result in injury or property damage.  

As of September 18, 2026​, the company has received 1 report of incident and no reports of injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and return it to place of purchase for a refund.

For more information, consumers can contact Henkel Customer Support Team by telephone at 1-800-234-4672 (English), Monday to Thursday from 6:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. PT and Friday from 6:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. PT. For French service consumers can call 1-800-463-3081, Monday to Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ET. Also, by email at skpcanada-support@henkel.com. 

Please note that, in addition to the requirements of the Cosmetic Regulations, the Food and Drugs Act (FDA) prohibits the sale of cosmetics that are either made with hazardous substances, or under unsanitary conditions, to users in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 90,089 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from ​November 2023​ to ​June 2026​.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in ​Hungary​
