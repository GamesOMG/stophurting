Recall

XL magnetic building blocks Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation.

WhatXL magnetic building blocks
BrandLittle Australian
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation if a child places small parts inside their mouth. There is also a risk of serious injury from laceration from sharp points. Incidents have occurred.
Sold atLittle Australian - online
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale28 June 2026 - 8 August 2026
Recalled byLittle Australian
Made inChina
ContactLittle Australian Email: support@littleaustralian.com Visit: https://littleaustralian.com/pages/contact
CategoryLearning and activity toys · Toys that are magnetic
Recall date2026-09-02

What was recalled

Large coloured magnetic building blocks

Why it was recalled

The product may not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. The blocks can break apart and expose sharp points and small parts if dropped or during use.

What to do

  1. Stop using the XL magnetic building blocks immediately and keep out of reach of children.
  2. Contact Little Australian to arrange a full refund. Consumers may reply to the recall email they receive from Little Australian.
  3. Little Australian will provide instructions about safe handling and disposal of the blocks.
  4. For further information about the recall, please contact Little Australian.
