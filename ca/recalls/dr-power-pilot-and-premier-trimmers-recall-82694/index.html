DR Power Pilot and Premier Trimmers Recall (October 2026)

Fire and burn hazards.

WhatDR Power Pilot and Premier Trimmers
BrandDR Power
The hazardfire and burn hazards
CategoryTools and accessories
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byManufacturer Generac Power Systems Jefferson, WI United States of America Manufacturer Rato Tech Singapore PTE Vietnam
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-10-08

What was recalled

This recall involves DR Power Trimmers. The model and serial number are printed on a white label that is on the side frame of your Trimmer Mower.  You can find the manufacture date of your unit on the serial number label affixed to the side frame of the unit, which is listed in YYYYMMDD format.

Why it was recalled

Grass and debris can accumulate in the unit and ignite, posing a fire and burn risk.

As of October 7, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada. In the United States, the company has received 19 reports of incidents and of those, 2 included injuries.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact DR Power for repair instructions.

For more information, consumers can contact Dr Power by telephone at 1-800-854-3194, from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday by email at recall@drpower.com or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and DR Power.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 343 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 42,860 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from November 2022 to July 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in the United States of America
