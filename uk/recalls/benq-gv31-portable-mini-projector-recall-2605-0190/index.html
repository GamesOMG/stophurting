BenQ GV31 Portable Mini Projector Recall (May 2026)

Risk of fire.

WhatBenQ GV31 Portable Mini Projector
BrandBenQ
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire as it has been identified that the battery may overheat and enter into thermal runaway, causing a fire.
Product typeCommunication and media equipment - Projection Systems
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-29 (notice 2605-0190)

What was recalled

White mini portable projector with rotating lens.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire as it has been identified that the battery may overheat and enter into thermal runaway, causing a fire.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by BenQ.

Consumers are asked to stop using the product immediately. To apply for a battery replacement or a device upgrade (GV32), please visit: BenQ Recall . Consumers can also contact the BenQ service hotline on (+44) (0) 207 216 0039.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0190

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
