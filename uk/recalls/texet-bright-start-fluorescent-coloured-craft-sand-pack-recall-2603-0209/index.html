Texet Bright Start Fluorescent Coloured Craft Sand Pack of 6 Recall (March 2026)

Risk to health as the sand included in the set may be contaminated with a quantity of asb….

WhatTexet Bright Start Fluorescent Coloured Craft Sand Pack of 6
BrandTexet
The hazardThe product presents a risk to health as the sand included in the set may be contaminated with a quantity of asbestos.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Arts/Crafts/Needlework Sand Supplies
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-03-20 (notice 2603-0209)

What was recalled

350g tubs of fluorescent craft sand sold in packs of 6. Sales period April 2024-February 2026.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk to health as the sand may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos. Asbestos is a banned substance because it poses a risk to health even at low levels of exposure.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Texet Sales Ltd (the importer) and YPO Limited and Mossy Office Supplies (distributors). For further information, please contact Texet or the distributor you purchased from: Texet Recall Page

We recommend owners stop using this product immediately and keep it out of the reach of children. Follow the below instructions for details on disposal and return of the product.

Disposal instructions:
  • Stop using the product immediately.
  • If the sand is still in its packaging, place in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double tape it securely, label it clearly and store in a secure location out of the reach of children.
  • If the sand has been used, clean up sites where the product was used using wet cloths to avoid generating dust. Wear gloves and a mask. Double bag the sand, gloves, mask and cloths.
  • Keep children and other people away from areas where the sand has been used until the area has been cleaned.
  • Return the product to store where specified by the retailer for a full refund.
  • If you are unable to return the product to the retailer, follow the steps above and contact your local authority / access their website for guidance on safe disposal of asbestos in your area.
  • For further information, please contact the retailer or visit the GOV UK page on asbestos in consumer products.
Additional information

PSD case number: 2603-0209

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
