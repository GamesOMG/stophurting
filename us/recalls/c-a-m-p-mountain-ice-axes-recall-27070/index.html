Recall

C.A.M.P. Mountain Ice Axes Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fall hazard — about 2,230 units.

WhatC.A.M.P. Mountain Ice Axes
How manyAbout 2,230 units
The hazardA screw on the recalled mountain ice axes can loosen during use, causing the handle or pick to detach, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fall hazard.
Sold atSold At: REI stores nationwide and online at Camp-usa.com, Backcountry.com, Amazon.com and REI.com from November 2025 through September 2026 for between $30 and $330.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled mountain ice axes immediately and register at https://www.camp-usa.com/x-bolt-recall-102026 to receive a free repair kit. Consumers should have the model and batch number available, which can be found on the side of the axes’ shaft. Consumers can either contact C.A.M.P. for a free repair kit with instructions to repair the axes themselves or have the firm repair the ice axes. The firm will send the consumer a prepaid shipping label and packaging to return the recalled axes to the firm.
ContactC.A.M.P. toll-free at 877-421-2267 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. MT Monday through Friday, email warranties@camp-usa.com or online at https://www.camp-usa.com/x-bolt-recall-102026 or go to www.camp-usa.com and click on “Safety Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27070)

What was recalled

This recall includes X-Dream, X-Dream Total Dry, X-All Mountain Hammer and X-All Mountain Adze. These products are designed for vertical ice climbing. The batch number is located on the side of the shaft. “CAMP” is written in white lettering on the ice axes shaft. The recalled nut and bolt kit were also sold as an accessory item as model number 3641. Recalled Models and Batches • X-Dream (model: 3570): Batches 10/24, 07/25, 10/25, 12/25 • X-Dream Total Dry (model: 3609): Batches 10/24, 07/25, 11/25 • X-All Mountain Hammer (model: 3630): Batches 10/24, 09/25 • X-All Mountain Adze (model: 3631): Batches 10/24, 09/25 • Accessory Kit (nut and bolt) (model: 3641)

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled mountain ice axes immediately and register at https://www.camp-usa.com/x-bolt-recall-102026 to receive a free repair kit. Consumers should have the model and batch number available, which can be found on the side of the axes’ shaft. Consumers can either contact C.A.M.P. for a free repair kit with instructions to repair the axes themselves or have the firm repair the ice axes. The firm will send the consumer a prepaid shipping label and packaging to return the recalled axes to the firm.. C.A.M.P. toll-free at 877-421-2267 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. MT Monday through Friday, email warranties@camp-usa.com or online at https://www.camp-usa.com/x-bolt-recall-102026 or go to www.camp-usa.com and click on “Safety Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
