Recall

CHE-US Magnetic Robot Toys Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion — about 101,282 units.

WhatCHE-US Magnetic Robot Toys
How manyAbout 101,282 units
The hazardThe recalled magnetic stick figures violate the mandatory standard for toys because when pulled apart, the figures’ arms and legs liberate high-powered magnets posing an ingestion hazard to children. When high-powered magnets are swallowed, the ingested magnets can attract each other, or other metal objects, and become lodged in the digestive system. This can result in perforations, twisting, and/or blockage of the intestines, blood poisoning and death.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from April 2025 through August 2026 for between t $9 and $18. · Guangzhou Shian Trading Co., Ltd., dba CHE-US/CHESLICR, of China.
ContactCHE-US by email at CHE-USMagneticRobotToysRecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27096)

What was recalled

This recall involves CHE-US magnetic stick figure toys. The robot-shaped toys were sold in sets of five or 10 purple, blue, yellow, red and green stick figures. Each stick figure has four small magnets, one in each hand and foot. “X004N3MGCB” is printed on the back of the product packaging.
