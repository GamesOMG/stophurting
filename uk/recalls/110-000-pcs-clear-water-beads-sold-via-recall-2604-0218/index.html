Recall

110,000 Pcs Clear Water Beads sold via Shein Recall (May 2026)

High risk of asphyxiation.

What110,000 Pcs Clear Water Beads sold via Shein
The hazardThe product presents a high risk of asphyxiation. If the product is swallowed in its hydrated form, it can block a young child’s airway.
Product typeDecorative articles/Homeware/Gardenware - Ornament Accessories
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-07 (notice 2604-0218)

What was recalled

Clear water gel beads in plastic packaging.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a high risk of asphyxiation. If the product is swallowed in its hydrated form, it can block a young child’s airway.

OPSS has published a Product Safety Alert for water beads, which can be accessed at : https://www.gov.uk/product-safety-alerts-reports-recalls/product-safety-alert-water-beads-psa7.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by SHEIN.

We recommend that consumers stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of children. Affected users have been contacted directly by the marketplace.

The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (Shein).

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2604-0218

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
