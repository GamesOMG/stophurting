DREO DR-HSH015 Space Heaters Recall (October 2026)

Fire and Burn hazard — about 338,800 (In addition, about 36,300 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatDREO DR-HSH015 Space Heaters
How manyAbout 338,800 (In addition, about 36,300 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled space heaters can overheat and ignite, posing fire and burn hazards.
Sold atSold At: Costco stores nationwide and online at Dreo.com, BestBuy.com, Kohls.com, Lowes.com, QVC.com, HomeDepot.com, B2BRenew.com and Costco.com from September 2025 through October 2026 for between $70 and $110.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled space heaters immediately and contact DREO for a free replacement space heater. Consumers will be asked to unplug the space heater, destroy it by cutting the electrical cord and follow instructions to send a photo of the destroyed heater.
ContactDREO toll-free at 833-820-0887 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, by email at spaceheater@realtimeresults.net, or online at www.dreo.com and click on “PRODUCT RECALLS” on the top of the page, or www.recallrtr.com/spaceheater for more information.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27054)

What was recalled

This recall involves DREO space heaters, model number DR-HSH015. The ceramic, electric heaters are black and measure about 11.5 inches wide and 13 inches tall and include a remote control. Only units manufactured from April 2025 through January 2026 are included in this recall. The manufacture date, in YYYY/MM format, and the model number are printed on a label on the heater’s underside and “DREO” on the front of the unit.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled space heaters immediately and contact DREO for a free replacement space heater. Consumers will be asked to unplug the space heater, destroy it by cutting the electrical cord and follow instructions to send a photo of the destroyed heater.. DREO toll-free at 833-820-0887 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, by email at spaceheater@realtimeresults.net, or online at www.dreo.com and click on “PRODUCT RECALLS” on the top of the page, or www.recallrtr.com/spaceheater for more information.
