Haunted House Jumbos and Filled Critters Toys Recall (July 2026)

The products present a risk to health as the sand inside the toy may be contaminated with….

WhatHaunted House Jumbos and Filled Critters Toys
BrandHaunted House
The hazardThe products present a risk to health as the sand inside the toy may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos.
Product typeToys – Sensory Toys
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-21 (notice 2607-0185)

What was recalled

Selection of stretchy insect toys.

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk to health as the sand inside the toy may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos. Asbestos is a banned substance because it poses a risk to health even at low levels of exposure.  

The products do not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The products have been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by PMS International.

We recommend owners stop using these products immediately and keep them out of the reach of children. Follow the below instructions for details on disposal and return of the products.

For further information, access the PMS International website: https://b2b.pmsinternational.com/product-recall/ or contact  productrecall@pmsinternational.com

Disposal instructions:
  • Stop using the product immediately.
  • If the toy is undamaged, place in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double tape it securely, label it clearly and store in a secure location out of the reach of children.
  • If the toy has been damaged and the sand inside is accessible, clean up sites where the product was used using wet cloths to avoid generating dust. Wear gloves and a mask. Double bag the sand, gloves, mask and cloths.
  • Keep children and other people away from areas where the sand has been used until the area has been cleaned.
  • Return the product to store where specified by the retailer for a full refund.
  • If you are unable to return the product to the retailer, follow the steps above and contact your local authority / access their website for guidance on safe disposal of asbestos in your area.
  • For further information, please contact the retailer or visit the GOV.UK page on asbestos in consumer products.
Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0185

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
