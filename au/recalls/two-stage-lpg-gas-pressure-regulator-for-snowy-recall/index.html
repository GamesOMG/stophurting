Two-stage LPG gas pressure regulator for Snowy River and Newgen caravans Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury or death.

WhatTwo-stage LPG gas pressure regulator for Snowy River and Newgen caravans
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death if the gas leaks near an ignition source and causes an explosion or fire. Risk of serious injury or death by asphyxiation if the leak causes a build-up of gas in an enclosed space. Incidents of the regulator failing and leaking LPG gas have occurred.
Sold atPlease see attached list of dealers who sold the product.

ContactRegent RV Call 1800 485 031 Email recall@snowyrivercaravans.com.au for Snowy River Caravans or recall@newgencaravans.com.au for NewGen caravans Customer portal link https://snowyrivercaravans.com.au/…
CategoryGas products and appliances · Caravan, motorhome and camper accessories
Recall date2026-08-17

What was recalled

Compact metal regulator fitted to the gas system of Snowy River and Newgen caravans.

  • Model: RV-One HDJYF1001-1
  • AGA approval: 9191

The regulator is mounted on the A-frame at the front of the caravan, between the gas
cylinders and the body of the van and is connected to the cylinders by flexible pigtail
hoses.

Please see attached list of chassis numbers with affected regulators. The chassis
number is engraved on the A-Frame and on the compliance plate inside the tunnel boot.

Why it was recalled

The diaphragm inside the gas regulator can fail, causing the regulator to leak LPG gas.

What to do

  1. Stop using the regulator immediately and ensure both gas bottle and regulator are turned off.
  2. Contact Regent RV via the customer portal link below to arrange for a licenced gas fitter to replace the regulator free of charge.
  3. For further information or assistance with this recall, please contact Regent RV.
