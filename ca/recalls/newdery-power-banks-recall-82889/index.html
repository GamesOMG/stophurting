NEWDERY Power Banks Recall (November 2026)

Fire hazard.

WhatNEWDERY Power Banks
BrandNEWDERY
The hazardfire hazard
CategoryElectronics
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byImporter Shenzhen Xinkewangda Electronics Co., Ltd., d/b/a NEWDERY, of China Shenzhen, Guangdong China Manufacturer Shenzhen Xinkewangda Electronics Co., Ltd Shenzhen, Guangdong China
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-05

What was recalled

This recall involves Newdery Power Banks Model ZHX-PB22. The power banks are encased in a clear plastic shell in the front that shows a black interior with two yellow cables attached to a black exterior shell. The brand name “NEWDERY” is engraved on the front and the model number “ZHX-PB22” is printed on the back. The products were previously sold on Amazon.ca.

Why it was recalled

The lithium-ion battery in the power banks can explode or ignite, posing fire and burn hazards to consumers.

As of October 30, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product. Affected purchasers have been refunded through Amazon.ca. Consumers who have questions, or who believe they did not receive a refund, may contact NEWDERY.

For more information, consumers can contact NEWDERY by telephone at 1-888-256-6673, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday, by email at support@newdery.com or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 962 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from June 2024 to November 2024.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
