Recall

Sweet Cubby Children's Loungewear Sets Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Burn hazard — about 12,461 units.

WhatSweet Cubby Children's Loungewear Sets
How manyAbout 12,461 units
The hazardThe recalled children’s loungewear sets violate the mandatory flammability standard for children’s sleepwear, posing a risk of serious burn injuries or death.
Sold atSold Online At: SHEIN.com from June 2025 through May 2026 for between $8 and $12. · Hong Kong Xuheyuan Trading Co., Ltd, dba Sweet Cubby, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled loungewear sets immediately, take them away from children and contact Sweet Cubby for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the loungewear sets by cutting them in half and send a photo of the destroyed loungewear sets to SweetCubby@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.
ContactSweet Cubby by email at SweetCubby@163.com.
Recall date2026-09-10 (recall no. 26752)

What was recalled

This recall involves all Sweet Cubby children’s loungewear sets. The recalled two-piece loungewear sets consist of a short-sleeved shirt and matching shorts. The sets were sold in various colors and in children’s sizes 5-6Y through 13-14Y. “Batch No.: 272779” and the size are printed on the sewn-in neck label and on the seam label.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled loungewear sets immediately, take them away from children and contact Sweet Cubby for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the loungewear sets by cutting them in half and send a photo of the destroyed loungewear sets to SweetCubby@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.. Sweet Cubby by email at SweetCubby@163.com.
