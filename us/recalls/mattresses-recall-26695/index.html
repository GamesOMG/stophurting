Recall

Mattresses Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 774 units.

WhatMattresses
How manyAbout 774 units
The hazardThe recalled mattresses violate the mandatory flammability standard for mattresses, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from November 2025 through March 2026 for between $120 and $140.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Qabefy for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress, by writing in permanent marker "Recalled," their name and the current date on the mattress's top surface, and to email a photo of the destroyed mattress to Sixiang2023@gmail.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactQabefy by email at Sixiang2023@gmail.com.
Recall date2026-08-13 (recall no. 26695)

What was recalled

This recall involves Qabefy-branded mattresses. They are 10 inches thick and were sold in twin sizes. They have a white top and black sides with orange trim. They were sold in a compressed box. "Item NO.: CD01101US" is printed on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Qabefy for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress, by writing in permanent marker "Recalled," their name and the current date on the mattress's top surface, and to email a photo of the destroyed mattress to Sixiang2023@gmail.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. Qabefy by email at Sixiang2023@gmail.com.
