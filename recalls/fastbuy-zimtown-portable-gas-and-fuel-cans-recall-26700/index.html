Recall

Fastbuy Zimtown Portable Gas and Fuel Cans Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Burn Hazard and Child Poisoning — about About 200 units.

WhatFastbuy Zimtown Portable Gas and Fuel Cans
How manyAbout About 200 units
The hazardThe fuel containers violate the requirement for closures under the Children's Gasoline Burn Prevention Act because the closure is not child-resistant, posing a risk of burns and poisoning to children.
Sold atOnline at Walmart.com and Zimtown.com from May 2026 through July 2026 for between $32 and $63.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the gas and fuel containers immediately and contact Fastbuy for a full refund. Consumers should disassemble the spout, cut it into pieces with scissors and dispose of it separately from the container. Consumers will be asked to write "RECALLED" on the container and submit photos of the product to ZimtownFuelCanrecall@outlook.com separately.
ContactFastbuy by email at ZimtownFuelCanrecall@outlook.com or online at www.zimtown.com and select recalls.
Recall date2026-08-13 (recall no. 26700)

What was recalled

This recall involves Zimtown Portable "Jerry" style Gas and Fuel Cans with spout. The metal 5.0 gallon (20L) fuel cans come in red and army green. The cans have a white spout and yellow vent cap. The assembled product dimensions are 4.76 x 8.49 x 19.29 inches.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the gas and fuel containers immediately and contact Fastbuy for a full refund. Consumers should disassemble the spout, cut it into pieces with scissors and dispose of it separately from the container. Consumers will be asked to write "RECALLED" on the container and submit photos of the product to ZimtownFuelCanrecall@outlook.com separately.. Fastbuy by email at ZimtownFuelCanrecall@outlook.com or online at www.zimtown.com and select recalls.
