Disposable vaping device GTMBAR 45K Recall (November 2026)

Lack of child-resistant packaging.

WhatDisposable vaping device GTMBAR 45K
BrandGTMBAR
The hazardlack of child-resistant packaging
CategoryChemicals
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byImporter Cigarettes électroniques LK Vape Inc. Blainville, Québec Canada Distributor Cigarettes électroniques LK Vape Inc. Blainville, Québec Canada
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-13

What was recalled

This recall involves the disposable vaping device GTMBAR 45K. The GTM BAR 45K, branded GTMBAR, is a disposable vaping device equipped with a rechargeable USB-C battery. It consists of a device containing 1.75 mL of e-liquid and a separate 18.25 mL prefilled container that is inserted during initial setup, for a total capacity of 20 mL. The nicotine concentration is 20 mg/mL.

Why it was recalled

The recalled products do not meet the packaging requirements for vaping products as required by the Vaping Products Labelling and Packaging Regulations under the Canada Consumer Products Safety Act. 

Vaping substances containing nicotine present a poisoning risk if they are ingested. Inappropriate packaging such as missing child-resistant closures can lead to unintentional exposure to the product and cause serious illness or injuries, including death.

As of November 12, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product, keep it out of the reach of children, and dispose of it in accordance with municipal hazardous waste disposal guidelines.

For more information, consumers may contact Cigarettes Électroniques LK Vape Inc. by telephone at (450) 434-8813 ext. 101, Monday to Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, or by email at info@lkvape.ca.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 24,950 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from December 2024 to October 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
