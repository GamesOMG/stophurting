Farm to Table Play Kitchen Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from strangulation and asphyxiation.

WhatFarm to Table Play Kitchen
BrandKidkraft
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from strangulation and asphyxiation if a child is snagged on an accessory hook. An incident has occurred in the USA resulting in a fatality.
Sold atTemple & Webster
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale23 August 2018 - 23 April 2025
Recalled byTemple & Webster
Made inChina
ContactTemple & Webster Call: 1300 090 0675 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm AEST). Email: support@templeandwebster.com.au Visit: https://www.templeandwebster.com.au/v/account/help/contact_us
CategoryLearning and activity toys
Recall date2026-09-04

What was recalled

Model kitchen play set for children, 5 modes of play, kids can cook, chop, clean, organize & create.
Includes:

  • bench, sink, cooktop, oven, refrigerator and chalkboard
  • play food and cooking accessories

Why it was recalled

The accessory hooks intended to hold pots and pans can become a protrusion and catch hazard. Children’s clothing can become snagged on these hooks, posing a strangulation hazard.

What to do

  1. Stop using the play kitchen immediately, remove the accessory hooks and dispose of them in general household waste. Once the hooks are removed, the product may continue being used.
  2. Contact Temple & Webster to arrange free redesigned replacement hooks.
  3. If you require further information or assistance with this recall, please contact Temple & Webster.
