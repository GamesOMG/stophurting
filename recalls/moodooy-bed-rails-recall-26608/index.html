Recall

Moodooy Bed Rails Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about About 228 units.

WhatMoodooy Bed Rails
How manyAbout About 228 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for?adult portable bed rails?because users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation.?In addition, the bed rails do not bear the required hazard warning labels.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com in February 2026 for about $38.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact YUEZHANG for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the black safety strap in half, write "RECALLED" on the upper and lower bed rails with a permanent marker, take a photo of the marked bed rail with the buyer's name on a piece of paper, and email the photo to Moodooy-recall@outlook.com.
ContactYUEZHANG by email at Moodooy-recall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-09 (recall no. 26608)

What was recalled

This recall involves the Moodooy-branded adult portable bed rail. The bed rail has a white frame with black trim and a black storage pocket. The brand name "Moodooy" and model "F311" are printed on the storage pocket. The bed rail is about 12 inches wide by about 21 inches tall to about 23 inches tall (when extended).

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact YUEZHANG for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the black safety strap in half, write "RECALLED" on the upper and lower bed rails with a permanent marker, take a photo of the marked bed rail with the buyer's name on a piece of paper, and email the photo to Moodooy-recall@outlook.com.. YUEZHANG by email at Moodooy-recall@outlook.com.
