Kao Liese Prettia Bubble Hair Colour Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury from a reaction to the contents in the product.

WhatKao Liese Prettia Bubble Hair Colour
BrandKao Liese
The hazardRisk of serious injury from a reaction to the contents in the product if consumers are unable to identify and read the warning label before use.
Sold atTong Li Supermarket Hornsby, Tong Li Supermarket Rhodes and Tong Li Supermarket Cabramatta
Where it was soldNationally
On sale1 June 2026 - 21 August 2026
Recalled byALOG (AUSTRALIA) WHOLESALE PTY LTD
Made inJapan
ContactALOG (AUSTRALIA) WHOLESALE PTY LTD Call: 0423 458 241 between 9 am and 5 pm AEST Monday to Friday Email: alogausops@gmail.com
CategoryCosmetic products
Recall date2026-09-18

What was recalled

Hair dye foam for home use available in a range of colours.

Why it was recalled

The warning label on the product is unclear, is in the wrong position and does not comply with regulatory requirements.

What to do

  1. Consumers should immediately stop using the product.
  2. Consumers can return the product, or proof of purchase of the product, to the place of purchase for a full refund.
  3. For more information, contact ALOG (AUSTRALIA) WHOLESALE PTY LTD.
