Recall

Huffy Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 E-Trikes Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury from Fall hazard — about 286 units.

WhatHuffy Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 E-Trikes
How manyAbout 286 units
The hazardThe recalled tricycles’ rear wheels can become loose or separate from the rear frame, posing a risk of serious injury from a fall hazard.
Sold atSold At: Independent bike shops nationwide and online atwww.buzzbicycles.com from November 2024 through July 2026 for between $1,400 and $2,500.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled tricycles immediately and contact Huffy for free replacement rear axle assemblies. The rear wheel axle assemblies will be sent directly to consumers or to a third-party who will repair the tricycle. Huffy Corporation will reimburse consumers up to $100 for charges incurred by the third-party repair.
ContactHuffy Corporation toll-free at 855-430-6240 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at service@huffy.com or online at https://www.buzzbicycles.com/recalls or at www.buzzbicycles.com and click on “Recalls” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27086)

What was recalled

This recall involves Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 E-Trike, model E4004. The tricycle is a pedal assist tricycle. The tricycle is gray with "BUZZ" logos on the left and right side. The tricycle has a 500-Watt motor and a 48-Volt battery. The tricycle has three 24-inch wheels. The model number is printed on a label near the pedals on the bottom side of the frame’s downtube.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled tricycles immediately and contact Huffy for free replacement rear axle assemblies. The rear wheel axle assemblies will be sent directly to consumers or to a third-party who will repair the tricycle. Huffy Corporation will reimburse consumers up to $100 for charges incurred by the third-party repair.. Huffy Corporation toll-free at 855-430-6240 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at service@huffy.com or online at https://www.buzzbicycles.com/recalls or at www.buzzbicycles.com and click on “Recalls” at the top of the page for more information.
