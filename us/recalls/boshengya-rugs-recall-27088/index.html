Recall

Boshengya Rugs Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 83 units.

WhatBoshengya Rugs
How manyAbout 83 units
The hazardThe recalled rugs violate the mandatory standard for the flammability of carpets and rugs, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire hazard.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from September 2026 through October 2026 for between $30 and $65. · Boshengya Dianzi Shangwu (Tianjin) Youxian Gongsi (Boshengya E-Commerce Tianjin Co., Ltd.), dba Boshengya, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the rugs immediately and contact Boshengya for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the rug by cutting it in half and send a photo of the destroyed rug to boshengya666@outlook.com. Consumers will then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactBoshengya by email at boshengya666@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27088)

What was recalled

This recall involves Boshengya-branded rugs. The pink or orange shag area rugs measure about 5 or 6 feet wide and 8 feet long. ‘INCREDIBLY SOFT’ is printed on a label on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the rugs immediately and contact Boshengya for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the rug by cutting it in half and send a photo of the destroyed rug to boshengya666@outlook.com. Consumers will then dispose of the recalled product.. Boshengya by email at boshengya666@outlook.com.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-11-12. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
