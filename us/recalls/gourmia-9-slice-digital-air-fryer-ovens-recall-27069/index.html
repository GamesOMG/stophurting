Recall

Gourmia 9-Slice Digital Air Fryer Ovens Recall (November 2026)

Burn hazard — about 175,951 units.

WhatGourmia 9-Slice Digital Air Fryer Ovens
How manyAbout 175,951 units
The hazardThe oven’s doors can unexpectedly close when bumped, posing a burn hazard to consumers retrieving hot food.
Sold atSold At: Target stores nationwide and online at Target.com from October 2021 through June 2026 for about $150.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled Ovens immediately and contact Gourmia for a free repair kit by mail. The repair kit will include an added doorstop and instructions on how to install.
ContactGourmia toll free at 888-834-0092 Monday through Friday 9 a.m. to 5p.m. or online at www.gourmia.com/recall.asp or Gourmia.com and click on the “Recall Information” link at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27069)

What was recalled

This recall involves Gourmia 9-SliceDigitalAir Fryer Ovens with model number GTF7530, and UPC Code 810002862779. The recalled ovens are stainless steel and have two spring-loaded glass front French doors with metal handles. The Ovens can be identified with model and UPC codes on packaging. The Model number GTF7530 and codes 3021 to 0126 can also be found on a label on back of the unit.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled Ovens immediately and contact Gourmia for a free repair kit by mail. The repair kit will include an added doorstop and instructions on how to install.. Gourmia toll free at 888-834-0092 Monday through Friday 9 a.m. to 5p.m. or online at www.gourmia.com/recall.asp or Gourmia.com and click on the “Recall Information” link at the top of the page for more information.
