What was recalled

Spin-on engine oil filter for diesel engines, sold as both a standalone part and as part of service kits. Suitable for the following vehicles:

  • Isuzu D-Max (TFR/TFS series, 06/2012-2026)
  • Isuzu MU-X (UCS85, 11/2013-06/2021)
  • Isuzu MU-X (UCR40/UCS40, 07/2021-2026)
  • Mazda BT50 (B30 TFR40/TFS40, 08/2020-2026) with 3.0L diesel 4JJ1-TCX or 4JJ3-TCX engines

Standalone oil filter (sold at Basco Wholesale and Allied Auto Online):

  • Part number BFO-15671 (found on packaging and filter can)
  • Barcode 9356247149707

Filter service kit containing affected oil filter (sold at Bunnings Warehouse):

  • Kit number BUNF5
  • Barcode 9356247157535

Why it was recalled

The base seal of the oil filter may prevent the thread from screwing on properly and cause the oil filter to loosen or come off while the vehicle is being driven, causing oil to spill out of the engine. This can happen even when the filter has been fitted correctly, and it can happen without any warning to the driver.

What to do

  1. Stop driving the vehicle immediately if you have the oil filter installed and do not use your vehicle until the oil filter is replaced. If you have not yet fitted the oil filter, do not install it.
  2. If the oil filter has been installed in your vehicle, Basco will cover the reasonable costs of having your vehicle inspected and oil filter replaced. Please provide your receipts to Basco when you contact them.
  3. If the oil filter has not been installed yet:
    • If you purchased the standalone oil filter from Basco or Allied Auto Online, contact Basco to arrange a free replacement or full refund,
    • If you purchased the filter service kit from Bunnings, return the kit to any Bunnings store for a full refund, or contact Basco to arrange a free replacement oil filter.
  4. Contact Basco for further information or assistance with this recall.
