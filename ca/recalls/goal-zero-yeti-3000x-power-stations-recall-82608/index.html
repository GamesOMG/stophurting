Goal Zero YETI 3000X Power Stations Recall (September 2026)

Fire and burn hazards.

WhatGoal Zero YETI 3000X Power Stations
BrandGoal Zero
The hazardfire and burn hazards
CategoryElectronics
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byDistributor Goal Zero Midvale, Utah United States Manufacturer ProVista Technology International Ltd. Kowloon Hong Kong
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-11

What was recalled

This recall involves Goal Zero YETI 3000X power stations. The silver and black portable power stations measure about 62 centimetres (24.4 inches) high, 47 centimetres (18.5 inches) wide, 57 centimetres (22.3 inches) deep and weigh approximately 42 kilograms (92 pounds). The power stations have yellow handles and a digital display at the front with electrical ports. The power stations sit on a rolling cart with built-in wheels and a telescoping handle. The Goal Zero logo is printed on the front bar of the cart and on a nameplate located on the underside of the power station’s lid along with “YETI 3000X”.

Why it was recalled

The recalled power station’s circuit board may overheat, posing a risk of fire and burn hazards.

As of August 31, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and install the free YETI 3000X firmware update available through the Goal Zero application or contact Goal Zero for the firmware installation at no cost. 

For more information, consumers can contact Goal Zero by telephone at 1-888-794-6250, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. MST Monday to Friday, by email at support@goalzero.com or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 509 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from May 2020 to August 2025.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
