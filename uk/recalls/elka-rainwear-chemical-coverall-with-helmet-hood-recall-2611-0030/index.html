What was recalled

Flame-retardant coverall with chemical and antistatic protection for professional work environments with high safety requirements. Codes can be found on the care label and product packaging.

Why it was recalled

The affected products are Category III PPE intended to provide protection against heat and flame, chemicals and electrostatic hazards. The manufacturer’s routine monitoring revealed that the claimed flame-protection performance was not met for the product. There is risk of burn injury due to inadequate protection.

The product is not in compliance with the Personal Protective Equipment Regulation (EU) 2016/425.

What to do

The manufacturer has undertaken a recall of all the affected products.

The product has been withdrawn from the market until further production using alternative fabric has completed appropriate the conformity assessment procedures.

End users should stop use of the product immediately and contact the manufacturer.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2611-0030

Notified by: Health and Safety Executive
