Ocun CONDOR HMS Triple Carabiners Recall (July 2026)

The products present a risk of serious injuries.

WhatOcun CONDOR HMS Triple Carabiners
BrandOcun
The hazardThe products present a risk of serious injuries because the gate can malfunction and may not close automatically.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Trekking/Mountaineering Sports Equipment
Made inUnknown
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-29 (notice 2607-0233)

What was recalled

Carabiner for rock-climbing and mountaineering.

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk of serious injuries because the gate can malfunction and may not close automatically. This creates a risk of unintended detachment during use, which could result in a fall from height. 

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/425 on Personal Protective Equipment.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by Fletcher Stewart. We recommend that owners stop using the product immediately. Please visit https://www.fletcherstewart.com/ocun-03550-product-recall to complete a recall form and arrange for a replacement.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0233

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
