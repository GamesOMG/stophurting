The hazardRisk of serious injury, death and/or property damage from the heat pads overheating or catching fire while in use. Incidents have occurred.
Sold atHarvey Norman stores

ContactSynargy Call: 07 3177 7715 from 8:30am to 5:00pm AEST, Monday to Friday. Email: heatpads@synargy.com Visit: www.synargy.com
CategoryIndoor furniture and furnishings
Recall date2026-07-15

What was recalled

Electric recliners and sofas with heat pads

  • Essex – 231946
  • Maxim – 231980
  • Sierra – 216872

Why it was recalled

The heat pad fitted within the affected sofas can overheat during use and may cause a fire.

What to do

  1. Stop using the heat pad function immediately and disconnect the sofa or recliner from the power outlet.
  2. Contact Synargy to arrange assessment and repair of the heat pad.
  3. An authorised repair agent will attend the consumer’s home, inspect and replace the affected heat pad with an upgraded part, free of charge.
  4. For more information about this recall please contact Synargy.
