Steambow Archery AR-Series Laser Sight Recall (June 2026)

Eye injury hazard.

Recall date2026-06-05

What was recalled

This recall involves the Steambow Archery AR-Series Laser Sight (Green), Model# 0000361 with UPC 8 12237 03065 7. The laser sight integrates with the rig via a solid aluminum mount. The green laser provides visibility with a wavelength of 532 nm and <5 mW power. The pack contains a green laser sight, battery, mount for installing on the magazine and necessary screws and tools.

Why it was recalled

Health Canada has determined, after testing, that this product is classified as a Class 3B handheld portable laser. Use of Class 3B or 4 handheld portable lasers present an increased risk to consumers of permanent eye injury. Injuries can result from the laser beam being directed in the eyes, even from short unintentional exposure. 

As of ​May 22, 2026​, the company has received no reports of incidents in Canada and no reports of injury. 

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and return it to Leapfrog Outdoor Sports and Apparel for a refund.

For more information, consumers can contact Leapfrog Outdoor Sports and Apparel by telephone at 587-486-0563, Tuesday to Friday from 10:00-17:00 MST, by email at info@leapfrogoutdoor.ca or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background
