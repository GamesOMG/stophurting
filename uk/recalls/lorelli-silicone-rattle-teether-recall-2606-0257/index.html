Lorelli Silicone Rattle Teether Recall (September 2026)

Risk of choking.

WhatLorelli Silicone Rattle Teether
BrandLorelli
The hazardThe product presents a risk of choking because the ball within the teether can detach during use, creating small parts which fit entirely within a small parts cylinder.
Product typeToys – Sensory Toys
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-04 (notice 2606-0257)

What was recalled

Silicone teether containing a ball, supplied in multiple colours.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of choking because the ball within the teether can detach during use, creating small parts which fit entirely within a small parts cylinder. These small parts pose a choking risk to young children, particularly those under 36 months of age who have a natural tendency to explore objects by placing them in the mouth.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Lorelli.

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately, and keep it out of the reach of children. Contact the distributor you purchased from to request redress.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2606-0257

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
