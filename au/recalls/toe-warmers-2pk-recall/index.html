Recall

Toe warmers 2pk Recall (July 2026)

Serious burn injuries and irritation to the skin or eyes.

WhatToe warmers 2pk
The hazardRisk of serious burn injuries and irritation to the skin or eyes if the product overheats or bursts while in use. Incidents of the warmer overheating and bursting have occurred.
Sold atThe Reject Shop
Where it was soldNationally
On sale3 April 2026 - 25 June 2026
Recalled byThe Reject Shop
Made inChina
ContactThe Reject Shop Email: service@rejectshop.com.au Visit: https://www.rejectshop.com.au/customerservice/product-recalls
CategoryHeating and cooling products
Recall date2026-07-14

What was recalled

Air-activated adhesive heat packs used for warming toes.

Batch: 260125

Why it was recalled

When the product is used, it may inflate and heat rapidly. The product can become very hot and burst open.

What to do

  1. Stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of children.
  2. If the product is still in its packaging, do not open it.
  3. Return the product to The Reject Shop for a full refund. Provide proof of purchase and/or return the unopened product or packaging where possible.
