Joolz Aer2 Car Seat Adapter Set Recall (May 2026)

Risk of injuries.

WhatJoolz Aer2 Car Seat Adapter Set
BrandJoolz
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries because the car seat adapter may fail to properly engage and lock with the spring-loaded pin on the stroller chassis.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Baby Car/Booster Seats
Made inChina
Risk levelMedium
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-29 (notice 2605-0189)

What was recalled

Black car seat adaptor to connect an infant car seat to a Joolz Aer2 stroller chassis.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries because the car seat adapter may fail to properly engage and lock with the spring-loaded pin on the stroller chassis. If this were to happen, the car seat can fully detach from the stroller and fall to the ground.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Joolz. Consumers are asked to stop using the product immediately.

For more information on this recall and to register for replacement adapters please visit: Sites-joolz-gb-Site or contact csa@joolz.com.

For products sold by John Lewis, access: Product Recalls - John Lewis & Partners
