Recall

SHEIN Distribution Corporation Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking — about 2,657 units.

WhatSHEIN Distribution Corporation Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets
How manyAbout 2,657 units
The hazardThe recalled toy sets violate the small parts ban because they are intended for children under three years old and the purple and orange knobs can detach from the product, posing a deadly choking hazard to young children.
Sold atSold Online At: US.Shein.com from November 2025 through July 2026 for about $10
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled toy sets immediately, take them away from children, and contact SHEIN for a refund. Consumers will be asked to affirm they disposed of the toy sets to receive a refund.
ContactSHEIN toll-free at 833-853-8668 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, by email at uscsteam@shein.com or online at https://us.shein.com/product-recalls/list or https://us.shein.com and click on “Product Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27083)

What was recalled

This recall involves Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets. The sets include seven toys in a variety of pastel colors and shapes. SKU “sk25070936134444119” is located on the upper right corner of a label on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled toy sets immediately, take them away from children, and contact SHEIN for a refund. Consumers will be asked to affirm they disposed of the toy sets to receive a refund.. SHEIN toll-free at 833-853-8668 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, by email at uscsteam@shein.com or online at https://us.shein.com/product-recalls/list or https://us.shein.com and click on “Product Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
