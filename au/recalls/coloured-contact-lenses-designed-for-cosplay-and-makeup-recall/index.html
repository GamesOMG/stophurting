What was recalled

White coloured contact lenses designed for cosplay and makeup applications. Supplied in blister packs.

Why it was recalled

The packaging solution for these contact lenses may be contaminated with Burkholderia cepacia complex bacteria.

What to do

  1. Stop using these contact lenses immediately and dispose of the contact lenses safely.
  2. Seek medical attention immediately if you experience any eye irritation or other eye symptoms.
  3. SHEIN will provide a full refund and send a recall notice by email. To receive the refund, read the recall notice in the email and click “Yes, confirm” to acknowledge receipt and accept the refund.
  4. Contact SHEIN if you have not received the email, are having difficulties with the email or if you need further information relating to this recall.
