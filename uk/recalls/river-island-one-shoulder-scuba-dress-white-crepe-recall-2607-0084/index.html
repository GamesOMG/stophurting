River Island One Shoulder Scuba Dress, White Crepe Skort and White Crepe Top Recall (July 2026)

Serious chemical risk as the attached brooch contains an excess concentration of cadmium.

WhatRiver Island One Shoulder Scuba Dress, White Crepe Skort and White Crepe Top
BrandRiver Island
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk as the attached brooch contains an excess concentration of cadmium.
Product typeClothing, textiles and fashion items - Clothing Adornment/Floral Accessories/Badges/Buckles
Made inUnknown
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-10 (notice 2607-0084)

What was recalled

One shoulder scuba dress in pink and blue, white crepe skort and white crepe top with bow/heart-shaped brooch details. All childrenswear sizes are affected.

Why it was recalled

The products present a serious chemical risk as the casings of the heart- and bow-shaped brooches contain an excess of cadmium.

Further information on the health effects of cadmium exposure can be accessed on GOV.UK here: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cadmium-properties-incident-management-and-toxicology

The products do not meet the requirements of the REACH Regulations 2008.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by River Island.

We recommend that you stop using the products immediately and return them to River Island for a full refund. For more information on returning the items, please access: https://www.riverisland.com/product-recall or contact River Island via customer.services@river-island.com, phone: +44 (0) 3445766444.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0084, 2607-0093, 2607-0095, 2607-0097

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
