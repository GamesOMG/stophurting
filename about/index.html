About

StopHurting tracks every U.S. consumer product recall, automatically. The moment the Consumer Product Safety Commission publishes a recall, a page appears here — what was recalled, why it's dangerous, and what to do about it, with a link to the official notice. No editorializing, no fear-mongering: if a page here ever disagrees with the official notice, the notice wins.

Before the recalls, this site fact-checked viral health claims. Those articles live on in the Myth Check archive — same mission, older format: sorting what's actually dangerous from what just sounds scary.

Nothing here is legal or medical advice.
