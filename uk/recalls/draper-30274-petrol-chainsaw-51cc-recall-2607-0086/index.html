Recall

Draper 30274 Petrol Chainsaw 51cc Recall (July 2026)

Risk of injuries.

WhatDraper 30274 Petrol Chainsaw 51cc
BrandDraper Tools Ltd
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries because the plastic chain brake lever can melt or deform during use.
Product typeMachinery – Chain Saws (Powered)
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-10 (notice 2607-0086)

What was recalled

Green handheld chainsaw.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries because the plastic chain brake lever can melt or deform during use. This could result in the chain brake mechanism failing, and the user being unable to stop the chain, leading to injury.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Draper Tools.

We recommend that owners stop using the product immediately and return it to your nearest Draper store.

For more information, please access Draper - Product Safety Recalls or contact Draper Tools at customerservice@drapertools.com or +44 (0) 23 8049 4344.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0086

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
