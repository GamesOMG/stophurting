Babybee toddler board with detachable seat Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury to a child from a fall.

WhatBabybee toddler board with detachable seat
BrandBabybee
The hazardRisk of serious injury to a child from a fall if the detachable seat breaks while in use. Incidents have occurred.
Sold atBabybee (online) Milly + Coup (online) The Memo (online and retail stores)
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale10 February 2025 - 16 August 2026
Recalled byBabybee Prams Pty Ltd
Made inChina
CategoryPrams and strollers
Recall date2026-08-21

What was recalled

The Babybee toddler board is a stroller accessory that can be used as a standing toddler board or with a detachable seat.

Only the detachable seat is recalled. The toddler board itself is not affected.

The toddler boards with the affected detachable seat have batch numbers X-001, X-002, X-012A and X-015D. These can be found on the underside of the toddler board.

Why it was recalled

The detachable seat may break at the point where the seat attaches to the main pole of the product. 

What to do

  1. Stop using the toddler board immediately and check the batch number on the underside of your Babybee toddler board.
  2. If your toddler board has batch number X-001, X-002, X-012A or X-015D, remove the detachable seat from the toddler board. The toddler board may continue to be used in standing mode without the detachable seat.
  3. Dispose of the affected detachable seat in your household general waste bin.
  4. Register your affected product at: https://babybeeonline.com/pages/recall
    Babybee will provide affected consumers with a replacement detachable seat free of charge. Replacement seats are expected to be available from approximately early October 2026. Registered consumers will be contacted when replacement seats are ready for dispatch and will receive updates if this timing changes.
  5. Contact Babybee if you require further information or assistance with this recall.
