GIANT SureLock Bicycle Locks with LED Key Recall (October 2026)

Ingestion hazard.

What was recalled

This recall involves GIANT bicycle locks supplied with an LED key. The LED key contains two CR1220 lithium manganese dioxide button/coin cell batteries.

Why it was recalled

The LED key supplied with the affected bicycle locks contains two CR1220 lithium manganese dioxide button/coin cell batteries. The button/coin cell batteries may become accessible from the LED key. If swallowed or inserted into the body, button/coin cell batteries can cause serious internal injuries. The identified hazard is associated with the LED key only. The bicycle lock itself is not affected. Keys supplied with the SureLock bicycle lock other than the LED key are not affected and remain functional.

As of September 4, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada or the United States.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the LED Key (the recalled product) accompanying the SureLock Bicycle Lock and return it to a GIANT retailer for a partial refund.

For more information, consumers can contact GIANT by telephone at 604-987-3600, 8 am to 5 pm PT, Monday to Friday, by email at canada@giantbicycle.com or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and GIANT.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 1,395 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 3,628 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from March 2024 to August 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
