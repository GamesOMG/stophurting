Baylis & Harding Oud, Cedar & Amber 1L Hand Wash Refill Recall (September 2026)

Microbiological risk as testing identified the presence of Pluralibacter gergoviae.

WhatBaylis & Harding Oud, Cedar & Amber 1L Hand Wash Refill
BrandBaylis & Harding PLC
The hazardThe product presents a microbiological risk as testing identified the presence of Pluralibacter gergoviae.
Product typeCosmetics, personal care and grooming – Cleansing/Washing/Soap - Body
Made inUnited Kingdom
Risk levelLow
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-16 (notice 2609-0060)

What was recalled

1 litre eco refill of oud, cedar and amber scented hand wash. The product was sold via Ocado, Sainsbury’s, and Waitrose.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a microbiological risk as testing identified the presence of Pluralibacter gergoviae. Contamination with this microorganism may lead to infection or adverse health effects, particularly in vulnerable users or those with a compromised skin barrier. 

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 (the ‘Cosmetics Regulation’).

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by Baylis and Harding, Amazon, Ocado, Sainsbury’s, and Waitrose.  

Consumers should stop using the product immediately and return it to their place of purchase for redress.  

For further information, please access specific retailer websites below: 

  • Sainsbury’s - https://help.sainsburys.co.uk/help/products/product-recall-baylis-harding-goodness-oud-cedar-and-amber-1-litre-hand-wash-refill-pouch  

  • Waitrose - https://www.waitrose.com/ecom/help-information/product-recalls
