Workbless Pressure Washers Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Shock and Electrocution hazard — about 2,200 (In addition, about 657 in Canada) units.

WhatWorkbless Pressure Washers
How manyAbout 2,200 (In addition, about 657 in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled pressure washers lack an integral ground-fault circuit-interrupter (GFCI) and an adequate-length power cord, posing serious risks of injury or death from shock and electrocution hazards.
Sold atAmazon.com, BestBuy, Kohls, Lowe's, Overstock, Target and Walmart from April 2025 through July 2026 for between $97 and $140.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled pressure washers immediately and contact Workbless for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the pressure washer by cutting the unplugged power cord in half and send a photo of the destroyed pressure washer to service@workbless.net. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed recalled product.
ContactWorkbless by email at service@workbless.net, toll-free at 833-725-3151 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT, Monday through Friday, or online at https://www.workbless.net/recall/.
Recall date2026-08-20 (recall no. 26715)

What was recalled

This recall involves Workbless-branded pressure washers, models WB0301 and WB0302. Model WB0301 is green and black and measures about 27.24 inches high, 10.83 inches wide and 8.39 inches deep. "WORKBLESS"," WB0301" and "ELECTRIC PRESSURE WASHER" are printed on a label on the back of the pressure washer. Model WB0302 is also green and black and measures about 29.57 inches high, 14.69 inches wide and 10.94 inches deep. "WORKBLESS", "WB0302" and "HEAVY DUTY ELECTRIC PRESSURE WASHER" are printed on a label on the back of the pressure washer. Both models came with four quick connect nozzles and a foam cannon.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled pressure washers immediately and contact Workbless for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the pressure washer by cutting the unplugged power cord in half and send a photo of the destroyed pressure washer to service@workbless.net. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed recalled product.. Workbless by email at service@workbless.net, toll-free at 833-725-3151 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT, Monday through Friday, or online at https://www.workbless.net/recall/.
