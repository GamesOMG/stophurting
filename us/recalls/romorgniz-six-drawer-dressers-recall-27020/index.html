Romorgniz Six-Drawer Dressers Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about 12,078 units.

WhatRomorgniz Six-Drawer Dressers
How manyAbout 12,078 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from May 2025 through August 2026 for between $100 and $170.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact Romorgniz for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the top, front and sides of the dresser, remove all drawers and send photos of the marked and disassembled dresser to Romorgnizrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product.
ContactRomorgniz via email at Romorgnizrecall@outlook.com, online at https://www.romorgniz.store/recall.html or https://www.romorgniz.store/ and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-08 (recall no. 27020)

What was recalled

This recall involves Romorgniz six-drawer dressers. The wooden dressers were sold in white and black, measure about 27.6 inches wide, 15.6 inches long, 43.2 inches tall and weigh 73.3 pounds.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact Romorgniz for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the top, front and sides of the dresser, remove all drawers and send photos of the marked and disassembled dresser to Romorgnizrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product..
