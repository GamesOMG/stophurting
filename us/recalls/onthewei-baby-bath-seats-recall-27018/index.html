Recall

ONTHEWEI Baby Bath Seats Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death to Children from Drowning — about 2,140 units.

WhatONTHEWEI Baby Bath Seats
How manyAbout 2,140 units
The hazardThe recalled bath seats violate the mandatory standard for infant bath seats because they are unstable and can tip over while in use, posing a risk of serious injury or death due to drowning.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com in April 2026 for between $40 and $50. · Hubei Youhong Trading Co., Ltd., dba ONTHEWEI, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled bath seats immediately and contact ONTHEWEI for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the bath seat by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the front of the bath seat, disassembling it by removing the back rest and arm restraints and cutting the three suction cups on the seat’s underside and send a photo of the destroyed bath seat showing the marked product to onthewei@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactONTHEWEI by email at onthewei@outlook.com
Recall date2026-10-08 (recall no. 27018)

What was recalled

This recall involves ONTHEWEI-branded baby bath seats. They are sold in gray, pink, blue and mist green and have detachable restraint arms and suction cups on the underside. “Model No: MY002” and “Date of manufacturer: 2026/1/10” are printed on a label on the back of the bath seat.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled bath seats immediately and contact ONTHEWEI for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the bath seat by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the front of the bath seat, disassembling it by removing the back rest and arm restraints and cutting the three suction cups on the seat’s underside and send a photo of the destroyed bath seat showing the marked product to onthewei@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. ONTHEWEI by email at onthewei@outlook.com
