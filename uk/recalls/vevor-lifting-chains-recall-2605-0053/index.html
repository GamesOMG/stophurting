Vevor Lifting Chains Recall (May 2026)

Risk of injuries as the chains can break under normal use and cause injuries to the user.

WhatVevor Lifting Chains
BrandVevor
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries as the chains can break under normal use and cause injuries to the user.
Product typeMachinery - Ropes/Chains/Cables (Fixings/Fasteners)
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-19 (notice 2605-0053)

What was recalled

Two models of lifting chain with orange fixings.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries as the chains can break under normal use and cause injuries to the user. 

The product does not meet the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by Vevor.  

We recommend that you stop using the product immediately and contact the distributor for redress. 

For further information, access the Vevor recall page at: Article - VEVOR UK

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0053

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
