Wizz Toys 6.5cm Decorative Wishing Bottle Recall (July 2026)

Risk to health as the coloured sand included in the bottles may be contaminated with a sm….

WhatWizz Toys 6.5cm Decorative Wishing Bottle
BrandWizz Toys
The hazardThe product presents a risk to health as the coloured sand included in the bottles may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos.
Product typeDecorative articles/Homeware/Gardenware - Ornaments (Non Powered)
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-21 (notice 2607-0182)

What was recalled

Variety of decorative wishing bottles.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk to health as the sand inside the bottles may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos. Asbestos is a banned substance because it poses a risk to health even at low levels of exposure.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by PMS International.

We recommend owners stop using the product immediately and keep it out of the reach of children. Follow the below instructions for details on disposal and return of the products.

For further information, access the PMS International website: https://b2b.pmsinternational.com/product-recall/ or contact  productrecall@pmsinternational.com

Disposal instructions:
  • Stop using the product immediately.
  • If the sand is still in its packaging, place in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double tape it securely, label it clearly and store in a secure location out of the reach of children.
  • If the sand has been used, clean up sites where the product was used using wet cloths to avoid generating dust. Wear gloves and a mask. Double bag the sand, gloves, mask and cloths.
  • Keep children and other people away from areas where the sand has been used until the area has been cleaned.
  • Return the product to store where specified by the retailer for a full refund.
  • If you are unable to return the product to the retailer, follow the steps above and contact your local authority / access their website for guidance on safe disposal of asbestos in your area.
  • For further information, please contact the retailer or visit the GOV.UK page on asbestos in consumer products.
Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0182

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
