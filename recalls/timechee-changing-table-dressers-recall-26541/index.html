Recall

Timechee Changing Table Dressers Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about About 10,354 units.

WhatTimechee Changing Table Dressers
How manyAbout About 10,354 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in a risk of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standard as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from September 2023 through May 2026 for about $190.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact Timechee for instructions on how to identify affected units and dispose of the dressers to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to email a photo to TimecheeRecall@timechee.com demonstrating disposal of the product.
ContactTimechee toll-free at 833-851-3720 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, or by email at TimecheeRecall@timechee.com.
Recall date2026-06-11 (recall no. 26541)

What was recalled

This recall involves Timechee Changing Table Dressers. The recalled dressers are made of wood and were sold in white. The dresser has five drawers and two shelves, and a two-section changing table top. It measures 47.2 inches long, by 19.5 inches wide, by 36.1 inches tall and weighs 160 pounds. The dresser comes packaged in a cardboard box with "KF200066US-01XN-003" printed on the side.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact Timechee for instructions on how to identify affected units and dispose of the dressers to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to email a photo to TimecheeRecall@timechee.com demonstrating disposal of the product.. Timechee toll-free at 833-851-3720 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, or by email at TimecheeRecall@timechee.com.
