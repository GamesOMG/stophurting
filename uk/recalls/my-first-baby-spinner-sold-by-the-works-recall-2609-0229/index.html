My First Baby Spinner sold by The Works Recall (October 2026)

Risk of choking.

WhatMy First Baby Spinner sold by The Works
BrandPlayworks
The hazardThe product presents a risk of choking as it has small parts that can detach or break off during reasonably foreseeable use.
Product typeToys - Baby/Infant Stimulation Toys (Non Powered)
Made inChina
Risk levelMedium
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-02 (notice 2609-0229)

What was recalled

Spinning toy with suction-cup base, intended for babies/toddlers. Colours may vary.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of choking as it has small parts that can detach or break off during reasonably foreseeable use. These small parts pose a choking risk to young children, particularly those under 36 months of age who have a natural tendency to explore objects by placing them in their mouths.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by The Works. We recommend owners stop using this product immediately and keep it out of the reach of babies and children.

For more information, please access: Product Recalls at The Works or contact The Works via enquiries@theworks.co.uk / 0121 313 6050.

Additional information

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
