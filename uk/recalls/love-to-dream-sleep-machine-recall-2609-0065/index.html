Recall

Love to Dream Sleep Machine Recall (September 2026)

Risk of fire.

WhatLove to Dream Sleep Machine
BrandLove to Dream
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire because the lithium-ion battery in the product may overheat during charging/
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Baby Safety Monitoring (Powered)
Made inUnknown
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-10 (notice 2609-0065)

What was recalled

A portable white noise sound machine with night light. The date code is on the side of the Sleep Machine, or on the back of the box. No other date code is currently included in the recall.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire because the lithium-ion battery in the product may overheat during charging, which could pose a fire hazard to consumers.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by the manufacturer Love to Dream and the following retailers: Boots, Harrods, and John Lewis and Partners.

Consumers should stop using the product immediately. For more information on disposal instructions and claiming a refund or replacement, contact Love to Dream on recall@lovetodream.com or visit the specific websites below:

  • Love to Dream UK
  • Boots
  • Harrods UK
  • John Lewis & Partners
Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0065

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
