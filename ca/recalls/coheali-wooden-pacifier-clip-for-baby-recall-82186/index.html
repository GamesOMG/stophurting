COHEALI Wooden Pacifier Clip for baby Recall (June 2026)

Choking hazard.

WhatCOHEALI Wooden Pacifier Clip for baby
The hazardChoking hazard
CategoryOther
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byDistributor Gallegils Shenzhen , Guangdong China
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-06-10

What was recalled

This alert involves the COHEALI Wooden Pacifier Clip for baby sold on Amazon.ca.

Why it was recalled

The product is intended for children under three years of age. The screw linking the wooden piece to the metal clip can become loose posing a choking risk to children.

What to do

Consumers who have this affected product should immediately stop using it and safely dispose of the item in such a way that it cannot be used again.

Regularly check the Healthy Canadians Recalls and Safety Alerts database for dangerous products and take action to remove items of concern.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of these products or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.
