Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stool Recall (July 2026)

Tip-over hazard.

WhatBoon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stool
BrandBoon
The hazardtip-over hazard
CategoryFurniture and furnishings
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byImporter TOMY International, Inc. Oak Brook, IL Unites States
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-07-16

What was recalled

This recall involves the Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stool. The stool is a folding step stool designed for use by children in the kitchen and is available in gray and white. The affected models are listed in the table below.

Why it was recalled

The stool may be unstable while in use, posing a tip-over hazard to users.

As of July 9, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries in Canada. In the United States, the company has received 11 reports involving the stools being unstable and no injuries have been reported.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact TOMY International, Inc. for a repair kit.

For more information, consumers can contact TOMY Consumer Care Team by telephone at 1-866-725-4407 between 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. CT Monday through Friday, by email at cs@tomy.com or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and TOMY International, Inc. 

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 2,033 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 105,391 were sold in the United States. 

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from January 2023 to March 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
