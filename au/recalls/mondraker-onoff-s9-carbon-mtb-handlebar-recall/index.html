Recall

Mondraker ONOFF S9 carbon MTB handlebar Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or death.

WhatMondraker ONOFF S9 carbon MTB handlebar
BrandMONDRAKER
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death if the handlebar cracks or breaks and causes a rider to lose control of the bicycle and fall. Incidents of the handlebar breaking have occurred.
Sold atPlease see attached list of affected serial numbers and traders.
Where it was soldNationally
On sale15 May 2025 - 5 June 2026
Recalled byBlue Factory Team, SLU t/a Mondraker
Made inTaiwan
ContactONOFF/Mondraker Email: recall@mondraker.com Visit: https://mondraker.com/wo/en/recall-s9
CategoryBicycles and scooters (push)
Recall date2026-07-24

What was recalled

OnOff S9 carbon MTB handlebar supplied as original on certain Mondraker mountain bikes.

  • Model: ONOFF S9

Affected bike models:

  • Crafty Carbon RR S
  • Crafty Carbon XR
  • Crafty Carbon RR SL
  • Crafty Unlimited Gulf
  • Dune Carbon XR
  • Foxy Carbon RR
  • Level XR
  • Neat RR SL
  • Zendit R
  • Zendit RR
  • Zendit RR S
  • Zendit XR
  • Raze Carbon RR

Please see attached list of affected serial numbers and traders.

Why it was recalled

Due to a manufacturing defect, the handlebar can crack or break unexpectedly during use.

How to identify it

The serial number can be found on the down tube or on the bottom bracket of the bike.

What to do

  1. Stop using the affected bicycle immediately.
  2. Check if your bicycle is an affected bicycle by entering the bicycle serial number on the ONOFF/Mondraker recall website.
  3. Contact the place of purchase or your authorised Mondraker dealer to arrange for a free inspection, replacement and installation of a new reinforced handlebar.
  4. For more information or assistance with this recall, contact the place of purchase or your authorised Mondraker dealer.
