Xinan Home WinxMatrs Mattresses Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 792 units.

WhatXinan Home WinxMatrs Mattresses
How manyAbout 792 units
The hazardThe recalled mattresses violate the mandatory flammability standard for mattresses, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from August 2025 through November 2025 for about $120.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Xinan Home for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED,” on the top surface of the mattress and on the mattress label and send photos of the marked mattress and of the label showing Xinan Home to WinxMatrsMattressRecall@outlook.com.
ContactXinan Home by email at WinxMatrsMattressRecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-09-24 (recall no. 26793)

What was recalled

This recall involves WinxMatrs-branded mattresses. The black mattresses are 10 inches thick and were sold in a twin size. They were sold compressed in a box. The size and the brand are printed on the mattress’ sewn-in labels.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled mattresses immediately and contact Xinan Home for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the mattress by writing in permanent marker “RECALLED,” on the top surface of the mattress and on the mattress label and send photos of the marked mattress and of the label showing Xinan Home to WinxMatrsMattressRecall@outlook.com.. Xinan Home by email at WinxMatrsMattressRecall@outlook.com.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-09-24. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
