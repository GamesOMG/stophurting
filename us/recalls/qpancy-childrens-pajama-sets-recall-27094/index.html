Recall

Qpancy Children’s Pajama Sets Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Burn hazard — about 1,309 units.

WhatQpancy Children’s Pajama Sets
How manyAbout 1,309 units
The hazardThe recalled children’s pajama sets violate the mandatory safety standards for children’s sleepwear posing a risk of serious burn injuries.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from March 2026 through August 2026 for about $20. · Shanghai Shengkailong Trading Co., Ltd., dba Qpancy, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled pajama sets immediately and contact Qpancy for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pajama in half and send a photo of the destroyed pajamas to qpancyrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.
ContactQpancy by email at qpancyrecall@outlook.com
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27094)

What was recalled

This recall involves Qpancy-branded matching pajama sets. They include children’s pajamas that consist of a long-sleeve, button-front top with a front pocket and shorts, and matching pajamas for dolls. The sets were sold in multicolored cat, pink bow, pink and white striped bow, and pink and white stripes, and in sizes 120 (4–5T) through 150 (10–11Y). The brand is printed on a neck label and the size on a sewn-in side-seam label.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled pajama sets immediately and contact Qpancy for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pajama in half and send a photo of the destroyed pajamas to qpancyrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.. Qpancy by email at qpancyrecall@outlook.com
