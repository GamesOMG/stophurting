Recall

Papablic, of USA Convertible Bedside Sleepers Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment, Suffocation and Fall hazard — about 248 units.

WhatPapablic, of USA Convertible Bedside Sleepers
How manyAbout 248 units
The hazardThe bedside sleepers with play yard extensions violate the mandatory safety standard for play yards because the mattress can become displaced causing a baby to become entrapped between the sleeping pad and bottom posing a deadly suffocation hazard. In addition, the latching and locking mechanism can fail, causing the product to collapse, posing a risk of serious injury or deadly entrapment and fall hazards.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com and papablic.com from April 2025 through July 2026 for about $190.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the convertible bedside sleeper immediately and contact Papablic for a refund. Consumers will be asked to disassemble the product, cut the mattress and fabric components in half and email photos of the destroyed components to support@papablic.com. Upon receipt of proof of destruction, Papablic will issue a full refund and consumers should dispose of the product.
ContactPapablic by email at support@papablic.com, or online at https://papablic.com/pages/black-and-gray-bassinet-with-play-yard-extension-recall or https://papablic.com and click on “Recall Information” at the bottom of the page.
Recall date2026-10-15 (recall no. 27027)

What was recalled

This recall involves Papablic bedside sleepers, model PAPA03001AI. These convertible sleepers function as both a bassinet and a play yard and have a gray exterior with an interior printed in a stars‑and‑moon pattern. The products have “Papablic” stitched in white on the front gray fabric panel. Model “PAPA03001AI” is printed on a label sewn to the interior fabric of the bassinet underneath the mattress pad.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the convertible bedside sleeper immediately and contact Papablic for a refund. Consumers will be asked to disassemble the product, cut the mattress and fabric components in half and email photos of the destroyed components to support@papablic.com. Upon receipt of proof of destruction, Papablic will issue a full refund and consumers should dispose of the product.. Papablic by email at support@papablic.com, or online at https://papablic.com/pages/black-and-gray-bassinet-with-play-yard-extension-recall or https://papablic.com and click on “Recall Information” at the bottom of the page.
