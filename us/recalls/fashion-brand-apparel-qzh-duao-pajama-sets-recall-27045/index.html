Recall

Fashion Brand Apparel QZH.DUAO Pajama Sets Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Burn hazard — about 300 units.

WhatFashion Brand Apparel QZH.DUAO Pajama Sets
How manyAbout 300 units
The hazardThe recalled children’s pajama sets violate the mandatory safety standards for children’s sleepwear, posing a risk of serious burn injuries.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from February 2026 through July 2026 for about $23. · Xingtai Yunchuang Commerce and Trade Ltd., dba Fashion Brand Apparel Co., of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled pajama sets immediately and contact Fashion Brand Apparel for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pajamas in half and email a photo of the destroyed pajama set to qzh.duaoproductrecall@outlook.com to receive the refund. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.
ContactFashion Brand Apparel at qzh.duaoproductrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27045)

What was recalled

This recall involves QZH.DUAO pajama sets. The pajama sets consist of short-sleeve tops and matching pants. They were sold in Pink Bow, Pink, Black, Light Blue, Purple, Mint Green, Hot Pink, Pink Bowknot, Blue Bowknot, Pink Strawberry and Pink Bow Flower and in sizes 4-5T through 13-14 years.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled pajama sets immediately and contact Fashion Brand Apparel for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pajamas in half and email a photo of the destroyed pajama set to qzh.duaoproductrecall@outlook.com to receive the refund. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.. Fashion Brand Apparel at qzh.duaoproductrecall@outlook.com.
