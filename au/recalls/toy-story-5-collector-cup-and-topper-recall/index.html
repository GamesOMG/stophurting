Toy Story 5 Collector Cup and Topper Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking.

WhatToy Story 5 Collector Cup and Topper
BrandGolden Link
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking if the topper breaks into small pieces and a child swallows it.
Sold atHoyts Cinemas
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale16 June 2026 - 29 June 2026
Recalled byHoyts
Made inTaiwan
ContactHoyts Call: 1300 146 987 (1300 1 HOYTS) Email: support@hoyts.zendesk.com Visit: www.hoyts.com.au/contact-us
CategoryKitchenware and containers · Novelty toys
Recall date2026-07-13

What was recalled

Toy Story 5 collectable cup with character figure topper.

Sold at Hoyts Cinemas.

Why it was recalled

The topper can break into small pieces and create a choking hazard for young children.

What to do

  1. Stop using the cup and topper immediately and keep out of reach of children.
  2. Return the product to a Hoyts cinema or contact Hoyts to arrange a refund.
  3. For further information or assistance with this recall, please contact Hoyts.
