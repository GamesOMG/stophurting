The Wonderland Collection K Mini Baby Thermal Drink Bottles Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking.

WhatThe Wonderland Collection K Mini Baby Thermal Drink Bottles
BrandThe Wonderland Collection
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking if small parts detach from the bottle and a child places them in their mouth.
Sold atKaisercraft
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale1 June 2026 - 9 September 2026
Recalled byKAISERCRAFT AUSTRALIA PTY LTD
Made inChina
ContactKaisercraft Head Office Call: 03 5240 2000, 9 am to 5 pm Monday to Friday Email: sales@kaisercraft.com.au Visit: https://www.kaisercraft.com.au/
CategoryBaby feeding aids
Recall date2026-09-17

What was recalled

Insulated drink bottles for babies.

Affected products include the following 350 mL Wonderland Collection K Mini Baby Thermal Drink Bottles:

  • Unicorn (product code KK4761)
  • Dino (product code KK4760)

Why it was recalled

The product may not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. The button clip cover may become detached from the product during use, creating small parts. 

What to do

  1. Stop using the product immediately and keep out of reach of children.
  2. Return the product to your nearest Kaisercraft store for a full refund.
