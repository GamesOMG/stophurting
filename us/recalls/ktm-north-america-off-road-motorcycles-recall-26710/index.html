Recall

KTM North America Off-Road Motorcycles Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Crash hazard — about 21,040 (In addition, about 4,140 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatKTM North America Off-Road Motorcycles
How manyAbout 21,040 (In addition, about 4,140 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe rear brake caliper can crack or break, reducing the brake system's effectiveness, posing a risk of serious injury or death due to crash hazard.
Sold atGASGAS and Husqvarna authorized dealers nationwide from September 2020 through June 2023 for between $7,300 and $13,000.
RemedyConsumers should stop riding the recalled motorcycles immediately and contact an authorized GASGAS or Husqvarna Motorcycle dealer to schedule a free repair at the dealership.
ContactGASGAS and Husqvarna toll-free at 888-985-6090 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, online at https://www.gasgas.com/en-us.html or https://www.husqvarna-motorcycles.com/en-us.html and click on "Safety Recall Information" located at the bottom of the page. Consumers can check the vehicle identification number (VIN) number at https://www.husqvarna-motorcycles.com/en-us/service/recalls-check.html or https://www.gasgas.com/en-us/service/recalls-check.html to determine if their unit is affected.
Recall date2026-08-20 (recall no. 26710)

What was recalled

This recall involves 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 off-road motorcycles. The GASGAS off-road motorcycles are red with the white GASGAS logo on both sides of the shrouds. The Husqvarna motorcycles are white, blue and yellow with the Husqvarna logo on both sides of the shrouds. The VIN and number are located on the right side of the steering head.
