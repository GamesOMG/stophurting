Recall

Primark Pepper Mills Recall (July 2026)

Serious chemical risk.

WhatPrimark Pepper Mills
BrandPrimark
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk because it contains excess concentrations of lead.
Product typeKitchen/cooking accessories - Salt/Pepper/Spice Mills (Non Powered)
Made inIndia
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-07 (notice 2607-0032)

What was recalled

Three models of pepper mill in brown and green.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious chemical risk because it contains excess concentrations of lead.

Further information regarding lead can be found on Gov.uk at the following link: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/lead-properties-incident-management-and-toxicology

The product does not meet the requirements of the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Primark. Consumers should stop using the product immediately and return it to their nearest Primark store for a full refund.

For further information, please access the Primark Live Chat: https://www.primark.com/help/GB

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2607-0032

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
