Recall

QIOMALA Pressure Washers Recall (November 2026)

Injury or Death from Shock and Electrocution hazard — about 80 units.

WhatQIOMALA Pressure Washers
How manyAbout 80 units
The hazardThe recalled pressure washers lack an adequate-length power cord, which can encourage use of an extension cord, posing a serious risk of injury or death from shock and electrocution hazards.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.comfrom April 2026through June 2026 for about $130.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled pressure washers immediately and contact QIOMALA for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to unplug the pressure washer, cut the power cord in half and send a photo of the destroyed power washer, showing the model number, to QIOMALA@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed recalled product.
ContactQIOMALA by email at QIOMALA@163.com.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27084)

What was recalled

This recall involves QIOMALA-branded pressure washers with model number “HWT12P”. The 1.2 GPM electric pressure washers were sold in green with black and in blue with black and measure about 14 inches deep, 11 inches wide and 15 inches high. The pressure washers came with a 500 ml soap tank, four quick disconnect nozzles and a 20-foot hose. Model number “HWT12P” is printed on a label on back of the unit, near the power cord entry point.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled pressure washers immediately and contact QIOMALA for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to unplug the pressure washer, cut the power cord in half and send a photo of the destroyed power washer, showing the model number, to QIOMALA@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed recalled product.. QIOMALA by email at QIOMALA@163.com.
