4-key keyboard switch clicker key chain Recall (September 2026)

Children are at risk of choking, severe internal burn injuries or death.

What4-key keyboard switch clicker key chain
The hazardChildren are at risk of choking, severe internal burn injuries or death if they swallow or place button/coin batteries inside their bodies. An incident of battery ingestion has occurred. Recalled byKingdomcity
Made inChina
ContactKingdomcity Call: 08 9409 2021, 9 am to 5 pm AWST, Monday to Friday Email: info.perth@kingdomcity.com Visit: https://www.kingdomcity.com
CategoryButton batteries · Novelty toys
Recall date2026-09-18

What was recalled

Novelty key chain with four keyboard-style buttons that light up when pressed. Provided as a free gift.

Why it was recalled

The product doesn’t comply with the mandatory standards for products containing button/coin batteries.

The battery isn’t secured, and children could access the button/coin battery.

The product is missing warning information about:

  • the dangers of button/coin batteries to children
  • what to do when a person suspects someone has swallowed or placed a button/coin battery inside any part of their body.

What to do

  1. Stop using the clicker toy immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Do not remove the batteries from the product. Return the product to any Kingdomcity location.
  3. Alternatively, safely dispose of the product by following the disposal instructions at Recycle Mate and notify Kingdomcity of the disposal.
