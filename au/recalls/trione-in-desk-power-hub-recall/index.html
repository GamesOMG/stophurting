Trione in-desk power hub Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury, death and/or property damage from fire or electric shock.

WhatTrione in-desk power hub
BrandJasonL
The hazardRisk of serious injury, death and/or property damage from fire or electric shock. Incidents have occurred.
Sold atJasonL
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale1 April 2025 - 6 July 2026
Recalled byJasonL
Made inChina
CategoryPower supply and storage
Recall date2026-07-15

What was recalled

Power and charging module hub designed to be installed in a desk workstation.

  • general power outlet, USB-A port, USB-C port, 5W wireless charging pad
  • product codes: OM-EAU5101E-BK, OM-EAU5101E-WT
  • Electrical approval number: E2026002423

Why it was recalled

Due to an internal fault the power hub can overheat, burn and melt.

What to do

1.      Stop using affected products immediately and disconnect from the power supply.
2.      Contact JasonL to arrange for one of the below remedies:

  • a full refund with a cable cover supplied to cover the opening in your desk.
  • a replacement product without wireless charging functionality with a partial refund.
  • a like for like replacement product.

3.      For more information or assistance with this recall, please contact JaonL
