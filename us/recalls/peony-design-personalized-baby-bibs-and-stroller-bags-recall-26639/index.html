Recall

Peony Design Personalized Baby Bibs and Stroller Bags Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking hazard — about 52 units.

WhatPeony Design Personalized Baby Bibs and Stroller Bags
How manyAbout 52 units
The hazardThe snap can detach from the recalled bibs and stroller bags, posing a risk of serious injury or death from a choking hazard to young children.
Sold atOnline at Etsy.com from October 2025 through July 2026 for between $15 and $45.
RemedyConsumers should take the recalled baby bibs and stroller bags away from children immediately, stop using them, and contact Peony Design to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to discard the recalled baby bibs and stroller bags.
ContactPeony Design by email at peonydesigncoshoppe@gmail.com for more information.
Recall date2026-07-23 (recall no. 26639)

What was recalled

This recall involves Peony Design personalized handmade baby bibs and stroller bags. The bibs have scalloped edges, a snap closure, and personalized embroidery on the front. The bibs were sold in fabric designs of geese, strawberries, heart tulip, plaid checks, and floral print. The stroller bags were made in coordinating fabrics including heart tulip, plaid check, floral print, and Winnie the Pooh. Each stroller bag has two straps with snaps at the ends for attachment and is personalized with embroidery and a ruffle around the top edge.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should take the recalled baby bibs and stroller bags away from children immediately, stop using them, and contact Peony Design to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to discard the recalled baby bibs and stroller bags.. Peony Design by email at peonydesigncoshoppe@gmail.com for more information.
