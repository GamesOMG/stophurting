ASDA Hapello Power Train Recall (August 2026)

Risk of choking.

WhatASDA Hapello Power Train
BrandASDA
The hazardThe product presents a risk of choking because it includes easily-detachable small parts.
Product typeToys - Car/Train Sets (Powered)
Made inUnknown
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-17 (notice 2608-0111)

What was recalled

Multicoloured battery power train toy.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of choking because a small part (the orange steam dome) may easily detach from the top of the train. This small part poses a choking risk to young children, particularly those under 36 months of age who have a natural tendency to explore objects by placing them in their mouths.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled by end users. Owners are advised to stop using the product and keep It out of the reach of young children.

Owners should return he product to their nearest ASDA store for a refund.

For further information, access the ASDA website at: Product Recall: Hapello Power Train

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2608-0111

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
