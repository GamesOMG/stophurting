Recall

SHEIN Distribution Corporation Spiral Toys Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking hazard — about 963 units.

WhatSHEIN Distribution Corporation Spiral Toys
How manyAbout 963 units
The hazardThe toys violate the small ball ban because they contain small balls and are intended for children under three years of age, posing a deadly choking hazard.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled toys immediately, take them away from children and contact SHEIN for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to affirm they disposed of the entire toy, including the small balls.
ContactSHEIN toll-free at 833-853-8668 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at uscsteam@shein.com or online at https://us.shein.com/product-recalls/list or https://us.shein.com and click on “Product Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-09-03 (recall no. 26732)

What was recalled

This recall involves the Spiral Toy (spinning toy). The toy comes with various colored platforms (three, five, or seven, depending on the model) that can be stacked and several yellow plastic balls with a bear head and a basket at the top. One model comes with a basket and a bull head. The packaging lists the manufacturer, manufacturer’s address and email, product name, batch code and various warnings on a sticker label. A separate sticker label has the SKU code “sl25013088678574868” with a bar code and “Made in China” along with various Chinese characters.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled toys immediately, take them away from children and contact SHEIN for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to affirm they disposed of the entire toy, including the small balls.. SHEIN toll-free at 833-853-8668 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at uscsteam@shein.com or online at https://us.shein.com/product-recalls/list or https://us.shein.com and click on “Product Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
