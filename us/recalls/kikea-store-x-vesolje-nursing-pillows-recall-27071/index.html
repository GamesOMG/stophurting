Recall

Kikea Store X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillows Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Suffocation — about 19,596 units.

WhatKikea Store X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillows
How manyAbout 19,596 units
The hazardThe recalled nursing pillows violate the mandatory safety standards for nursing pillows because they can obstruct an infant’s breathing, posing a serious risk of injury or death from suffocation.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from April 2025 through June 2026 for between $24 and $34. · Shenzhen Yiyi Jiajia Trading Co., Ltd., doing business asKikea Store, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Kikea Store for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half after confirming the recalled pillow with the firm and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to zsally929@gmail.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product.
ContactKikea Store by email at zsally929@gmail.com.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27071)

What was recalled

This recall involves X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillows, model number NP001. The nursing pillows are C-shaped and sold in white with leaves pattern, dark brown with multicolored feather pattern and multicolored puzzle piece pattern. There are no markings or labels on the product.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Kikea Store for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half after confirming the recalled pillow with the firm and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to zsally929@gmail.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product.. Kikea Store by email at zsally929@gmail.com.
