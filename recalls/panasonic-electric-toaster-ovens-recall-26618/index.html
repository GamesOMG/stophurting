Recall

Panasonic Electric Toaster Ovens Recall (July 2026)

Shock and Fire hazard — about About 11,480 (In addition, about 2,184 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatPanasonic Electric Toaster Ovens
How manyAbout About 11,480 (In addition, about 2,184 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe power cord insulation can be insufficient due to a protective fiberglass sleeve not covering it adequately, posing a risk of shock and/or fire hazard.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com, Costco.com, Panasonic.com and other online platforms nationwide from October 2024 through April 2026 for about $170.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled Electric Toaster Oven immediately and contact Panasonic for a full refund.
ContactPanasonic toll-free at 888-943-2391 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at Panasonic6895@sedgwick.com and online at shop.panasonic.com/toaster-oven-recall for more information.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26618)

What was recalled

This recall involves all Panasonic Electric Toaster Ovens, Model No. NB-G200. The Electric Toaster Ovens are stainless steel with the temperature knob located on the bottom. The brand name Panasonic is written on the front of the product. The Electric Toaster has six cooking menus with pre-set buttons, with a digital timer (up to 25 minutes) and temperature settings (250-Degree F to 500-Degree F). The model number is located on the name plate label on the back of the Electric Toaster Oven.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled Electric Toaster Oven immediately and contact Panasonic for a full refund.. Panasonic toll-free at 888-943-2391 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at Panasonic6895@sedgwick.com and online at shop.panasonic.com/toaster-oven-recall for more information.
