How manyAbout 9,000 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from November 2024 through July 2026 for about $170. · Fujian Laobosi Cross-Border E-commerce Co., Ltd., dba FJTJBSI, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact FJTJBSI for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the dresser and send a photo of the marked dresser to FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactFJTJBSI by email at FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-09-10 (recall no. 26764)

What was recalled

This recall involves the FJTJBSI-branded wood dressers. The black or white dressers measure about 15.1 inches long, 27.6 inches wide and 42.2 inches high, and weigh 68 pounds. The dressers have six drawers with silver or black handles. “LBS-XD-4-2-BLK-023” or “LBS-XD-4-2-WIT-024” is printed on a label on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact FJTJBSI for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the dresser and send a photo of the marked dresser to FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. FJTJBSI by email at FJTJBSIrecall@outlook.com.
