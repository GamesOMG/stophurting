Recall

Amana Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps Recall (June 2026)

Burn and fire hazard.

WhatAmana Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps
BrandAmana
The hazardburn and fire hazard
CategorySpecialized products
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byDistributor Daikin Comfort Technologies Houston, TX United States Manufacturer Amber Enterprises India Ltd. Rajpura Dist. Patiala, Punjab India
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-06-25

What was recalled

This recall involves certain models of Amana brand Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps. 

The affected products are white-coloured and the brand name, Amana, is printed on most of the units' control covers. The units are used to provide room climate control.  

The model number is located on the front of the unit on the front edge of the base pan on a white sticker.  It will have the model beginning with PB** and the serial number is under the bar code that is under the model number. 

Why it was recalled

The product does not comply with the electrical requirements in the applicable UL Standard. The heating element may be energized any time the unit is plugged in, regardless of the mode of the unit, and a ground fault in the equipment’s electric heater may pose a burn or fire hazard.  

As of March 27, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries in Canada. In the United States, the company has received one report of plastic on the product melting and no reports of injuries.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled Amana Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps and contact Daikin Comfort Technologies for instructions to immediately shut down and unplug the product. Consumers will be instructed to cut the product’s cord and submit a photo of the cut cord, in front of the unit’s serial plate, in exchange for a full refund.

For more information, consumers can contact Daikin Comfort Technologies by telephone toll-free at 855-812-8989 between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. CT. 

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Daikin Comfort Technologies.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada. 

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form. 

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls. 

Background

Number Sold 

The company reported that 53 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 7,030 units were sold in the United States.  

Time Period Sold 

The affected products were sold from April 2025 to December 2025. 

Place of Origin 

Manufactured in India
