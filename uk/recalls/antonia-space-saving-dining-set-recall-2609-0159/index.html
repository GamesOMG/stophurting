Recall

Antonia Space Saving Dining Set Recall (September 2026)

Risk of injuries.

WhatAntonia Space Saving Dining Set
BrandJD Williams
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries because the table legs may detach from the tabletop due to a loose bracket.
Product typeFurniture – Household/Office Tables
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-30 (notice 2609-0159)

What was recalled

Dining set with glass table with metal legs, and four upholstered chairs.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries because the table legs may detach from the tabletop due to a loose bracket. This could cause the table to become unstable or collapse unexpectedly, resulting in the glass tabletop shattering and creating a risk of cuts and lacerations from broken glass, or impact injuries if the table collapses onto a person.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by JD Williams.

Affected customers have been contacted directly by the supplier. If you own this product and have not been contacted, stop using it and contact the supplier for a refund, repair or replacement.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0159

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
