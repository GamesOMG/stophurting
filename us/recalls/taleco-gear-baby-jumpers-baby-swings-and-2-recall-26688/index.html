Recall

Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers, Baby Swings and 2-in-1 Baby Jumper & Swings Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fall and Strangulation hazard — about 41,100 (In addition, 150 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatTaleco Gear Baby Jumpers, Baby Swings and 2-in-1 Baby Jumper & Swings
How manyAbout 41,100 (In addition, 150 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe baby jumper, baby swing and 2-in-1 baby jumper & swing can become unstable, posing fall and impact hazards. Additionally, the hanging restraint straps and seat openings can pose strangulation hazards.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com, Walmart.com and TalecoGear.com from April 2021 through July 2026 for between $65 and $120.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled products immediately and contact Taleco Gear to receive a full refund. Consumers will be provided with instructions on how to destroy the recalled products and provide photos of the destroyed parts to obtain a full refund.
ContactTaleco Gear by email at TalecoGearRecall@163.com or online at https://www.talecogear.com/recall or www.talecogear.com and click on RECALL INFORMATION for more information.
Recall date2026-08-13 (recall no. 26688)

What was recalled

This recall involves G Taleco Gear Baby Jumpers, Baby Swings and Baby Jumper & Swings, sold in black, white, gray, pink, blue and green. The following model numbers are included in the recall: Product Name Model Number Baby Jumper jumper-01 Baby Swing swing-1 2-in-1 Jumper & Swing-A jumper-01 2-in-1 Jumper & Swing-B jumper-01 The model number can be found on the fabric wash and care label sewn into the fabric seat cover.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled products immediately and contact Taleco Gear to receive a full refund. Consumers will be provided with instructions on how to destroy the recalled products and provide photos of the destroyed parts to obtain a full refund.. Taleco Gear by email at TalecoGearRecall@163.com or online at https://www.talecogear.com/recall or www.talecogear.com and click on RECALL INFORMATION for more information.
