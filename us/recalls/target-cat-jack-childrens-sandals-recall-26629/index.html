Recall

Target Cat & Jack Children's Sandals Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking hazard — about 211,000 units.

WhatTarget Cat & Jack Children's Sandals
How manyAbout 211,000 units
The hazardThe sandals' decorative pearls can fall off, posing a risk of serious injury or death from a choking hazard.
Sold atTarget stores nationwide and online at target.com from January 2026 through May 2026 for about $20.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled sandals immediately, take them away from children and contact Target for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to return the sandals to any store or use a prepaid label to return the sandals by mail.
ContactTarget toll-free at 800-591-3869 from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. CT daily, online at www.target.com/help/articles/news-safety/product-recalls or at www.target.com and click on "Recalls" at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26629)

What was recalled

This recall involves Cat & Jack-branded children's sandals. The sandals are tan and have two raffia straps with gold buckles and plastic pearls. The brand is printed on the shoe's sole and bottom. The sandals were sold in sizes 5T through 12T.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled sandals immediately, take them away from children and contact Target for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to return the sandals to any store or use a prepaid label to return the sandals by mail.. Target toll-free at 800-591-3869 from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. CT daily, online at www.target.com/help/articles/news-safety/product-recalls or at www.target.com and click on "Recalls" at the bottom of the page for more information.
