Woodure Toddler Kitchen Step Stool Model WD1357 Recall (September 2026)

Fall and entrapment hazards.

WhatWoodure Toddler Kitchen Step Stool Model WD1357
BrandWoodure
The hazardfall and entrapment hazards
CategoryFurniture and furnishings
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byImporter FIPET INC Sheridan, WY United States of America
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-09

What was recalled

This recall involves Woodure-branded children’s tower kitchen step stool, model WD1357. The tower stool is made of plywood and measures 45.7cm deep by 42.9cm wide by 87.9 cm high. The product label identifies the model as WD1357. 

Why it was recalled

The recalled tower stool model WD 1357 can collapse or tip over while in use, and a child’s torso can fit through the openings on the front and back sides, posing a risk of serious injury and death due to tip over, fall, and entrapment hazards.

As of September 6, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries from these products in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled Model WD1357 step stool and keep it away from children until a repair kit has been properly installed. Consumers can contact Woodure to obtain a free repair kit, which includes protective nets, stabilizing feet and installation instructions. 

For more information, consumers can contact Woodure by email at woodure@wwoodure.com or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 2,000 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from July 2024 to June 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
