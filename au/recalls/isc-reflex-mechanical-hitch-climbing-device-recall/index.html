Recall

ISC REFLEX mechanical hitch climbing device Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from falling.

WhatISC REFLEX mechanical hitch climbing device
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from falling if the mechanical hitch slips while in use. Incidents have occurred.
Sold atLRV8 Rescue – instore and online Stihl Shop - Burleigh
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale23 May 2025 - 8 August 2026
Recalled byLRV8 Rescue
Made inUnited Kingdom
ContactLRV8 Rescue Call: 0404 869 888 Email: sales@lrv8.com.au Visit: https://lrv8.com.au/pages/contact-us
CategoryClimbing and abseiling
Recall date2026-09-01

What was recalled

Mid-line attachable mechanical hitch climbing device.

SKU: MH285A1

Only hitches purchased from LRV8 Rescue are affected by this recall.

Why it was recalled

The mechanical hitch can slip while in use.

What to do

  1. Stop using ISC REFLEX mechanical hitches immediately.
  2. Return affected ISC REFLEX hitches to LRV8 Rescue with proof of purchase (or your email or your mobile number) for a full refund.
  3. Contact LRV8 if you require further information or assistance with this recall.
