Recall

Little Grape Land Nursing Pillows Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Suffocation — about 1,430 units.

WhatLittle Grape Land Nursing Pillows
How manyAbout 1,430 units
The hazardThe recalled nursing pillows advertised for infant feeding and tummy time violate the mandatory safety standards for nursing pillows?and infant support cushions?because they can obstruct an infant's breathing, posing a serious risk of injury or death from suffocation.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from August 2025 through April 2026 for between $28 and $30.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Little Grape Land for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to recall@evermorepartner.com.
ContactLittle Grape Land by email at recall@evermorepartner.com, or online at https://littlegrapeland.com/recall or https://littlegrapeland.com/ and click "Recall" at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-06-04 (recall no. 26525)

What was recalled

This recall involves Little Grape Land Nursing Pillows. The nursing pillows are U-shaped and come in a rose floral, alligator, bear, butterfly, cactus, construction truck, forest deer, green leaves, little bunny, spring flower and woodland animal patterns. There are no markings or labels on the product.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Little Grape Land for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to recall@evermorepartner.com.. Little Grape Land by email at recall@evermorepartner.com, or online at https://littlegrapeland.com/recall or https://littlegrapeland.com/ and click "Recall" at the bottom of the page for more information.
