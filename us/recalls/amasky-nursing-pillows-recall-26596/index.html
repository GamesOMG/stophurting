Recall

AMASKY Nursing Pillows Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Suffocation — about 4008 units.

WhatAMASKY Nursing Pillows
How manyAbout 4008 units
The hazardThe recalled nursing pillows advertised for infant feeding and tummy time violate the mandatory safety standards for nursing pillows?and infant support cushions because they can obstruct an infant's breathing, posing a serious risk of injury or death from suffocation.
Sold atAmazon.com from January 2026 through March 2026 for between $24 and $30.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Pretty-Life for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to amasky@126.com.
ContactPretty-Life toll-free at 888-820-0881 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, or by email at amasky@126.com.
Recall date2026-07-02 (recall no. 26596)

What was recalled

This recall involves AMASKY Nursing Pillows. The nursing pillows are C-shaped and sold in white with a crocodile pattern as well as other various colors and patterns. "Brand: AMASKY," "Model No.: BXP99/BXP93/BXP94/BXP96/BXP97" and "Batch Number: 202506001" are printed on a label attached to the pillow.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled nursing pillows immediately and contact Pretty-Life for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half and send a photo of the destroyed pillow to amasky@126.com.. Pretty-Life toll-free at 888-820-0881 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, or by email at amasky@126.com.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-07-02. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
