Recall

Amana Through the Wall (TTW) air conditioners or heat pumps Recall (September 2026)

Fire hazard.

WhatAmana Through the Wall (TTW) air conditioners or heat pumps
BrandAmana
The hazardfire hazard
CategorySpecialized products
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byDistributor Daikin Comfort Technologies Houston, Texas United States Manufacturer Amber Enterprises India Ltd. Rajpura Dist. Patiala, Punjab India
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-03

What was recalled

This recall involves certain models of Amana brand Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps.

The affected products are white-coloured and the brand name, Amana, is printed on most of the units' control covers. The units are used to provide room climate control.

The model number is located on the front of the unit on the front edge of the base pan on a white sticker. It will have the model beginning with PB** and the serial number is under the bar code that is under the model number.

Why it was recalled

The snubber circuit can emit flames if it shorts, posing a risk of serious injury due to fire hazard.

As of September 1, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries in Canada. In the United States, the company has received 2 reports of a component on the control board shorting. No injuries have been reported.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled Amana Through-the-Wall Air Conditioners or Heat Pumps and contact Daikin Comfort Technologies to submit a request for a full refund of the unit.

For more information, consumers can contact Daikin Comfort Technologies by telephone toll-free at 833-730-0939 between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. CT or by visiting the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Daikin Comfort Technologies.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 126 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 4,633 units were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from April 2025 to June 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in India
