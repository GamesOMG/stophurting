Ambiano Ionic Hairdryers Recall (October 2026)

Risk of electric shock or fire as, over time, the power cord may become damaged.

WhatAmbiano Ionic Hairdryers
BrandAmbiano
The hazardThe product presents a risk of electric shock or fire as, over time, the power cord may become damaged.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment – Hairstyling (Powered)
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-16 (notice 2610-0111)

What was recalled

Ionic hairdryers sold in black, white, yellow and orange colours.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of electric shock or fire as, over time, the power cord may become damaged, allowing access to live parts or causing overheating. If the user were to touch an accessible live part, they could receive an electric shock.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Aldi.

Consumers who have purchased the product should stop using it immediately and return it to their nearest Aldi store for a full refund.

For further information or assistance, please access: Product Recall Ambiano Ionic Hairdryer - Customer Support ALDI.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0111

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
