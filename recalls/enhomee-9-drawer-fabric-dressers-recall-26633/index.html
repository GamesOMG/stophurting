EnHomee 9-Drawer Fabric Dressers Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about About 16,809 units.

WhatEnHomee 9-Drawer Fabric Dressers
How manyAbout About 16,809 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risk of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atOnline at Walmart.com from September 2023 through March 2026 for about $80.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact Raybee-Direct for instructions on how to identify affected units and dispose of the dressers to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to submit a photo to RaybeeRecall@outlook.com demonstrating disposal of the product.
ContactContact Raybee-Direct by email at RaybeeRecall@outlook.com
Recall date2026-07-23 (recall no. 26633)

What was recalled

This recall involves EnHomee 9-Drawer Fabric Dressers. The recalled dressers come in white, brown, gray, and black. The dressers have nine fabric drawers, and the frames are made of metal. They are 15.3 inches wide, 27.5 inches tall, 47.2 inches long, and weigh 41.3 pounds. Only orders placed before March 30th 2026, are part of this recall.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact Raybee-Direct for instructions on how to identify affected units and dispose of the dressers to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to submit a photo to RaybeeRecall@outlook.com demonstrating disposal of the product.. Contact Raybee-Direct by email at RaybeeRecall@outlook.com
