HAMAX Buddy Baby Seat Recall (October 2026)

Risk of choking due to a detachable safety label on the harness strap.

WhatHAMAX Buddy Baby Seat
BrandHAMAX
The hazardThe product presents a risk of choking due to a detachable safety label on the harness strap.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Cycles Transport - Children seats
Made inUnknown
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-26 (notice 2610-0181)

What was recalled

Red and black baby seat attachment for a bicycle.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of choking due to the textile safety label attached to the harness strap (pictured). This label may detach, creating a small part. A young child may place the small part in their mouth and choke.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Decathlon. We recommend that you keep this product out of reach of children until the label is removed. Decathlon has advised consumers to:

  1. Check the shoulder harness strap on the seat

  2. If the textile label shown in the image is present, completely cut it off the harness strap.

  3. Once the label has been removed, the choking hazard is eliminated, and the seat may be used.

  4. If the seat does not have this label, it is not affected.

Alternatively, bring the product to your nearest Decathlon store for label removal, or for a refund. For more information, please access: https://www.decathlon.co.uk/lp/c/product-recall or contact customer.enquiries@decathlon.com.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0181

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
