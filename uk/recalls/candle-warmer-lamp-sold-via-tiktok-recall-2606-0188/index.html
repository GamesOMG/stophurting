Candle Warmer Lamp sold via TikTok Recall (September 2026)

Serious risk of fire as the plug does not meet the dimensional requirements of the releva….

WhatCandle Warmer Lamp sold via TikTok
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of fire as the plug does not meet the dimensional requirements of the relevant standard, BS 1363.
Product typeLighting Equipment - Plug-In Lighting
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-04 (notice 2606-0188)

What was recalled

A gold-coloured lamp packaged in a white box, labelled as “Aromatherapy lamp – The fragrance of faith.”

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of fire because the plug does not meet the dimensional requirements of BS 1363 and cannot make a secure connection with the socket. During use, this could cause the plug to overheat and catch fire.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016 or the Plugs and Sockets etc. (Safety) Regulations 1994.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by the fulfilment centre, TCS Virtual Shopping.

If you have not been contacted directly by TCS Virtual Shopping regarding the recall, you should contact the seller you purchased from for redress.

The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (TikTok).

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2606-0188

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
