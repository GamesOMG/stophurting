Recall

5-Drawer Dressers Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about 1,980 units.

What5-Drawer Dressers
How manyAbout 1,980 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from September 2023 through June 2026 for about $180.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact KAIFAM for instructions on how to identify affected units and receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write "RECALLED" on the top, front, and sides of the dressers with a permanent marker and remove all five drawers. Consumers will be asked to email photos of the marked and disassembled dressers to KAIFAM_recall@outlook.com to receive a full refund.
ContactKAIFAM by email at KAIFAM_recall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-30 (recall no. 26646)

What was recalled

This recall involves KAIFAM 5-Drawer Dressers. The recalled dressers are made of metal and come in white and black. The dressers measure 15.7 inches wide, 31.5 inches long and 53.5 inches tall, and weigh 94.1 pounds. "SKU: N-YSXR0055B" and "Item: Steel Cabinet" are printed on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Contact KAIFAM for instructions on how to identify affected units and receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write "RECALLED" on the top, front, and sides of the dressers with a permanent marker and remove all five drawers. Consumers will be asked to email photos of the marked and disassembled dressers to KAIFAM_recall@outlook.com to receive a full refund.. KAIFAM by email at KAIFAM_recall@outlook.com.
