Weet-Bix Wearable Blanket Recall (August 2026)

Serious burn injuries.

WhatWeet-Bix Wearable Blanket
The hazardThere is a risk of serious burn injuries if the product is exposed to a heat or flame source. Consumers may not be aware of this risk.
Sold atSanitarium Weet-Bix website
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale23 February 2023 - 17 August 2026
Recalled bySanitarium Health Food Company
Made inChina
ContactSanitarium Health Food Company Call 1800 673 392 Email hello@sanitarium.com.au Visit http://www.weetbix.com.au/recall
CategoryClothing (daywear) · Clothing (sleepwear)
Recall date2026-08-20

What was recalled

Wearable hooded blanket in child and adult sizes.

Why it was recalled

The products do not comply with the mandatory standard for children’s nightwear.

The products do not include the required fire hazard warning label.

What to do

  1. Stop using the blanket immediately and place away from heat sources or flames.
  2. Contact Sanitarium to arrange to return the product for a full refund.
  3. For further information or assistance with this recall, please contact Sanitarium.
