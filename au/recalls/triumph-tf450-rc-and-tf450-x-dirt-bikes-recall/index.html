Triumph TF450-RC and TF450 X Dirt Bikes Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death.

WhatTriumph TF450-RC and TF450 X Dirt Bikes
BrandTriumph Motorcycles
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death if the open or broken valves cause a decrease in engine performance and lead to a crash.
Sold atAuthorised Triumph Motorcycles dealers
Where it was soldNationally
On sale8 January 2025 - 22 September 2026
Recalled byTRIUMPH MOTORCYCLES AUSTRALIA
Made inThailand
ContactTriumph Motorcycles Australia Email: info@triumphmotorcycles.com.au Visit: https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com.au/dealers/dealer-search
CategoryDirt bikes and miniature motorbikes
Recall date2026-09-29

What was recalled

Triumph TF450 dirt bikes. Affected models include:

  • 2025 TF450-RC Edition
  • 2026 TF450-C
  • 2026 TF450-E
  • 2026 TF450-X

Please see attached list of affected VINs.
 

Why it was recalled

The exhaust rocker shaft may seize or stick, causing the exhaust valves to remain open or break.

What to do

  1. Stop using the affected dirt bikes immediately.
  2. Contact your authorised Triumph dealer to arrange for a free replacement of the cylinder head.
