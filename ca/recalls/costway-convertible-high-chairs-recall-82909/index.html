Costway Convertible High Chairs Recall (November 2026)

Fall and entrapment hazards.

WhatCostway Convertible High Chairs
BrandCostway
The hazardfall and entrapment hazards
CategoryHousehold items
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byImporter Costway, Inc. Ontario Canada Manufacturer TAIZHOU HAHAYA CHILDREN WARES CO.,LTD ZHEJIANG China
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-06

What was recalled

This recall involves Costway 3-in-1 convertible high chairs designed to convert into an infant table and chair set or to be used as a booster seat. It features a 3-position adjustable feeding tray, an adjustable seat back, and a 5-point harness system. The SKU for the affected products is BB4640. They were available in several color combinations, including purple and white, red and white, blue and white, and brown and white.

Why it was recalled

The recalled high chairs do not contain a passive restraint system, and a child can slip through the opening between the feeding tray and seat and become entrapped or fall, posing a risk of serious injury or death.

As of October 28, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

For more information on Health Canada’s assessment of high chairs in Canada, see Notice of danger to human health or safety and risk mitigation for high chairs, and Information on Health Canada's approach to the General Prohibitions under the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact Costway for a refund.

For more information, consumers can contact Costway by telephone at (+1) 844-242-1885 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday or by email at recall@costway.com.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 55 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from January 2024 to August 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
