ASDA Ladies’ Handbags Recall (September 2026)

Serious chemical risk due to excess phthalates.

WhatASDA Ladies’ Handbags
BrandASDA
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk due to excess phthalates.
Product typeClothing, textiles and fashion items - Personal Bags
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-02 (notice 2609-0009)

What was recalled

Three black ladies’ handbags. Please see below for a list of product identifiers.

Why it was recalled

The products present a serious chemical risk because they contain excessive concentrations of Di(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate (DEHP). Phthalates may harm the health of children, possibly causing damage to the reproductive system. 

The product does not meet the requirements of the REACH Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by ASDA.

We recommend that consumers stop using the product immediately. Return the product to any ASDA store for a full refund. For further information, please visit Product Recall - Ladies Handbags or contact ASDA on 0330 053 0111.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2609-0009

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
