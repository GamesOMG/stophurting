Recall

RIEMANN P20 Hyperpigmentation Defence Face Cream SPF50+ 50g sold by Next Recall (August 2026)

The specific batches of this product sold by Next were found to be past their expiry date.

WhatRIEMANN P20 Hyperpigmentation Defence Face Cream SPF50+ 50g sold by Next
BrandRIEMANN P20
The hazardThe specific batches of this product sold by Next were found to be past their expiry date. This means that the suncream may not provide adequate protection, increasing the risk of sun-related skin damage and sunburn.
Product typeCosmetics, personal care and grooming - Sun Protection Products
Made inDenmark
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-03 (notice 2607-0230)

What was recalled

Specific batches of Riemann P20 face cream sold online by Next. Only products purchased online from Next are affected by this recall.

Why it was recalled

The specific batches of this product sold by Next were found to be past their expiry date. After the expiry date has passed, the UV filters in the suncream can break down, and the suncream may not provide adequate protection, increasing the risk of sun-related skin damage and sunburn.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Next.

Affected customers have been contacted directly regarding this recall. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Next Customer Services Team on 0333 777 8000.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0230

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
