Recall

Liuqibax Adult Bed Rails Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about 12,000 units.

WhatLiuqibax Adult Bed Rails
How manyAbout 12,000 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. In addition, the bed rails do not bear the required hazard warning labels.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from July 2024 through May 2026 for about $60.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled bed rails immediately and contact Luqiba for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the bed rails by dismantling them, cutting off the safety strap and storage bag and writing in permanent marker "RECALLED" on the upper and lower rails, and email a photo of the destroyed rails to Liuqibax@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.
ContactLuqiba by email at Liuqibax@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-08-06 (recall no. 26668)

What was recalled

This recall involves Liuqibax-branded adult portable bed rails, Model BR-11. The black or white bed rails measure about 30.5 inches wide by 40.5 inches tall and weigh 6 pounds. "LIUQIBAX" is printed on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled bed rails immediately and contact Luqiba for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the bed rails by dismantling them, cutting off the safety strap and storage bag and writing in permanent marker "RECALLED" on the upper and lower rails, and email a photo of the destroyed rails to Liuqibax@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.. Luqiba by email at Liuqibax@outlook.com.
