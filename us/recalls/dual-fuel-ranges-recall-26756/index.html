Recall

Dual Fuel Ranges Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire or Explosion hazard — about 3,992 (In addition, about 336 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatDual Fuel Ranges
How manyAbout 3,992 (In addition, about 336 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardDual Fuel Ranges with a missing cap from the gas test port can cause a gas leak,​ posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire or explosion hazards.
Sold atAuthorized appliance dealers nationwide from March 2025 through July 2026 for about $6,000 to $20,000.
RemedyRepair
ContactAGA Rangemaster recall line toll-free at 888-257-7912 from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, online at www.agarangeusa/testcap-recall  or www.lacornueusa/testcap-recall and click on “Recall Information” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-09-10 (recall no. 26756)

What was recalled

This recall involves freestanding AGA Elise and Mercury Series Dual Fuel Ranges, in 36- and 48-inch widths and freestanding La Cornue CornuFé Series Dual Fuel Ranges in 36- and 43-inch widths manufactured between March 19, 2025 and July 24, 2026. All units have gas surface burners, set for Natural or LP gas, with one to three electric ovens depending on size.  The products are sold in stainless steel, white and 13 other colors, which can be found at www.agarangeusa.com and www.lacornueusa.com. All colors, trim options and gas types (Natural or LP) are included.  The serial number is on the rating plate, which is located on the bottom panel of the unit under the storage drawer. 

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Repair. AGA Rangemaster recall line toll-free at 888-257-7912 from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, online at www.agarangeusa/testcap-recall  or www.lacornueusa/testcap-recall and click on “Recall Information” at the bottom of the page for more information.
