Mattressman Comforcel Elite and Supreme Mattresses Recall (October 2026)

WhatMattressman Comforcel Elite and Supreme Mattresses
BrandMattressman
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire as, if the anti-slip part on the bottom surface of the mattress is exposed to an ignition source, it could ignite and continue to flame.
Product typeFurniture – Household Mattresses
Made inMalaysia
Risk levelLow
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-13 (notice 2609-0226)

What was recalled

Memory foam double mattress (Elite) and gel foam double mattress (Supreme).

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk of fire because the anti-slip part on the bottom surface of the mattress failed flammability testing. If exposed to an ignition source, the mattress could ignite and flame, causing a fire.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by Mattressman Ltd. 

Affected customers have been contacted directly by the retailer. If you own this product and have not been contacted, stop using it and contact Mattressman (Submit a request – Mattressman) for a refund or replacement.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0226

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
