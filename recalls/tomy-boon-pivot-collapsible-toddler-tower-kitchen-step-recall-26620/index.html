TOMY Boon PIVOT Collapsible Toddler Tower Kitchen Step Stools Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury and Death from Tip-Over and Fall hazard — about 116,908 (In addition, about 2,033 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatTOMY Boon PIVOT Collapsible Toddler Tower Kitchen Step Stools
How manyAbout 116,908 (In addition, about 2,033 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled toddler towers can tip over while in use, posing a risk of serious injury and death, due to tip over and fall hazards.
Sold atOnline at Target.com, Amazon.com and Babylist.com from January 2023 through June 2026 for around $150.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled tower stools immediately and store them away from children until repaired. Contact TOMY for a stabilizing repair part and installation instructions. The firm will mail the free repair parts directly to consumers.
ContactTOMY toll free at 866-725-4407 from 8 a.m. and 4 p.m. CT, Monday through Friday, by email at cs@tomy.com or online at https://recall.tomy.com/pivot or https://us.tomy.com and click on "Product Recalls" at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26620)

What was recalled

This recall involves Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stools. The metal and plastic tower stools were sold in gray and white and measure about 22 inches wide, 23 inches deep, and 35 inches tall. "boon" is visible along the top rail, and a warning label is visible along the side of the standing platform.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled tower stools immediately and store them away from children until repaired. Contact TOMY for a stabilizing repair part and installation instructions. The firm will mail the free repair parts directly to consumers.. TOMY toll free at 866-725-4407 from 8 a.m. and 4 p.m. CT, Monday through Friday, by email at cs@tomy.com or online at https://recall.tomy.com/pivot or https://us.tomy.com and click on "Product Recalls" at the top of the page for more information.
