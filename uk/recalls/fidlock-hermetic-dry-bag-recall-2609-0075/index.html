Fidlock Hermetic Dry Bag Recall (September 2026)

Serious risk of injuries due to accessible small magnets.

WhatFidlock Hermetic Dry Bag
BrandFidlock GmbH
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of injuries due to accessible small magnets.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Swimming/Surfing/Diving Sports Equipment - Replacement Parts/Accessories
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-10 (notice 2609-0075)

What was recalled

Blue waterproof magnetic self-sealing bag. Only products with the lot number 2409140930201 are being recalled.

Why it was recalled

Products from lot number 2409140930201 have a manufacturing defect affecting the adhesive bond between the closure strips and the bag film. As a result, the closure strips may detach partially or completely during use, causing the product to lose its intended function and potentially releasing the enclosed magnets.

The magnets constitute small parts and present a risk of injuries if swallowed, especially by young children. If one or more magnets are swallowed, they can attract each other within the body, causing serious internal injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Fidlock GmbH.

Consumers should cease use of the product immediately, keep it out of reach of children, and dispose of it safely. Contact Fidlock directly via recall_2609@fidlock.com to register for a refund or replacement.
