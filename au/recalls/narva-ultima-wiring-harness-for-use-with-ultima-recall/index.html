Narva Ultima wiring harness for use with Ultima MK3 215 hybrid driving lights Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death and/or property damage from fire.

WhatNarva Ultima wiring harness for use with Ultima MK3 215 hybrid driving lights
BrandNarva
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death and/or property damage from fire if the harness overheats and catches fire. Incidents have occurred, including a vehicle catching fire.

Recall date2026-09-14

What was recalled

Narva Ultima wiring harness for use with Ultima MK3 215 hybrid driving lights (SKU 71744).

Why it was recalled

The wiring harness can overheat while in use and may melt, cause a fire or a short circuit.

What to do

  1. Stop using affected products immediately. Do not attempt to repair, modify or replace the affected harness.
  2. Register your details using the online form on the NARVA website to replace the affected wiring harness.
  3. Brown & Watson International will contact you with further instructions.
  4. If you require further information or assistance with this recall, please contact Brown & Watson International.
