Recall

Truststone Group XO Poppy Power Trip Magnetic Wireless Power Banks Recall (September 2026)

Fire and Burn hazard — about 32,400 units.

WhatTruststone Group XO Poppy Power Trip Magnetic Wireless Power Banks
How manyAbout 32,400 units
The hazardThe lithium-ion battery in the recalled power banks can overheat and ignite, posing fire and burn hazards to consumers.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled power banks immediately and contact Truststone Group for a full refund in the form of a virtual gift card. The virtual gift card can be used at any establishment that accepts Mastercard.
ContactTruststone Group toll-free at 833-820-0888 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at xopowerbank@realtimeresults.net, online at https://www.recallrtr.com/xopowerbank and click on “Important Recall Information” to learn more.
Recall date2026-09-03 (recall no. 26740)

What was recalled

This recall involves XO Poppy Power Trip Magnetic Wireless Power Banks with model number PYPBK5M. The power banks come in three colors and can be identified by their appearance and packaging: cream colored (PY-PBK5M-CR2); cream with pink bow print (PY-PBK5M-BW8); and black with teddy bear print (PY-PBK5M-TB2). Specific model numbers can be found printed on the bottom of the original packaging, directly above the barcode.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled power banks immediately and contact Truststone Group for a full refund in the form of a virtual gift card. The virtual gift card can be used at any establishment that accepts Mastercard.. Truststone Group toll-free at 833-820-0888 from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at xopowerbank@realtimeresults.net, online at https://www.recallrtr.com/xopowerbank and click on “Important Recall Information” to learn more.
