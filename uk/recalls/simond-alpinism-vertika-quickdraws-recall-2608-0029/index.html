SIMOND Alpinism & Vertika Quickdraws Recall (August 2026)

The wire gate riveting on the carabiners may break or detach.

WhatSIMOND Alpinism & Vertika Quickdraws
BrandSIMOND
The hazardThe wire gate riveting on the carabiners may break or detach. If this occurs while the product is in use at height, the user could fall and receive serious injuries.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Trekking/Mountaineering Sports Equipment
Made inFrance
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-11 (notice 2608-0029)

What was recalled

Multiple models of carabiners for use in climbing. Please see Annex A for further product details.

Why it was recalled

The wire gate riveting on the carabiners may break or detach. If this occurs while the product is in use at height, the user could fall and receive serious injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation 2016/425 on Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Decathlon.

Consumers should stop using their affected quickdraw immediately and return it to any Decathlon store for a full refund. For more information, contact Decathlon Customer Relations on +44 (0)748 306 8343/ customer.enquiries@decathlon.com or visit: Product recall

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2608-0029

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
