Urban Outfitters Sleepover Roll Mat Recall (May 2026)

High risk of fire.

WhatUrban Outfitters Sleepover Roll Mat
BrandUrban Outfitters
The hazardThe product presents a high risk of fire because it was found that the outer cover fabric of the mattress failed the ‘match’ test.
Product typeFurniture - Household Mattresses
Made inIndia
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-05-29 (notice 2605-0083)

What was recalled

Portable mattress available in various colours.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a high risk of fire because it was found that the outer cover fabric of the mattress failed the ‘match’ test. The mattress may ignite and cause a fire when near a source of ignition.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Urban Outfitters and is no longer available for sale.

Consumers are advised to cease use of this product immediately and keep it away from any ignition source. For further information, including the returns process, please access the Urban Outfitters Website at: Mattress Product Recall or contact productrecalleu@urbn.com.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0083

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
