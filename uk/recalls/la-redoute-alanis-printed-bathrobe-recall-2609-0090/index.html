La Redoute Alanis Printed Bathrobe Recall (September 2026)

The product is missing a required fire label, meaning consumers are not properly informed….

WhatLa Redoute Alanis Printed Bathrobe
BrandLa Redoute
The hazardThe product is missing a required fire label, meaning consumers are not properly informed of fire safety risks.
Product typeClothing, textiles and fashion items - Night Dresses/Shirts
Made inUnknown
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-15 (notice 2609-0090)

What was recalled

Blue bathrobe with printed design, towel backing and belted waist.

Why it was recalled

The product is missing a required fire label, meaning consumers are not properly informed of fire safety risks.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Nightwear (Safety) Regulations 1985.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by La Redoute. Affected consumers have been contacted directly.

Consumers should stop using the product immediately. If you believe you own an affected product and have not been contacted, please contact La Redoute via 0330 303 0199.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0090

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
