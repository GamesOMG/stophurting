Recall

Combination Smoke and Carbon Monoxide (CO) Detectors Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Failure to Alert Consumers to Fire — about 20 units.

WhatCombination Smoke and Carbon Monoxide (CO) Detectors
How manyAbout 20 units
The hazardThe recalled detectors can fail to alert consumers of a fire, posing a risk of serious injury or death from smoke inhalation or burns.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from November 2025 through April 2026 for about $40.
RemedyConsumers should contact Treatlife Technology to receive a full refund. Consumers should continue using the recalled detectors until they purchase and install a replacement detector. Once a new detector is installed, consumers should write "recalled" on the recalled product, remove the batteries, dispose of the detector in their household garbage, and dispose of the batteries in accordance with local and state regulations.
ContactTreatlife Technology by email at recall@treatlife.com or online at www.treatlife.tech/pages/recalls or www.treatlife.tech and click on "Recalls" at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-06-25 (recall no. 26582)

What was recalled

This recall involves Treatlife Smoke and Carbon Monoxide (CO) Detectors. The alarms are AA battery operated and have a colored light and test button. The alarms are white and circular in shape. The FCC ID "2ANDL-XR3" and the date of manufacture "2023.DEC.02" are printed on the bottom side of the alarm.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should contact Treatlife Technology to receive a full refund. Consumers should continue using the recalled detectors until they purchase and install a replacement detector. Once a new detector is installed, consumers should write "recalled" on the recalled product, remove the batteries, dispose of the detector in their household garbage, and dispose of the batteries in accordance with local and state regulations.. Treatlife Technology by email at recall@treatlife.com or online at www.treatlife.tech/pages/recalls or www.treatlife.tech and click on "Recalls" at the bottom of the page for more information.
