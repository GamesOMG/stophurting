Eurowrap 25pk Blue Party Balloons Recall (August 2026)

Serious chemical risk.

WhatEurowrap 25pk Blue Party Balloons
BrandEurowrap
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk because it contains excess N-nitrosamines and N-nitrosatable substances.
Product typeToys – Toys/Games Variety Packs
Batch / lot5330002193
SKU / product code12924-BCC
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-14 (notice 2608-0108)

What was recalled

Pack of 25 party balloons.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious chemical risk because it contains excess N-nitrosamines and N-nitrosatable substances. Nitrosamines are carcinogenic, and users can be exposed through ingestion or dermal exposure. A child could be exposed to them when touching or putting the product in the mouth.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Eurowrap. Consumers should stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of children. Return the product to the retailer for a full refund or replacement.

For more information, please access the Eurowrap website at: https://www.eurowrap.com/news/important-safety-warning or contact Eurowrap at: contact@eurowrap.com.

Additional information
