Diall 16A Blue Site Connector and Blue Site Socket (Male and Female) Recall (September 2026)

Risk of electric shock.

WhatDiall 16A Blue Site Connector and Blue Site Socket (Male and Female)
BrandDiall
The hazardThe product presents a risk of electric shock because the live and neutral connection markings are reversed, meaning the product can be wired incorrectly.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment - Connectors (Electrical)
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-15 (notice 2609-0064)

What was recalled

Electrical site connectors (male and female connectors).

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of electric shock because the live and neutral connection markings are reversed. This means the product could be wired incorrectly, presenting a risk of electric shock, overheating and fire.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Kingfisher International Products Limited.

If you have product(s) with the batches above, disconnect the product fully from the electricity supply. Switching it off at the wall is not sufficient, the plug must be removed from the socket. If the product is permanently wired, isolate the circuit at the consumer unit. Once disconnected return the item to your nearest B&Q store for a refund.

For more information, please access: https://www.diy.com/product-information/product-recall.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0064

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
