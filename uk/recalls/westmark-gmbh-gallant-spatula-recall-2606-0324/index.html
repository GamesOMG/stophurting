Recall

Westmark GmbH Gallant Spatula Recall (July 2026)

The affected spatulas present a health risk.

WhatWestmark GmbH Gallant Spatula
BrandWestmark
The hazardThe affected spatulas present a health risk because they contain methylenedianiline at levels above the permitted limit.
Product typeKitchen/cooking accessories - Spatulas/Scoops/Ladles
Made inGermany
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-07 (notice 2606-0324)

What was recalled

A black and red spatula marked with 03/25 date stamp.

Why it was recalled

The affected spatulas present a health risk because they contain methylenedianiline at levels above the permitted limit. This substance may migrate into food during normal use, potentially posing a risk to consumer health.

The product does not meet the requirements of The Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (England) Regulations 2012 or the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by TK Maxx.

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately and return it to their nearest TK Maxx or Homesense store for a refund or replacement. 

For more information, please access: Product Recall - TK Maxx UK or call 01923 473561.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2606-0324

Lead Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
