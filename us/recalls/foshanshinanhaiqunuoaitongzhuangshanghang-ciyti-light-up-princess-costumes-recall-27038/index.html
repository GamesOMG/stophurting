FoShanShiNanHaiQuNuoAiTongZhuangShangHang CIYTI Light-Up Princess Costumes Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Battery Ingestion — about 94 units.

WhatFoShanShiNanHaiQuNuoAiTongZhuangShangHang CIYTI Light-Up Princess Costumes
How manyAbout 94 units
The hazardThe costumes violate the mandatory safety standard for toys because the button cell batteries can be accessed easily by children, posing a deadly ingestion hazard. If button cell or coin batteries are swallowed, the ingested batteries can cause serious injuries, including internal chemical burns, and death.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon from February 2025 through June 2025 for between $21 and $35. · FoShanShiNanHaiQuNuoAiTongZhuangShangHang, of China
RemedyConsumers should take the princess costumes away from children immediately, stop using them and contact FoShanShiNanHaiQuNuoAiTongZhuangShangHang for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” with permanent marker on the dress, crown, wand, and remote, then throw away the toys and submit a photo of the disposed toys to Norakid2026@outlook.com. Note: Button cell batteries are hazardous. Batteries should be disposed of or recycled by following local hazardous waste procedures.
ContactEmail at Norakid2026@outlook.com
Recall date2026-10-22 (recall no. 27038)

What was recalled

This recall involves CIYTI Light-Up Princess Costumes. The costumes include a blue dress with a crown and wand that light up, and a remote control. The crowns, scepters, and remote controls contain button batteries. There are no markings on the product. The package has “CIYTI’ on the front.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should take the princess costumes away from children immediately, stop using them and contact FoShanShiNanHaiQuNuoAiTongZhuangShangHang for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” with permanent marker on the dress, crown, wand, and remote, then throw away the toys and submit a photo of the disposed toys to Norakid2026@outlook.com. Note: Button cell batteries are hazardous. Batteries should be disposed of or recycled by following local hazardous waste procedures.. Email at Norakid2026@outlook.com
