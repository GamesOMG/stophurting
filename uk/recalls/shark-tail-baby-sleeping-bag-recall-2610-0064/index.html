Shark Tail Baby Sleeping Bag Recall (October 2026)

Serious risk of asphyxiation when used in a sleep space for babies.

WhatShark Tail Baby Sleeping Bag
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation when used in a sleep space for babies.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Baby Sleeping Bags
Made inUnknown
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-22 (notice 2610-0064)

What was recalled

White and light blue hooded baby sleeping bag featuring a shark-themed design.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation when used in a sleep space for babies. Without armholes to retain a child in position, a baby may slip into the sleeping bag and the material may cover a baby’s nose and mouth, which could result in asphyxia leading to serious or even fatal harm.  Similarly, due to natural movement of the material, a hood may cover a baby’s nose and mouth, which can lead to suffocation or asphyxiation. 

OPSS has published a Product Safety Alert for Baby Sleeping Bags, which highlights the risk they may pose to babies: Product Safety Alert: Baby Sleeping Bags (PSA9) - GOV.UK

Further information on safe sleeping for babies can be accessed on the NHS website at the following link: https://www.nhs.uk/start-for-life/baby/baby-basics/newborn-and-baby-sleeping-advice-for-parents/safe-sleep-advice-for-babies/

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

OPSS are requiring the recall of the product from end users. If you own this product, stop using it and contact the retailer/supplier for a refund.The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (Wish).

Additional information
