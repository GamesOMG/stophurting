Misco Sports Light Up Racket Set Recall (June 2026)

Risk of injuries and burns.

WhatMisco Sports Light Up Racket Set
BrandMisco
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries and burns because it includes button batteries which are easily accessible.
Product typeToys - Indoor/Outdoor Games
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-19 (notice 2606-0102)

What was recalled

Black racket set with lights.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries and burns because it includes button batteries which are easily accessible. If a child is using the product unattended, they may be able to remove the battery cover and expose the batteries. If placed in the mouth and ingested, the batteries may cause internal burns and injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by TK Maxx.

Consumers should stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of young children. Return the product to any TK Maxx or Homesense store for a full refund. For more information, please access: Product Recall - TK Maxx UK or call 01923 473561.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2606-0102

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
