Recall

Arlopu Slide Playsets Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury and Death from Head and Neck Entrapment hazard — about 10,318 units.

WhatArlopu Slide Playsets
How manyAbout 10,318 units
The hazardThe recalled products contain multiple openings where a child’s torso can fit through the opening, but a child’s head cannot, posing a risk of serious injury and death due to head and neck entrapment hazards. Additionally, the basketball nets on the recalled products with model number KT-CS17 can come unhooked from the rim, forming a loop large enough for a child’s head, posing entanglement and strangulation hazards.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com and Walmart.com from April 2023 through August 2026 for between $90 and $130.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the Arlopu Slide Playsets immediately and store them away from children. Contact Arlopu for a free repair kit, which includes redesigned panels, basketball hoops and installation instructions. Consumers will be asked to provide a photo of the product showing the model number to arlopu_support@163.com. Upon receipt of the photo, the firm will mail the free repair kit directly to consumers. Once the repair has been completed, consumers should write “RECALLED” with permanent marker on the removed components and dispose of the removed components.
ContactArlopu toll-free at 800-657-9218 from 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at arlopu_support@163.com or online at https://arlopu.com/ and click on “Recall Information” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27066)

What was recalled

This recall involves Arlopu Slide Playsets with model numbers KT-CS17 and LY-31. The plastic playsets are designed for indoor or outdoor use and contain multiple activity components, including a slide and a basketball hoop. The playsets are available in tan, red, blue, yellow, gray and green. Model number KT-CS17 is printed on a label located on the underside of the elevated platform of the playset. Model LY-31 is printed on a label located on the upper back panel behind the slide of the playset.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the Arlopu Slide Playsets immediately and store them away from children. Contact Arlopu for a free repair kit, which includes redesigned panels, basketball hoops and installation instructions. Consumers will be asked to provide a photo of the product showing the model number to arlopu_support@163.com. Upon receipt of the photo, the firm will mail the free repair kit directly to consumers. Once the repair has been completed, consumers should write “RECALLED” with permanent marker on the removed components and dispose of the removed components.. Arlopu toll-free at 800-657-9218 from 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at arlopu_support@163.com or online at https://arlopu.com/ and click on “Recall Information” at the top of the page for more information.
