Recall

Nevenka Baby pull string giraffe toy Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation.

WhatNevenka Baby pull string giraffe toy
BrandNevenka
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation if a child places the silicone pull strings in their mouth or the feet detach from the toy’s pull strings and the child places them in their mouth.
Sold atNevenka, Woolworths
Where it was soldInternational · Online
On sale1 November 2025 - 31 July 2026
Recalled byY WAREHOUSE PTY LTD
Made inChina
ContactY Warehouse Email: bretpotestas@ywarehouse.com
CategoryRattles and toy teethers
Recall date2026-08-12

What was recalled

Giraffe motor skills toy with silicone pull strings, textured surfaces and colours.

Why it was recalled

The product does not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. The product’s silicone pull strings that are too long and can reach the back of a child's throat causing choking or asphyxiation. The feet on the ends of the silicone pull strings can detach from the product and cause a child to choke if they place them in their mouth.

What to do

  1. Stop using the toy immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Cut the strings of the toy and take a photograph before disposing of it in household waste.
  3. Contact Y Warehouse directly with your order details and the photograph to arrange for a full refund.
  4. For further information or assistance with this recall, contact Y Warehouse.
