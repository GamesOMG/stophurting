Recall

Nuby Foldable Bathtub for babies and infants Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or death from drowning.

WhatNuby Foldable Bathtub for babies and infants
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from drowning if the product is not used as intended.
Sold atALDI Stores
Where it was soldNationally
On sale4 July 2026 - 6 July 2026
Recalled byALDI
Made inChina
ContactALDI Call: 13 25 34 Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm AEST. Email: customer@service.aldi.com.au Visit: https://www.aldi.com.au
CategoryBaby bathing aids
Recall date2026-07-10

What was recalled

Nuby Foldable baby bathtub

  • Sold at ALDI
  • GTIN: 9312552613876
  • SKU: 737002

Why it was recalled

Permanent warning labels are missing, and consumers may not be aware of important safety information about the use of the product.

What to do

  1. Stop using the bathtub immediately.
  2. Return to an ALDI store for a full refund.
