HAIARA Power Banks Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire and Burn hazard — about 11,000 units.

WhatHAIARA Power Banks
How manyAbout 11,000 units
The hazardThe power bank’s lithium battery can explode and ignite, posing fire and burn hazards to consumers.
Sold atSold At: Amazon.com from May 2025 through September 2026 for between $18 to $22. · Huizhou Yancheng Trading Co., Ltd. d/b/a Mitrytech, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled power banks immediately. Consumers who purchased their power banks on Amazon.com from Mitrytech should contact Mitrytech for instructions on how to receive a full refund. To receive a refund, consumers will be required to submit a photo of their recalled power bank with the word “recalled” written on it in permanent marker and confirm disposal of the power bank in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
ContactEmail at HAIARArecall@outlook.com or online at www.haiara.net/recalls.html or www.haiara.net and click on “Recalls” for more information.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27056)

What was recalled

This recall involves HAIARA power banks, model W5 PRO, sold on Amazon.com by Mitrytech. The power banks are black and have “20W” printed on the front and “Model: W5 PRO” printed on the side. The seller name, Mitrytech, and brand name, HAIARA, are located on the purchase receipt. This warning applies to power banks with no date code (formatted “YYYYMMDD”) printed on the side.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled power banks immediately. Consumers who purchased their power banks on Amazon.com from Mitrytech should contact Mitrytech for instructions on how to receive a full refund. To receive a refund, consumers will be required to submit a photo of their recalled power bank with the word “recalled” written on it in permanent marker and confirm disposal of the power bank in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.. Email at HAIARArecall@outlook.com or online at www.haiara.net/recalls.html or www.haiara.net and click on “Recalls” for more information.
