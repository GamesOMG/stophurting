Recall

RootStim Minoxidil Hair Regrowth Treatment Bottles Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Child Poisoning — about 11,000 units.

WhatRootStim Minoxidil Hair Regrowth Treatment Bottles
How manyAbout 11,000 units
The hazardThe recalled hair regrowth products contain minoxidil, which must be in child-resistant packaging, as required by the Poison Prevention Packaging Act. The bottle is not child-resistant, posing a risk of serious injury or death from poisoning if the contents are ingested by young children.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from August 2025 through February 2026 for between $20 and $40. · RootStim LLC, of New York, New York
RemedyConsumers should secure the recalled products out of sight and reach of children immediately and contact RootStim for instructions on receiving a replacement product. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the bottle, discard the bottle and send a photo of the discarded bottle to finergraceing@gmail.com.
ContactRootStim at 202-743-1714 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at finergraceing@gmail.com or online at https://rootstim.com and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-29 (recall no. 27058)

What was recalled

This recall involves three RootStim Minoxidil Hair Regrowth Treatment bottles: RootStim 5% Minoxidil Foam Hair Regrowth Treatment with Rosemary and Biotin, 6 fl. oz. The Foam comes in a blue and white package, with an orange accent band or orange lettering. The package contains three 2 fl. oz. pump-foam bottles. UPC code 810169130594 is printed on a label on the bottom of the box. RootStim Minoxidil Hair Regrowth Treatment 5% Minoxidil Rosemary & Biotin, 2 fl. oz. The spray comes in a magenta box with a 2 fl. oz spray-pump bottle. UPC code 810169301005 is on a label on the bottom of box. RootStim Minoxidil Hair Regrowth Treatment 5% Minoxidil with Rosemary, 4 fl. oz. The serum comes in a blue and white package with an orange accent band containing two 2 fl. oz. amber glass bottles with black spray-pump caps. UPC code 810169301012 is on a label on the bottom of the box.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should secure the recalled products out of sight and reach of children immediately and contact RootStim for instructions on receiving a replacement product. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the bottle, discard the bottle and send a photo of the discarded bottle to finergraceing@gmail.com.. RootStim at 202-743-1714 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at finergraceing@gmail.com or online at https://rootstim.com and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
