Recall

DUMOS Nine-Drawer Dressers Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about About 8,981 units.

WhatDUMOS Nine-Drawer Dressers
How manyAbout About 8,981 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from December 2025 through June 2026 for about $54.
RemedyConsumers should immediately stop using the recalled dressers if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Consumers should contact DUMOS for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the dressers by cutting the drawers in half and writing in permanent marker "RECALLED", and to send a photo of the destroyed dressers to dumosdresserrecall@dumos.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled products.
ContactDUMOS toll-free at 877-820-7778 from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at dumosdresserrecall@dumos.com or online at https://dumos.ehengjian.com/sys/recall or https://dumos.ehengjian.com/sys/home and click "Recall" at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-08-13 (recall no. 26690)

What was recalled

This recall involves DUMOS Nine-Drawer Fabric Dressers. They were sold as television stand dressers in white, black, gray and brown, have a metal frame that measures about 13.4 inches wide, 47 inches long and 31.3 inches tall, and weigh 31.5 pounds. The dressers have LED lights and built-in power outlets and ports with an attached electrical cable. SKU WMEDX-FC-TVK9CDC-RT, WMEDX-FC-TVK9CDC-GY, WMEDX-FC-TVK9CDC-BK or WMEDX-FC-TVK9CDC-WT is printed on a label attached to the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled dressers if they are not anchored to the wall and place them in an area that children cannot access. Consumers should contact DUMOS for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the dressers by cutting the drawers in half and writing in permanent marker "RECALLED", and to send a photo of the destroyed dressers to dumosdresserrecall@dumos.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled products..
