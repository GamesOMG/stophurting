Sammic Portable Electric Liquidiser Recall (August 2026)

Risk of electric shock.

WhatSammic Portable Electric Liquidiser
BrandSammic
The hazardThe product presents a risk of electric shock because it is possible for the user to access live parts under fault conditions.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment – Juicers (Powered)
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-21 (notice 2608-0199)

What was recalled

Portable electric liquidiser for commercial use. For a full list of identifiers, please see Annex A.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of electric shock because it is designed as a double‑insulated electrical appliance, meaning that a second protective insulation must prevent user exposure in case the basic insulation fails.

The manufacturer has identified that, in certain identification numbers, an external aluminium part accessible to the user may come into contact with the aluminium motor support if the first insulation layer fails. If an internal insulation fault occurred between the motor winding and the motor support, the fault could be transmitted to the accessible aluminium part, potentially exposing the user to electric shock.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled by the manufacturer. For more information, please access: https://sammic-com.web.app/en/recall-xm or contact recallexport@sammic.com.
