Recall

Lithonia Lighting RSX Luminaires with Adjustable Tilt Arm Mountings Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury from Impact hazard — about 11,532 units.

WhatLithonia Lighting RSX Luminaires with Adjustable Tilt Arm Mountings
How manyAbout 11,532 units
The hazardThe knuckle of the recalled adjustable tilt arm mountings can fail, allowing the luminaire to fall from the mounting arm, posing a risk of serious injury due to impact hazard.
Sold atSold At: Commercial distributors nationwide including Turtle & Hughes Inc. and Consolidated Electrical Distributors for commercial outdoor lighting applications from August 2019 to August 2026 for between $400 and $2200.
RemedyConsumers should keep clear of the area directly beneath the Luminaire and contact Lithonia Lighting to schedule a free repair. Lithonia Lighting will arrange for a qualified professional to replace the recalled adjustable tilt arm mount with a new complete adjustable arm assembly.
ContactLithonia Lighting toll-free at 855-307-2454 between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, online at https://lithonia.acuitybrands.com/ and click on “Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-22 (recall no. 27041)

What was recalled

This recall involves Luminaires with certain adjustable tilt mounting arms manufactured between August 2019 to July 2020 and August 2024 to April 2026: AASP, AARP, AAWB, and AAWSC. The adjustable tilt arm mounts the RSX luminaire to a square pole, round pole, wall bracket or wall bracket with surface mount conduit. The arm mount allows the luminaire to be tilted from 0 degrees to 90 degrees above horizontal. Consumers should contact Lithonia Lighting to confirm their Luminaire with adjustable tilt mounting arms are included in the recall.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should keep clear of the area directly beneath the Luminaire and contact Lithonia Lighting to schedule a free repair. Lithonia Lighting will arrange for a qualified professional to replace the recalled adjustable tilt arm mount with a new complete adjustable arm assembly.. Lithonia Lighting toll-free at 855-307-2454 between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, online at https://lithonia.acuitybrands.com/ and click on “Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
