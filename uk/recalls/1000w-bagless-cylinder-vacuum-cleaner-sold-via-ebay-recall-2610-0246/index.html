Recall

1000W Bagless Cylinder Vacuum Cleaner sold via eBay Recall (November 2026)

Risk of fire.

What1000W Bagless Cylinder Vacuum Cleaner sold via eBay
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire because the cross-sectional area of the supply cord was 0.454 mm², which is smaller than the required minimum of 0.75 mm².
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment - Household Vacuum Cleaners
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-06 (notice 2610-0246)

What was recalled

A red and grey bagless cylinder vacuum cleaner supplied with a flexible hose and accessories.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire because the cross-sectional area of the supply cord was 0.454 mm², which is smaller than the required minimum of 0.75 mm². Undersized supply cords may overheat during normal use, which could lead to the product catching fire. Improvements are also required to both the product markings and warnings.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by the eBay seller – super.energy1. Affected customers have been contacted directly by the seller. If you own this product, stop using it immediately and contact the eBay seller for a refund.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0246

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
