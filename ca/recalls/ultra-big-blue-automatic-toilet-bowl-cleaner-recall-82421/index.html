Ultra Big Blue Automatic Toilet Bowl Cleaner Recall (August 2026)

Improper labelling and lack of child-resistant packaging.

WhatUltra Big Blue Automatic Toilet Bowl Cleaner
BrandUltra Big Blue
The hazardimproper labelling and lack of child-resistant packaging
CategoryChemicals
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled by​​ Manufacturer ​ Fresh Products LLC Perrysburg, Ohio ​​United States of America​ Importer Advantage Maintenance Products Ltd Paris, Ontario Canada
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-08-04

What was recalled

This recall involves Ultra Big Blue Automatic Toilet Bowl Cleaner (255 g / 9 oz). The product consists of a solid chemical product in a plastic container with a perforated lid. For use, the product, with the lid on, is placed upright in the tank of the toilet and left in the tank until empty.

Why it was recalled

The recalled product does not meet the labelling and child-resistant packaging requirements of the Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations, 2001 under the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act.

The lack of appropriate labelling information and child-resistant packaging could result in unintentional exposure to the products and lead to serious illness or injury including death.

As of July 28, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recall product and dispose of it according to municipal hazardous waste guidelines. 

For more information, consumers can contact Advantage Maintenance Products Ltd. by telephone at 1-800-265-9957 extension 226, 8:00AM - 4:30 PM ET, Monday to Friday or by email at info@advantagemaint.com.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 72,273 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from July 2020 to July 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in United States of America
