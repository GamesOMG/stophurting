Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speaker Recall (August 2026)

Risk of fire as the lithium-ion battery may overheat during use.

WhatCambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speaker
BrandCambridge Audio
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire as the lithium-ion battery may overheat during use.
Product typeCommunications and media equipment - Portable Speakers
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-27 (notice 2608-0060)

What was recalled

A portable rechargeable Bluetooth loudspeaker powered by an internal lithium-ion battery pack, in dark grey, light grey and blue colours.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire as the lithium-ion battery may overheat during use and enter thermal runaway. This may result in smoke, fire and ejection of battery material.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Cambridge Audio. Affected consumers will be contacted directly with instructions. We recommend that owners of affected products stop using them immediately.

For further information, access the Cambridge Audio webpage at: YOYO (M) Battery Safety Recall Information - Cambridge Audio International

The Cambridge Audio Support Hub can be accessed at: Support Hub.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2608-0060

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
