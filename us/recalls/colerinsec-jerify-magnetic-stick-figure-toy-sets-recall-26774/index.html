Recall

Colerinsec Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets Recall (September 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Magnet Ingestion — about 38,507 units.

WhatColerinsec Jerify Magnetic Stick Figure Toy Sets
How manyAbout 38,507 units
The hazardThe magnetic humanoid toy Sets violate the mandatory standard for toys because they can liberate loose high-powered magnets posing an ingestion hazard to children. When high-powered magnets are swallowed, the ingested magnets can attract each other or other metal objects and become lodged in the digestive system. This can result in perforations, twisting, and/or blockage of the intestines, blood poisoning and death.
Sold atSold At: Amazon.com from October 2024 through June 2026 for about $27 · Shenzhen Weihua Chenyu Technology Co., Ltd, dba Junkins, of China
RemedyConsumers should take the magnetic humanoid toys away from children immediately, stop using them and contact Colerinsec for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the magnetic toys and submit a photo of the disposed toys to coushdwugintwer@163.com.
ContactColerinsec by email at coushdwugintwer@163.com.
Recall date2026-09-17 (recall no. 26774)

What was recalled

This recall involves Jerify Magnetic Humanoid Toys 30 PCS. The 30-piece magnetic toys come in three units of brown, white, green, peach, yellow, pink, blue, orange, purple, and red figures in the shape of a human. Each figure measures about 2.7 inches tall by 2.5 inches wide. The magnetic toys contain magnets in the hand and foot locations. There is no labeling on the product. The magnetic toys come in an unlabeled clear bag enclosed in a brown box with the brand name “Jerify” and number “X003ADA3TD” located on the sticker.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should take the magnetic humanoid toys away from children immediately, stop using them and contact Colerinsec for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the magnetic toys and submit a photo of the disposed toys to coushdwugintwer@163.com.. Colerinsec by email at coushdwugintwer@163.com.
