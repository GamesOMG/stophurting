Recall

GOPO Toys Pull String Teething Toys Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking — about About 70,410 units.

WhatGOPO Toys Pull String Teething Toys
How manyAbout About 70,410 units
The hazardThe recalled teething toys violate the mandatory standard for toys because the silicone strings are smaller and longer than permitted. The strings can reach the back of children's throat and become lodged, posing a serious risk of respiratory distress and deadly choking hazard.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from August 2023 through March 2026 for between $11 to $15.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the teething toy immediately, take it away from children and contact GOPO Toys to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the toy by cutting all silicone strings and writing in permanent marker "DESTROYED" on the main body of the toy and send a photo of the destroyed toy to recalls@gopotoys.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.
ContactGOPO Toys at 800-445-2344, email at recalls@gopotoys.com, or online at https://recall.gopotoys.com for more information.
Recall date2026-06-18 (recall no. 26562)

What was recalled

This recall involves GOPO TOYS pull string teething toys. The teething toys consist of an off-white disc shape with a gray ball in the center and six multi-colored, silicone tentacle-looking pull strings that run through the ball. The disc has seven soft push buttons. The brand name and "Pull String Toy" is printed on the front of the product packaging, and the batch number 250905, 250530, 250120, 240315, 231005 or 230610 on the back of packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the teething toy immediately, take it away from children and contact GOPO Toys to receive a full refund. Consumers will be asked to destroy the toy by cutting all silicone strings and writing in permanent marker "DESTROYED" on the main body of the toy and send a photo of the destroyed toy to recalls@gopotoys.com. Consumers should then dispose of the destroyed product.. GOPO Toys at 800-445-2344, email at recalls@gopotoys.com, or online at https://recall.gopotoys.com for more information.
