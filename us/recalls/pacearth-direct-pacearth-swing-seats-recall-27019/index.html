Recall

Pacearth Direct PACEARTH Swing Seats Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury from Fall hazard — about 110,550 units.

WhatPacearth Direct PACEARTH Swing Seats
How manyAbout 110,550 units
The hazardThe seats, metal carabiners or quick links, or webbing straps of the recalled swing seats can break during use, posing a risk of serious injury due to fall hazard.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from May 2017 through June 2026 for between $27 and $57. · Pacearth Direct, of City of Industry, California
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled swing seats immediately and contact Pacearth Direct for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the swing seat with permanent marker, take a photo of it, including the PACEARTH logo and email to recall@pacearth.com.
ContactPacearth Direct by email at recall@pacearth.com
Recall date2026-10-08 (recall no. 27019)

What was recalled

This recall involves PACEARTH U-type swing seats. The swing seats were sold in various colors including green, blue, pink, red and turquoise. The chain is coated in yellow plastic. Metal carabiners or quick links and yellow webbing straps were included with the swing seats to attach the swing to outdoor swing sets or tree branches. The swing seats were sold as a single unit or a pack of two.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled swing seats immediately and contact Pacearth Direct for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write “RECALLED” on the swing seat with permanent marker, take a photo of it, including the PACEARTH logo and email to recall@pacearth.com.. Pacearth Direct by email at recall@pacearth.com
