Recall

MNIENT Adult Portable Bed Rails Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about 330 units.

WhatMNIENT Adult Portable Bed Rails
How manyAbout 330 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. The bed rails do not meet structural stability or retention strap requirements, posing a fall hazard. Additionally, the bed rails' push pins and push pin holes are incorrectly sized, posing a laceration hazard.
Sold atAmazon.com from January 2026 through March 2026 for about $66.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact Zhuzhoushizuisuanshangmaoyouxiangongsi for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the foam padding on the bed rail handle grips, take a photo of the destroyed bed rail and email the photo to mnientrecall@outlook.com.
ContactZhuzhoushizuisuanshangmaoyouxiangongsi by email at mnientrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26630)

What was recalled

This recall involves MNIENT-branded adult portable bed rails, model LQX-110055. The recalled bed rails have a white frame with black foam rubber handle grips, a black fabric pouch, and measure about 13 inches wide by 39 inches high. The MNIENT brand name and model number are on the front of the product packaging and on the product label located on the black fabric pouch.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact Zhuzhoushizuisuanshangmaoyouxiangongsi for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the foam padding on the bed rail handle grips, take a photo of the destroyed bed rail and email the photo to mnientrecall@outlook.com.. Zhuzhoushizuisuanshangmaoyouxiangongsi by email at mnientrecall@outlook.com.
