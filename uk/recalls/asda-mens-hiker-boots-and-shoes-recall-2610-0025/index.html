ASDA Men’s Hiker Boots and Shoes Recall (October 2026)

Serious chemical risk.

WhatASDA Men’s Hiker Boots and Shoes
BrandASDA
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk because testing identified hte presence of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), including perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) above maximum permitted levels.
Product typeClothing, textiles and fashion items – Athletic Footwear - Specialist
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-06 (notice 2610-0025)

What was recalled

Men’s hiking boots in black, grey and khaki.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious chemical risk because testing identified the presence of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), including perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA), above the permitted regulatory limit. Prolonged exposure to these substances may pose a risk to human health and may cause damage to the reproductive system.

The product does not meet the requirements of the REACH Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by ASDA.

If you own the product, stop using it immediately and return it to the nearest ASDA store for a full refund. You do not need a receipt.

For more information, please see Product Recall: Mens Hiker Boots and Shoes or contact ASDA on 0330 053 0111.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2610-0025

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
