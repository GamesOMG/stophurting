LeMieux Ltd Junior Vector Balance Stirrup Recall (November 2026)

Risk of injuries as the stirrup may catch a rider’s boot during a fall.

WhatLeMieux Ltd Junior Vector Balance Stirrup
BrandLeMieux Ltd
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries as the stirrup may catch a rider’s boot during a fall.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Equestrian Stable Equipment/Accessories
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-20 (notice 2611-0124)

What was recalled

LeMieux branded riding stirrup with a spring-loaded arm. The Vector Balance & Control Stirrups are not affected.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries as the stirrup may catch a rider’s boot during a fall. The raised edge beside the release arm, combined with the size of the foot opening, can stop the foot from coming free, even when the release arm has opened. A rider could then be dragged and injured.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by LeMieux Ltd.

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately. Please visit Product Recall for further information on returning the product and obtaining a refund.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2611-0124

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
