Adora Pecker Finger vibrator Recall (September 2026)

Serious burn injuries, death or property damage.

WhatAdora Pecker Finger vibrator
Brandno brand name
The hazardRisk of serious burn injuries, death or property damage if the cable overheats or catches fire while connected to any USB power source. Incidents of overheating have occurred.
Sold atThe Hot Spot website, eBay
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale22 March 2023 - 27 July 2026
Recalled byAdora Erotica trading as The Hot Spot
Made inChina
ContactThe Hot Spot Call: 1300 921 376 between 9am and 3pm, AEST, Monday to Friday Email: admin@thehotspot.com.au Visit: https://www.thehotspot.com.au/safety-recall
CategoryPersonal care products
Recall date2026-09-07

What was recalled

Adult product. Pink USB rechargeable silicone vibrator to fit one finger.

Why it was recalled

The product’s USB charging cable may short circuit and overheat and/or catch fire while connected to any USB power source.

What to do

  1. Stop using the product immediately and unplug the cable from any power source. Do not plug it into any other power source.
  2. Cut the USB cable using scissors and dispose of both cable and product. Photograph the cut cable and disposed product, then email the photograph and your details to admin@thehotspot.com.au to receive a full refund.
  3. Alternatively, return both cable and product to The Hot Spot for a full refund.
  4. If you have already disposed of the cable or the vibrator, or need further information and assistance with this recall, please contact The Hot Spot.
