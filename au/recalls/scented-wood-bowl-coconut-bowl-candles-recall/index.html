Recall

Scented wood bowl & coconut bowl candles Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury or death from burns and/or property damage.

WhatScented wood bowl & coconut bowl candles
BrandVerve & Vibe
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from burns and/or property damage if the candle catches fire.
Sold atVerve & Vibe website, eBay store and Amazon store
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale1 September 2025 - 25 February 2026
Recalled byVERVE & VIBE AUSTRALIA
Made inIndia
ContactVerve & Vibe Call: 0400 567 954 Email: info@vervevibe.com.au Visit: https://www.vervevibe.com.au/
CategoryDecorations, candles and novelties
Recall date2026-07-24

What was recalled

Scented candles in various wood and coconut bowls.

Why it was recalled

The products do not comply with the permanent ban on combustible candle holders. The candles have wood and coconut containers and contain dried flowers that present a fire risk.

What to do

  1. Stop using the candle immediately and place it away from any ignition source.
  2. Contact Verve & Vibe for a free replacement product or a full refund.
