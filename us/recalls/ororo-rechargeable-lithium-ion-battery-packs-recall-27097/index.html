Recall

Ororo Rechargeable Lithium-Ion Battery Packs Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury from Fire and Burn hazard — about 269 (In addition, about 24 were sold in Canada) units.

WhatOroro Rechargeable Lithium-Ion Battery Packs
How manyAbout 269 (In addition, about 24 were sold in Canada) units
The hazardThe recalled lithium-ion battery packs can overheat, posing a risk of serious injury from fire and burn hazards.
Sold atSold Online At: Ororowear.com and Amazon.com from September 2026 through October 2026 for about $109.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the battery packs immediately and contact ororo for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to go to Ororowear.com/pd10k-pro-recall to register and to receive disposal instructions. Once registered, consumers should dispose of the recalled battery packs in accordance with local and state regulations. Note: Do not throw this recalled lithium-ion battery or device in the trash, the general recycling stream (e.g., street-level or curbside recycling bins), or used battery recycling boxes found at various retail and home improvement stores. Recalled lithium-ion batteries must be disposed of differently than other batteries, because they present a greater risk of fire. Your municipal household hazardous waste (HHW) collection center may accept this recalled lithium-ion battery or device for disposal. Before taking your battery or device to a HHW collection center, contact that office ahead of time and ask whether it accepts recalled lithium-ion batteries. If it does not, contact your municipality for further guidance.
ContactOroro at 702-751-7639 between 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday, email at recall@ororowear.com, or online at Ororowear.com/pd10k-pro-recall or Ororowear.com and click on “Recall” for more information.
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27097)

What was recalled

This recall involves ororo’s rechargeable lithium-ion battery packs, model PD10K-Pro. The black battery packs have a built-in USB-C cable and a digital display. “ororo” is written on the front of the battery pack. The model and a seven alphanumeric serial number starting with 67U, 685, 686 or 68A is printed on the back.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the battery packs immediately and contact ororo for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to go to Ororowear.com/pd10k-pro-recall to register and to receive disposal instructions. Once registered, consumers should dispose of the recalled battery packs in accordance with local and state regulations. Note: Do not throw this recalled lithium-ion battery or device in the trash, the general recycling stream (e.g., street-level or curbside recycling bins), or used battery recycling boxes found at various retail and home improvement stores. Recalled lithium-ion batteries must be disposed of differently than other batteries, because they present a greater risk of fire. Your municipal household hazardous waste (HHW) collection center may accept this recalled lithium-ion battery or device for disposal. Before taking your battery or device to a HHW collection center, contact that office ahead of time and ask whether it accepts recalled lithium-ion batteries. If it does not, contact your municipality for further guidance..
