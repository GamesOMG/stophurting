Heeve® 3-Tonne 3.2m x 390mm Aluminium Loading Ramps Recall (September 2026)

Death or serious injury and/or property damage.

WhatHeeve® 3-Tonne 3.2m x 390mm Aluminium Loading Ramps
BrandHeeve
The hazardRisk of death or serious injury and/or property damage if the ramp collapses and causes heavy equipment to topple or fall. Incidents of the ramp failing have occurred.
Sold atRamp Champ

CategoryHeavy vehicle parts and accessories
Recall date2026-09-25

What was recalled

Pairs of aluminium loading ramps for loading and unloading heavy wheeled or tracked equipment onto and off trucks or trailers. The affected ramps are 3.2 m long and 390 mm wide and are rated to 3,000 kg.

All production numbers prior to RCPO242 (particularly RCPO222 and RCPO231) are affected.

Why it was recalled

The ramps are missing a structural reinforcement at the top of the ramp, which may cause the ramp to fail while in use.

What to do

  1. Stop using the affected ramps immediately. Affected units do not have a 10 mm flat-bar reinforcement welded along the top connection plate at the end of the ramp that attaches to the trailer. Affected ramps have a sticker with the following details:
    • Year of Manufacture: 2025
    • Product SKU: H0019
    • Load Capacity: 1500 KG
       
  2. If you are unsure whether your ramp is affected, contact Ramp Champ Customer Service with your order number or proof of purchase for confirmation.
     
  3. If your ramp is affected, contact Ramp Champ Customer Service for more information and to arrange for a refund.
