WhatLittle Daisy Dream Fruit Feeder
BrandLittle Daisy Dream
The hazardThe affected soothers present a risk of choking as when used, parts can detach and make food accessible to the baby unexpectedly.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Baby Feeding Accessories
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-07 (notice 2607-0008)

What was recalled

Baby feeder in the shape of a soother. Affected products were purchased after May 2024.

Why it was recalled

The affected soothers present a risk of choking as when used, parts can detach and make food accessible to the baby unexpectedly.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Little Daisy Dream.  

For more information, please access the Little Daisy Dream website at: https://littledaisydream.com/blogs/product-recall/product-recall

Little Daisy Dream have contacted their affected consumers about the recall directly and have removed the product listing from their website.

We recommend owners stop using this product immediately. Contact the distributor you purchased from to request redress.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2607-0008

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
