Pull String Interactive Toys Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation.

WhatPull String Interactive Toys
BrandPlayground
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation if a child places the silicone pull strings in their mouth or if small parts detach from the toy and the child places them in their mouth.
Sold atPlease see attached list of retailers.

CategoryLearning and activity toys · Toys for babies and toddlers
Recall date2026-08-18

What was recalled

Pull string silicone sensory toy for infants in two styles:

  • Dusty (SKU: 4236397)
  • Jimmy (SKU: 4236484)

Why it was recalled

The product may not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. The product’s silicone pull strings that are too long and can reach the back of a child's throat causing choking or asphyxiation. Small parts may detach from the product and cause a child to choke if they place them in their mouth.

What to do

  1. Stop using the toy immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Cut the strings of the toy and take a photograph of the toy before disposing of it in household waste.
  3. Email the photograph and your order details to info@livingtextiles.com.au to receive a full refund within 30 days.
  4. For further information or assistance with this recall, please email Living Textiles Australia.
