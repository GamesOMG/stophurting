Recall

Kidsview Infant Bouncers Recall (November 2026)

Collapse and Impact Injury to Infants — about 412 units.

WhatKidsview Infant Bouncers
How manyAbout 412 units
The hazardThe bouncers can collapse while in use, posing a risk of impact injury to infants in the bouncer.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from August 2022 through November 2023 for between $60 and $170. · Zhongshan Haimao Cross-border Information Technology Co., Ltd., dba Kidsview, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled infant bouncers immediately and contact Kidsview for an Amazon gift card. Consumers will be asked to check if their infant bouncer is recalled by viewing the date code located on a label on the back of the bouncer’s seat. Consumers with recalled bouncers will be instructed to write “RECALLED” on the product’s fabric seat with permanent marker, take a photo of it, including the label with the date code in the photo and email to haimaoziying@163.com.
ContactKidsview by email at haimaoziying@163.com.
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27092)

What was recalled

This recall involves Kidsview infant bouncers manufactured in August, September, or October 2022. The recalled bouncers have date codes beginning with 2022/08, 2022/09 or 2022/10 located on a label sewn on the back of the fabric seat. The bouncers were sold in khaki and include an accessory bar with animal toys hanging from the bar.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled infant bouncers immediately and contact Kidsview for an Amazon gift card. Consumers will be asked to check if their infant bouncer is recalled by viewing the date code located on a label on the back of the bouncer’s seat. Consumers with recalled bouncers will be instructed to write “RECALLED” on the product’s fabric seat with permanent marker, take a photo of it, including the label with the date code in the photo and email to haimaoziying@163.com.. Kidsview by email at haimaoziying@163.com.
