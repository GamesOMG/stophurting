Recall

Liizousuda Paint Thinner Bottles Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Child Poisoning — about About 4,200 units.

WhatLiizousuda Paint Thinner Bottles
How manyAbout About 4,200 units
The hazardThe paint thinner contains low-viscosity petroleum distillates, which must be in child-resistant packaging, as required by the?Poison Prevention Packaging Act. The bottles are not child-resistant, posing a risk of serious injury or death from poisoning if the contents are swallowed by young children. Additionally, petroleum distillates can get into the lungs, causing chemical pneumonia and/or pulmonary damage, which can be fatal.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from March 2025 through February 2026 for about $10.
RemedyConsumers should secure the recalled paint thinner out of the sight and reach of children immediately and contact Shenzhen Gudebo Technology for information on how to obtain a replacement bottle with a child-resistant closure, into which consumers should transfer the recalled bottle's contents.
ContactShenzhen Gudebo Technology by email at gudebo888@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26619)

What was recalled

This recall involves Liizousuda paint thinner bottles. The paint thinner comes in a clear bottle with a black cap and a wraparound label. The label states "Odorless Mineral Spirit" and "Artist Oil Thinning Medium" in large text.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should secure the recalled paint thinner out of the sight and reach of children immediately and contact Shenzhen Gudebo Technology for information on how to obtain a replacement bottle with a child-resistant closure, into which consumers should transfer the recalled bottle's contents.. Shenzhen Gudebo Technology by email at gudebo888@outlook.com.
