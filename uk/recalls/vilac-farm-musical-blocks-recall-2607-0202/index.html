Vilac Farm Musical Blocks Recall (July 2026)

Serious risk of choking.

WhatVilac Farm Musical Blocks
BrandVilac
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of choking as it includes small parts that can detach or break off during reasonably foreseeable use, creating small parts that fit entirely within a small parts cylinder.
Product typeToys – Musical Toys (Powered)
Made inFrance
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-29 (notice 2607-0202)

What was recalled

Coloured musical blocks with farm-theme artwork.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of choking as it includes small parts that can detach or break off during reasonably foreseeable use, creating small parts that fit entirely within a small parts cylinder. These small parts pose a choking risk to young children, particularly those under 36 months of age who have a natural tendency to explore objects by placing them in their mouths.  

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by the retailer, Bebeco. Affected Bebeco customers have been contacted directly.  

We recommend that owners stop using the product immediately and keep it out of children’s reach. Contact the distributor you purchased from to request redress.

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0202

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
