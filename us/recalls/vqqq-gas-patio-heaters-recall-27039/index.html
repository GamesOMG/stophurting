Recall

VQQQ Gas Patio Heaters Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire and Burn hazard — about 100 units.

WhatVQQQ Gas Patio Heaters
How manyAbout 100 units
The hazardComponents on the recalled patio heaters can malfunction causing gas to be released, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire and burn hazards.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from July 2024 through August 2026 for about $346. · Shanxi Yuhe Sheng Maoyi Youxian Gongsi, dba VQQQ, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled patio heaters immediately, shut the valve at the portable gas source, disconnect the gas hose and contact VQQQ for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker "Recalled" on the heater’s base and send a photo of the marked heater to vqqqrecall@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactVQQQ by email at vqqqrecall@163.com
Recall date2026-10-22 (recall no. 27039)

What was recalled

This recall involves VQQQ-branded natural gas patio heaters, models VQQQ-A1 and VQQQ-001. They have a bronze pole with a round base and a silver mushroom-shaped top with an adjustable height of 55 inches to 78 inches. A 24-foot-long gray gas hose is attached to the heater’s base. The brand and model numbers are printed on the product’s purchase receipt.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled patio heaters immediately, shut the valve at the portable gas source, disconnect the gas hose and contact VQQQ for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker "Recalled" on the heater’s base and send a photo of the marked heater to vqqqrecall@163.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. VQQQ by email at vqqqrecall@163.com
