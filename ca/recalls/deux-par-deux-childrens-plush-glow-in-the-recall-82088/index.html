Deux par Deux Children’s Plush Glow in the Dark Bathrobe in Dinosaur and Unicorn Print Recall (May 2026)

Flammability hazard.

WhatDeux par Deux Children’s Plush Glow in the Dark Bathrobe in Dinosaur and Unicorn Print
BrandDeux par Deux
The hazardflammability hazard
CategoryClothing
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byImporter Deux par Deux Montreal, QC Canada
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-05-19

What was recalled

This recall involves the Deux par Deux plush glow in the dark bathrobe in dinosaur print and in unicorn print. The dinosaur print robe is in blue and white colour and has dinosaur spikes sewn to the hood along with a self-tie wrap belt. The SKU is H20R92_000. The unicorn print robe is in multiple colours of pink, blue, white, and yellow. The robe has ears and a unicorn horn sewn to the hood with a self-tie wrap belt and heart shaped pockets. The SKU is H20R90_000.

Why it was recalled

The recalled robes violate the flammability requirements for children’s sleepwear, posing a risk of burn injuries to children.

Loose-fitting children’s sleepwear can contact ignition sources such as stove elements, candles, and matches more readily than tight-fitting sleepwear, and once ignited will burn rapidly, potentially resulting in severe burns to large areas of the child’s body.

As of May 7, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injuries in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled bath robes and return them for a full refund or store credit.

For more information, consumers can contact Deux par Deux by telephone at 1-438-230-6147, Monday to Friday from 9:30am to 3:00pm EST or by email at ecommerce@deuxpardeux.com .

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form .

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website . You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 287 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from July 2025 to May 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
