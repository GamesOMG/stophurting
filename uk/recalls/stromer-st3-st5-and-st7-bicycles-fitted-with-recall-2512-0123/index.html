Stromer ST3, ST5 and ST7 Bicycles fitted with WREN Front Suspension Forks Recall (December 2025)

Risk of injuries as the suspension forks can fail prematurely and crack, break or separat….

WhatStromer ST3, ST5 and ST7 Bicycles fitted with WREN Front Suspension Forks
BrandStromer
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries as the suspension forks can fail prematurely and crack, break or separate during use.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment – Cycles (Powered)
Made inSwitzerland
Risk levelMedium
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2025-12-12 (notice 2512-0123)

What was recalled

Bikes with automatic pedal support fitted with WREN suspension forks.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries as the suspension forks can fail prematurely and crack, break or separate during use. This means that the rider may lose control of the bike, leading to injuries.  

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users.  

Stromer have contacted affected customers and advised that they stop using the bikes immediately. Replacement parts are in production. Consumers will be notified once parts become available and advised to take their bike to their nearest authorised dealer.  

Update: On 4th September 2026, the product recall was expanded.

This recall expansion concerns a batch with modified design (oval hole vs round hole), due to the possibility that these forks present the same safety risks as the recalled WREN forks. Consumers who own bike that is fitted with a newly identified affected batch have been contacted directly and should contact their dealer to arrange for their front suspension fork to be replaced free of charge.

Please contact safety@stromerbike.com for further information, or visit: https://www.stromerbike.com/en/voluntary-recall.

Additional information
