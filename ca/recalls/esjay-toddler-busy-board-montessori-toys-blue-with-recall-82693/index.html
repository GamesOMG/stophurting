Esjay Toddler Busy Board Montessori Toys (blue with dinosaurs) Recall (October 2026)

Choking hazard.

WhatEsjay Toddler Busy Board Montessori Toys (blue with dinosaurs)
BrandEsjay
The hazardchoking hazard
CategoryToys and games
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled by​​ Manufacturer ​ Shenzhen Yishijie Technology Co., Ltd Shenzhen, Guangdong Province ​​CHINA​
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-10-07

What was recalled

This recall involves Esjay Toddler Busy Board Montessori Toys (blue with dinosaurs). The busy board is made of blue felt, has a carrying handle, and an elastic cord and button closure. The front is printed with various cartoon dinosaurs, and the back is printed with 26 small squares, each corresponding to a letter of the alphabet. Each square contains the letter and a cartoon animal. The busy board has five pages in total and the page with red small parts is the second page.

Why it was recalled

The red small parts on the second page can detach and pose a choking hazards to children under 3 years of age.  

As of ​September 22, 2026​, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recall products and contact Esjay to obtain a replacement page.

Consumers are to unzip the page with red small parts, write “RECALLED” on both sides of the page and throw it away. Send a photo of the disposed page to Esjay US at esjay-service@outlook.com to receive a replacement page.

For more information, consumers can contact Esjay US at 1-888-206-9309 from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, by email at esjay-service@outlook.com, or online at www.esjay.store/recall or www.esjay.store and click “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 32,340 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from ​August 2023​ to ​September 2026​.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in ​China​
