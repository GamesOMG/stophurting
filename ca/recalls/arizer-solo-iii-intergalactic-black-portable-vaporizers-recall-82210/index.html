Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) Portable Vaporizers Recall (June 2026)

Fire and burn hazards.

WhatArizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) Portable Vaporizers
BrandArizer
The hazardfire and burn hazards
CategoryElectronics
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byImporter 7111495 Canada Inc., dba Arizer Tech Waterloo, ON Canada
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-06-18

What was recalled

This recall involves the Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) Portable Vaporizer. The Solo III is a portable rechargeable battery-powered multi-purpose portable heater powered by an internal lithium-ion battery.

The serial number is etched on the bottom of the device and is also printed on the outside of the product packaging. UPC 628078802274 is printed on the product packaging.

Why it was recalled

The internal lithium-ion battery can ignite or explode, posing fire and burn hazards.

As of June 2, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries in Canada. In the United States, the company has received four reports of incidents and no reports of injuries.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact 7111495 Canada Inc. for a free replacement.

For more information, consumers can contact 7111495 Canada Inc. by telephone at 1-888-291-0521, Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EST, by email at recall@arizer.com or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and 7111495 Canada Inc.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 1,687 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 5,000 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from May 2025 through January 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China.
