Petite Amélie Wooden Mobile Holder Recall (November 2026)

Risk of injuries.

WhatPetite Amélie Wooden Mobile Holder
BrandPetite Amélie
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries because the glued joint of the arm can loosen.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Baby Cots/Cot Beds/Bassinet
Made inChina
Risk levelHigh
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-06 (notice 2611-0018)

What was recalled

Wooden mobile holder designed to attach to cot. Every wooden cot mobile arm MOBARMWLD or MOBARMWL is included in this recall, regardless of batch number.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries because the glued joint of the arm can loosen. This could cause the arm and mobile to detach and fall into the cot or playpen. If a child is underneath the product when it falls, they may receive injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Petite Amelie.

Consumers should stop using the product immediately, remove it from the cot or playpen, and keep it out of reach of children. Affected consumers have been contacted directly and a refund will be issued automatically to the payment method used. Please contact hello@petiteamelie.co.uk for more information or visit Recall: wooden cot mobile arm MOBARMWLD and MOBARMWL - Petite Amélie.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2611-0018

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
