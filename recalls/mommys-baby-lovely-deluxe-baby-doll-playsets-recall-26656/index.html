Recall

Mommy's Baby Lovely Deluxe Baby Doll Playsets Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking hazard — about About 1,034 units.

WhatMommy's Baby Lovely Deluxe Baby Doll Playsets
How manyAbout About 1,034 units
The hazardThe recalled doll playsets violate the small parts ban because the toys are for children under three years old and some of the accessories are small parts, posing a deadly choking hazard.
Sold atOnline at Temu.com from August 2024 through November 2025 for about $31.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled toys immediately, take them away from children, and contact TOYHW for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the baby doll playset and email a photo of the disposed product to TOYHWRecall@163.com to receive a full refund.
ContactTOYHWRecall@163.com.
Recall date2026-07-30 (recall no. 26656)

What was recalled

This recall involves Mommy's Baby Lovely Deluxe Baby Doll Playset, model NEW319A. The recalled playset includes a baby doll, a toy stroller, and plastic accessories including a miniature potty, a baby bib, a pacifier, a plate, a spoon, two cups, two toothbrushes, two bottles of body wash, a bar of soap, and a soap dish. "Product Model: NEW319A" is printed on a sticker affixed to the front of the box.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled toys immediately, take them away from children, and contact TOYHW for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to throw away the baby doll playset and email a photo of the disposed product to TOYHWRecall@163.com to receive a full refund.. TOYHWRecall@163.com.
