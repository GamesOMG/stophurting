Recall

Hopelight Adult Portable Bed Rails Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about About 5,770 units.

WhatHopelight Adult Portable Bed Rails
How manyAbout About 5,770 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. In addition, the bed rails do not bear the required hazard warning labels.
Sold atAmazon.com from August 2023 through May 2026 for between $24 and $28.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact MOCCI SHOP for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the storage bag, cut the handrails' foam, write "RECALLED" on the upper and lower bed rails with a permanent marker, take a photo of the marked bed rail with the consumer's name on a piece of paper and email the photo to hopelightrecall@outlook.com.
ContactMOCCI SHOP by email at hopelightrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-06-11 (recall no. 26543)

What was recalled

This recall involves Hopelight adult portable bed rails. The bed rail comes in silver and black, measure 11.5 inches wide by 23.5 inches tall, is made of metal and weighs 4 pounds. It has the model number, F311, printed on the product's packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact MOCCI SHOP for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the storage bag, cut the handrails' foam, write "RECALLED" on the upper and lower bed rails with a permanent marker, take a photo of the marked bed rail with the consumer's name on a piece of paper and email the photo to hopelightrecall@outlook.com.. MOCCI SHOP by email at hopelightrecall@outlook.com.
