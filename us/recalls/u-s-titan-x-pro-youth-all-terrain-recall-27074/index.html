Recall

U.S. Titan X-Pro Youth All-Terrain Vehicles (ATVs) Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Crash hazard — about 117 units.

WhatU.S. Titan X-Pro Youth All-Terrain Vehicles (ATVs)
How manyAbout 117 units
The hazardThe recalled ATVs violate the mandatory ATV safety standard because the speed-adjusting module included with the vehicle allows for the ATV’s speed to be adjusted in excess of the maximum speed for vehicles intended for children 6 years old and older, posing a risk of serious injury or death from a crash.
Sold atSold Online At: Walmart.com, Powersportsmax.com, Xprousa.com, Amazon.com and eBay.comfrom July 2025 through September 2025for between $680 and $980. · Moshin, LLC., dba. X-PRO, of Carrollton, Texas
RemedyConsumers should stop using the speed-adjusting module included with the vehicle immediately and contact X-PRO for a $100 refund. Consumers can continue using the vehicle without the module. Consumers will be asked to return the module using a prepaid shipping label.
ContactX-Pro at (888) 842-6607 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. CT Monday through Friday, email at recall@moshinllc.com, or online at www.moshinllc.com/eagle110atv_recall.html or www.moshinllc.com and click on “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27074)

What was recalled

This recall involves X-Pro Eagle youth ATVs. The gas-powered, 110cc vehicles were sold in black, blue, red, pink, spider black, spider blue, spider red, leaf pink and tree camo. “X-PRO” is printed on the ATV’s side panel and “This ATV is subject to Titan Imports Inc.’s Action Plan approved by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission” on a plate on the left front side of the frame.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the speed-adjusting module included with the vehicle immediately and contact X-PRO for a $100 refund. Consumers can continue using the vehicle without the module. Consumers will be asked to return the module using a prepaid shipping label.. X-Pro at (888) 842-6607 from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. CT Monday through Friday, email at recall@moshinllc.com, or online at www.moshinllc.com/eagle110atv_recall.html or www.moshinllc.com and click on “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
