Hashmi Kajal Eyeliner Recall (July 2026)

Serious chemical risk.

WhatHashmi Kajal Eyeliner
BrandHashmi
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk as it contains mercury, cadmium, arsenic and lead, which are prohibited for use in cosmetic products.
Product typeCosmetics, personal care and grooming - Cosmetic/Make Up - Eyes
Made inPakistan
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-29 (notice 2607-0193)

What was recalled

Cosmetic product intended to be applied around the eyes. 4.5g tube of firm black paste packaged in a card and plastic bubble pack.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious chemical risk as it contains mercury, cadmium, arsenic and lead. These chemicals are prohibited for use in cosmetic products.

Further information on the health effects of mercury, cadmium, arsenic and lead exposure can be accessed on GOV.UK at the following links:  

  • Mercury: general information - GOV.UK
  • Cadmium: general information - GOV.UK
  • Lead: general information - GOV UK
  • Arsenic: general information – GOV UK

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 (‘The Cosmetics Regulation’).

What to do

OPSS published a Product Safety Report for this product on the 29th July 2026. The business (Hallan Cash & Carry) has subsequently voluntarily recalled the product from end users.

Owners are advised to stop using the product and return it to Hallan Cash & Carry for a full refund.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2607-0193

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
