Recall

Koorlian Mattresses Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire hazard — about 251 units.

WhatKoorlian Mattresses
How manyAbout 251 units
The hazardThe recalled mattresses violate the mandatory flammability standard for mattresses, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from November 2025 through March 2026 for between $110 and $170.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled mattress immediately and contact Dreams Bedding Technology to receive a free cover to put over their mattress to bring it into compliance with the mandatory flammability standards.
ContactDreams Bedding Technology toll-free at 833-224-9277 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at Koorlian@outlook.com or online at https://koorlian.com/pages/customer-recall or https://koorlian.com/ and click "Recall" at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-08-20 (recall no. 26714)

What was recalled

This recall involves Koorlian branded mattresses. The mattresses are 10 inches thick and were sold in a Twin XL size. They have a white top and blue sides and were sold compressed in a box. "Dreams Bedding Technology PTE LTD" the manufacture date and prototype ID "KY15Q290" are printed on a sewn-in white label located on one side of the mattress.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled mattress immediately and contact Dreams Bedding Technology to receive a free cover to put over their mattress to bring it into compliance with the mandatory flammability standards.. Dreams Bedding Technology toll-free at 833-224-9277 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, email at Koorlian@outlook.com or online at https://koorlian.com/pages/customer-recall or https://koorlian.com/ and click "Recall" at the top of the page for more information.
