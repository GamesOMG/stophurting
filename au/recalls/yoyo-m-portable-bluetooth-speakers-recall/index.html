Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury, death and/or property damage from burns and fire.

WhatYoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers
BrandCambridge Audio
The hazardRisk of serious injury, death and/or property damage from burns and fire if the battery overheats and catches fire. Incidents have occurred with the product catching fire, including during charging, and have led to property damage.
Sold atSynergy Audio Visual Pty Ltd
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale1 January 2016 - 31 December 2024
Recalled byCambridge Audio
Made inChina
ContactCambridge Audio Visit: https://www.cambridgeaudio.com/row/en/product-safety/yoyo-m-au Email: recall.australia@cambridgeaudio.com Call: 1300 888 602, 10-4pm Sydney/Melbourne time, Monday to Friday
CategoryLithium-ion batteries · Home electrical appliances
Recall date2026-09-29

What was recalled

Portable rechargeable Bluetooth loudspeakers powered by an internal lithium-ion battery pack, charged via the supplied mains adaptor. The speakers are sold in pairs in light grey, dark grey and blue colours.

Each Yoyo (M) speaker has a serial number printed on its underside, in the format FD C109XX XXXX XXXX. The characters after "FD" are the model reference. All serial numbers are affected.

Why it was recalled

The lithium-ion battery pack in the speaker can overheat and catch fire when being used or when being charged.

What to do

  1. Stop using affected products immediately and disconnect from the power supply.
  2. Complete the online form at https://www.cambridgeaudio.com/row/en/product-safety/yoyo-m-au. You will need the reference and serial number from the underside of each speaker and a photograph of the product showing the serial number.
  3. Cambridge Audio will provide a refund after verifying the claim and will provide instructions for safe disposal of the product at an appropriate facility that accepts lithium-ion batteries.
  4. Do not place the product in household rubbish or recycling bin.
