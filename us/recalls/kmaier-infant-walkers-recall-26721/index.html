Kmaier Infant Walkers Recall (August 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fall hazard.

WhatKmaier Infant Walkers
The hazardThe recalled walkers violate the mandatory standard for infant walkers because they can fit through a standard doorway and fail to stop at the edge of a step, posing a deadly fall hazard.
ContactKmaier by email at TargetplusSJ@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-08-27 (recall no. 26721)

What was recalled

This recall involves Kmaier-branded infant walkers. The pink, green or white walkers have a tray, a fabric seat and a round base with six-wheels. The walkers can be adjusted to nine different height levels. "Model No.: RV001" is printed on a label located under the walker's seat.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-08-27. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
