Agrish Reannounces and Expands of Adult Portable Bed Rails Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about 35,083 units (About 5,000 of these bed rails were previously recalled in January 2026) units.

WhatAgrish Reannounces and Expands of Adult Portable Bed Rails
How manyAbout 35,083 units (About 5,000 of these bed rails were previously recalled in January 2026) units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because when the bed rails are attached to a bed, users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. The bed rails do not meet structural stability or retention strap requirements, posing a fall hazard. Additionally, the bed rails’ push pins and push pin holes are incorrectly sized, posing a laceration hazard.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from April 2025 through May 2026 for between $50 and $80.
RemedyConsumers should immediately stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails and contact Agrish Direct for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to go to http://www.agrish.online/recall to register for the recall and receive instructions on how to dispose of the recalled bed rail.
ContactAgrish Direct at email at agrish.us@outlook.com, or online at http://www.agrish.online/recall or www.agrish.online and click “More” then “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-01 (recall no. 27014)

What was recalled

This recall involves Agrish-branded adult portable bed rails, models AG-12011, 12014 and 12016, for use on adult beds. The model numbers are printed in the owner’s manual and labeled on the bed rail’s metal post. The recalled bed rails have white tubing with rounded gray foam rubber handle grips. Each model has multiple batch numbers and manufacture dates listed on the unit and the instruction manual.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails and contact Agrish Direct for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to go to http://www.agrish.online/recall to register for the recall and receive instructions on how to dispose of the recalled bed rail.. Agrish Direct at email at agrish.us@outlook.com, or online at http://www.agrish.online/recall or www.agrish.online and click “More” then “Recall” at the top of the page for more information.
