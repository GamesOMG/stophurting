Recall

WonderStone Infant Walkers Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fall hazard — about About 70 units.

WhatWonderStone Infant Walkers
How manyAbout About 70 units
The hazardThe recalled infant walkers violate the mandatory standard for infant walkers because they can fit through a standard doorway and fail to stop at the edge of a step, posing a risk of serious injury or death due to a fall hazard.
Sold atOnline at Walmart.com in April 2026 for about $80.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled infant walkers immediately and contact Wonder Stone Toys for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to disassemble the walker, remove and cut the fabric seat, write "Recalled" on the top of the tray in permanent marker and send a photo of the disassembled infant walker showing the cut seat and marked tray to wonderstonerecall@outlook.com.
ContactWonder Stone Toys by email at wonderstonerecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-09 (recall no. 26609)

What was recalled

This recall involves WonderStone-branded Infant Walkers sold in green and pink. The walkers have a gray fabric seat, a white tray with a detachable music box, six wheels, a foot pad and a white hand lever. "Model No: 616" or "Model No: 616-1" is printed on a white label located under the tray.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled infant walkers immediately and contact Wonder Stone Toys for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to disassemble the walker, remove and cut the fabric seat, write "Recalled" on the top of the tray in permanent marker and send a photo of the disassembled infant walker showing the cut seat and marked tray to wonderstonerecall@outlook.com.. Wonder Stone Toys by email at wonderstonerecall@outlook.com.
