Horace Vetiver Primavera Beard Oil Recall (October 2026)

Risk to health.

WhatHorace Vetiver Primavera Beard Oil
BrandOLBO (Horace)
The hazardThe product presents a risk to health because it was found that the fragrance concentration exceeds safety limits for leave-on facial products.
Product typeCosmetics, personal care and grooming - Essential Oils
Made inFrance
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-21 (notice 2609-0073)

What was recalled

30 ml beard oil in glass bottle

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk to health because it was found that the fragrance concentration exceeds safety limits for leave-on facial products. This could cause consumers to experience skin irritation or allergic reactions, particularly in users with sensitive skin.

The product does not meet the requirements of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 (the ‘Cosmetics Regulation’).

What to do

The product has been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by OLBO (Horace).

Consumers should cease use of the product immediately and contact the supplier for a refund or replacement. Contact Horace on team@horace.co for more information.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0073

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
