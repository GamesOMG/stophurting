Axkid MiniKid Core Car Seats Recall (October 2026)

The child car seat did not achieve the level of performance required under the manufactur….

WhatAxkid MiniKid Core Car Seats
BrandAxkid
The hazardThe child car seat did not achieve the level of performance required under the manufacturer’s own safety standards.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment – Baby Car/Booster Seats
Made inSweden
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-22 (notice 2609-0213)

What was recalled

Belt-installed, extended rear-facing car seat (ERF) designed for children from newborn up to approximately 7 years of age, covering 40 to 125cm and up to 28kg.

Why it was recalled

The child car seat did not achieve the level of performance required under the manufacturer’s own safety standards.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Axkid. Visit Product Safety Message - Axkid to register for a free replacement Axkid Minikid 4 Pro. 

You will need the Car Seat ID to register. You can find your Car Seat ID on the product label underneath your car seat, as pictured above. It starts with “E9”.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0213

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
