Recall

Allstar Sharper Image Tabletop and Infinity Fire Pits Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury from Burn and Fire hazard — about 265,000 units.

WhatAllstar Sharper Image Tabletop and Infinity Fire Pits
How manyAbout 265,000 units
The hazardIgnited flammable liquid can escape from the recalled fire pits or trigger flame jetting from the fuel containers, posing a risk of serious injury from burn and fire hazards.
Sold atSold At: Ace Hardware, Burlington, Home Depot, Lowe’s, Kohl’s, Marshalls, Nordstrom, Rite Aid, Target, TJ Maxx, Walgreens, Walmart and other retailers nationwide, and online, including Amazon.com and Hsn.com from January 2023 through June 2026 for between $30 and $50.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled fire pits immediately and contact Allstar for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to register at www.firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com, write “RECALLED – DO NOT USE” in permanent marker, and submit three photographs following the instructions on Allstar’s recall page. After registration is confirmed, consumers should dispose of any remaining fuel in accordance with local hazardous-waste requirements and discard the recalled fire pits safely.
ContactAllstar toll-free at 855-215-5032 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at allstarmarketing6861@sedgwick.com or online at www.firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com or www.allstarmg.com and click on “Fire Pit Safety Recall” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27082)

What was recalled

This recall involves outdoor Sharper Image-Branded Tabletop Fire Pits and Sharper Image-Branded Tabletop Infinity Fire Pits. The units have a “77” logo on the front, a Lot Code sticker in the center of the base, a round black sticker marked “CHINA” with a 5-digit code, the letters KS-CY or KS-FC, and a date code in YYYY-MM-DD format. The Sharper Image-Branded Tabletop Fire Pits feature a small cylindrical concrete body with a removable snuffing lid and an open burn chamber at the top. The Sharper Image-Branded Tabletop Infinity Fire Pits feature a steel base and tempered‑glass side panels.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled fire pits immediately and contact Allstar for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to register at www.firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com, write “RECALLED – DO NOT USE” in permanent marker, and submit three photographs following the instructions on Allstar’s recall page. After registration is confirmed, consumers should dispose of any remaining fuel in accordance with local hazardous-waste requirements and discard the recalled fire pits safely..
