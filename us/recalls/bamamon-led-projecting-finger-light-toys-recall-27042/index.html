BAMAMON LED Projecting Finger Light Toys Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Battery Ingestion — about 6,533 units.

WhatBAMAMON LED Projecting Finger Light Toys
How manyAbout 6,533 units
The hazardNone reported.
Sold atSold At: Amazon.com from March 2025 through August 2026 for about $13 to $14. · Shenzhen Maiduoduo Trading Technology Co., Ltd., dba Bamamon, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the toys immediately and contact Bamamon. Consumers will be asked to email a photograph showing the product being properly disposed of in the trash to bamamonproductrecall@outlook.com.
ContactBamamon by email at bamamonproductrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-10-22 (recall no. 27042)

What was recalled

This recall involves Bamamon LED Projecting Finger Light toys which contain three button cells batteries. The finger lights come in 32 pieces in a box in red, orange, blue, black, purple, clear, green and yellow. The finger lights project eight space themed patterns such as astronauts, rockets, UFO, galaxy and solar systems printed on labels located on the front of the package.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the toys immediately and contact Bamamon. Consumers will be asked to email a photograph showing the product being properly disposed of in the trash to bamamonproductrecall@outlook.com.. Bamamon by email at bamamonproductrecall@outlook.com.
