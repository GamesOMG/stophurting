GASGAS Motocross and Cross Country motorbikes (MY21-24) Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury or death.

WhatGASGAS Motocross and Cross Country motorbikes (MY21-24)
BrandGASGAS
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death if the brake calliper fails and causes an accident. An incident of the brake calliper failing causing an accident has occurred overseas.
Sold atPlease see attached list of dealers who sold the product.
Where it was soldNationally
On sale1 November 2022 - 14 August 2026
Recalled byKTM Australia Pty Ltd
Made inAustria
ContactKTM Australia Pty Ltd Call: 1800 644 771 Email: recalls-au@gasgas.com Visit: https://www.gasgas.com/en-au.html
CategoryDirt bikes and miniature motorbikes
Recall date2026-08-27

What was recalled

Full-sized MX motocross and Cross Country models with BRAKTEC rear brake system.

  • 2-stroke and 4-stroke models
  • MC and EX models variants
  • Model years 2021 – 2024

Please see attached list of affected VINs.

These motorbikes are not road-registerable.

Why it was recalled

The rear brake calliper assembly may crack and break under excessive stress loads.

What to do

  1. Stop using the dirt bike immediately. Do not use the bike until the rear brake calliper has been replaced by an authorised dealership.
  2. Contact your local authorised dealer to schedule an appointment to have the rear brake calliper, including the calliper bracket, replaced at no cost to you. Do not attempt to replace the calliper yourself.
  3. For further information or assistance with this recall, please contact your authorised dealer or KTM Australia Pty Ltd.
