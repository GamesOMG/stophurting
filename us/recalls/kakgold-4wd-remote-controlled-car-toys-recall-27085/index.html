Recall

Kakgold 4WD Remote-Controlled Car Toys Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Battery Ingestion.

WhatKakgold 4WD Remote-Controlled Car Toys
The hazardThe recalled remote-controlled cars violate the mandatory standard for toys because they contain button cell and lithium-ion batteries in the remote control that can be easily accessed by children, posing a deadly ingestion hazard to children. When button cell or coin batteries are swallowed, the ingested batteries can cause serious injuries, internal chemical burns and death
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from July 2026 throughSeptember 2026 for between $34 and $50. · Shenzhenshi Juteng Lechuang Technology Co., Ltd., dba Kakgold, of China
ContactKakgold by email at kakrecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-11-12 (recall no. 27085)

What was recalled

This recall involves the Kakgold 4WD stunt vehicles with a remote-control containing button cell and lithium-ion batteries. The toy car has a lime green and blue body, a black chassis and four black wheels with lime green trim and diagonal rollers. The vehicle body features checkered patterns, an “X” design and the words “PRO” and “RACING.” A white hang tag attached to the vehicle body displays “Do Not Twist the Shaft” in red lettering and illustrations marked with red crosses.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-11-12. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
