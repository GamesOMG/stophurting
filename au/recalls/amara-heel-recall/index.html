Amara Heel Recall (July 2026)

Serious injury from falls.

WhatAmara Heel
The hazardRisk of serious injury from falls if the shoe’s heel detaches while being worn. Incidents of the heel detaching have occurred.
Sold atFRANKIE4 website and concept stores David Jones website and stores
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale22 March 2026 - 25 June 2026
Recalled byFRANKIE4 HOLDINGS PTY LTD
ContactFRANKIE4 Call (07) 3106 3351 between 9am and 5pm AEST, Monday to Friday. Email info@frankie4.com.au Visit https://frankie4.com/pages/customer-care
CategoryJewellery and fashion accessories
Recall date2026-07-17

What was recalled

Women’s smooth leather court shoe (pump) with squared-off toe and 6.5cm heel height.

P/O number stamped on inside of shoe and on box.

Colours:

  • Black (P/O 3685)
  • Camel (P/O 3686)

Why it was recalled

The shoe’s heel may become detached during wear without warning.

What to do

  1. Stop wearing the heels immediately.
  2. Contact the FRANKIE4 Customer Care team to arrange to return the heels and receive a full refund issued to your original payment method. The return will be arranged at no cost to you. Alternatively, return the heels to any FRANKIE4 Concept Store.
  3. If you have gifted or resold your Amara heels, please pass this notice on to the person who received the heels.
