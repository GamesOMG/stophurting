Habico Ltd 350g Jars of Coloured Sand Recall (July 2026)

The products present a risk to health as the sand in the jars may be contaminated with a….

WhatHabico Ltd 350g Jars of Coloured Sand
BrandHabico Ltd
The hazardThe products present a risk to health as the sand in the jars may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment – Arts/Crafts/Needlework Sand Supplies
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-07-09 (notice 2606-0268)

What was recalled

350g jars of coloured play sand purchased on or after 1st June 2023. Jars with and without batch codes are currently subject to recall. The affected product can be identified by the statement ‘distributed by Habico Ltd, LS12 4BD’ printed on the label. For retailer-specific identifiers, please see Annex A.

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk to health as the sand in the jars may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos. Asbestos is a banned substance because it poses a risk to health even at low levels of exposure.

The products do not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The products have been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by Habico Limited, ESPO, and YPO Limited. For more information, please contact Habico Limited via 0113 263 1500 / sales@habico.co.uk For distributor contact information, please see Annex A.

We recommend owners stop using the product immediately and return it to the retailer where specified for a full refund. Follow the below instructions for details on disposal and return of the product.

Annex A: Distributor Product Identifiers Contact Information ESPO Coloured Craft Sand: 60038, set of 8 x 350g, Coloured Craft Sand: 277363, set of 20 x 350g, Pastels Sand: 233439, set of 6 x 350g, Fluorescent Craft Sand: 06760, set of 6 x 350g ESPO website. Email: product.recall@espo.org - please use reference HABICO23 in the email subject line, YPO Limited 733604 Sand Fluorescent Assorted Colours 350g Pack of 6, 747335 Coloured Sand 350g Shakers – Pack of 20, Assorted YPO website (see link for contact details) Disposal instructions:
  • Stop using the product immediately.
  • If the sand is still in its packaging, place in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double tape it securely, label it clearly and store in a secure location out of the reach of children.
  • If the sand has been used, clean up sites where the product was used with wet cloths to avoid generating dust. Wear gloves and a mask. Double bag the sand, gloves, mask and cloths.
  • Keep children and other people away from areas where the sand has been used until the area has been cleaned.
  • Return the product to store where specified by the retailer for a full refund.
  • If you are unable to return the product to the retailer, follow the steps above and contact your local authority / access their website for guidance on safe disposal of asbestos in your area.
  • For further information, please contact the retailer or visit the GOV.UK page on asbestos in consumer products.
