Baby Sensory Activity Toys Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation.

What was recalled

Infant and toddler developmental toys made of plastic and/or soft silicone. Affected products feature bright colours, textured surfaces, rattling and spinning elements, pull strings and sensory stimulation details. Affected products include the following:

Why it was recalled

The products may not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. Silicone pull strings on the products are too long and can reach the back of a child's throat causing choking or asphyxiation. Small parts may detach from the products and cause a child to choke if they place them in their mouth.

What to do

  1. Stop using the products immediately and keep it out of reach of children.
  2. SHEIN will contact affected consumers by email with a link to confirm disposal of the product.
  3. SHEIN will provide a full refund when consumers confirm disposal via the link in the email.
  4. Contact SHEIN if you have not received the email or are having difficulties with the email.
