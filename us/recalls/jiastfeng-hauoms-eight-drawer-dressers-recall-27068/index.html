Recall

Jiastfeng HAUOMS Eight-drawer Dressers Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Tip-Over and Entrapment hazard — about 1,392 units.

WhatJiastfeng HAUOMS Eight-drawer Dressers
How manyAbout 1,392 units
The hazardThe recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from May 2025 through September 2026 for about $150. · Zhengzhoushi Rupiao Electronic Commerce Co., Ltd., dba Jiastfeng, of China
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact Jiastfeng for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the top, front and sides of the dresser, remove all drawers and send photos of the marked and disassembled dresser to recall@hauoms.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product.
ContactJiastfeng by email at recall@hauoms.com.
Recall date2026-11-05 (recall no. 27068)

What was recalled

This recall involves HAUOM-branded Eight-drawer dressers. The wooden black dressers measure about 27.6 inches wide, 15.8 inches long, 59.1 inches tall, and weigh about 112.4 pounds. "SKU: AJ_DJ504298_1" is printed on the product packaging.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact Jiastfeng for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the top, front and sides of the dresser, remove all drawers and send photos of the marked and disassembled dresser to recall@hauoms.com. Consumers should then dispose of the product.. Jiastfeng by email at recall@hauoms.com.
