Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers Recall (September 2026)

Fire hazard.

WhatCambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers
BrandCambridge Audio
The hazardfire hazard
CategoryElectronics
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byDistributor Cambridge Audio/Audio Partnership Plc London United Kingdom
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-09-22

What was recalled

This recall involves Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) portable Bluetooth speakers available in dark gray, light gray and blue. They come in a boxed pair of two speakers. Both speakers carry the same FD number. There is no batch number for this product, and every unit is affected, wherever and whenever it was bought.

Why it was recalled

The Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) portable Bluetooth speaker lithium-ion batteries can overheat, posing a fire hazard.

As of September 14, 2026, the company has received no reports of incident or injury in Canada.

What to do

Consumers who have these affected products should immediately stop using them and contact Cambridge Audio to verify if the product is affected by the recall.

Consumers should keep the recalled speakers until they receive further instruction from Cambridge Audio. Keep the speakers switched off and unplugged and do not charge them. Store them somewhere cool, dry and out of the way, clear of anything that burns easily (such as paper, cardboard and flammable liquids), and away from radiators, direct sunlight and excessive heat. The refund process can be found at the company's website. Once Cambridge Audio has processed the claim and confirmed a refund, the consumer may dispose of the product safely in such a way that they cannot be used again. Contact your municipality for instructions on how to safely transport and dispose of lithium-ion batteries. Batteries are considered hazardous waste. Do not place them in household garbage.

For more information, consumers can contact Cambridge Audio/Audio Partnership Plc by telephone at 888-805-5001, from 9am to 5pm Eastern Time (ET), Monday to Friday, by email at recall.canada@cambridgeaudio.com or visit the company website.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 309 units of the affected products were sold in Canada.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from 2016 to 2022.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
