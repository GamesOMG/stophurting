BlueMello Baby Swaddle Blanket sold via Amazon Recall (November 2026)

Serious risk of asphyxiation as the baby may slip down inside the swaddle.

WhatBlueMello Baby Swaddle Blanket sold via Amazon
The hazardThe product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation as the baby may slip down inside the swaddle.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment - Swaddle Blanket/Wrap Cloth
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-17 (notice 2610-0066)

What was recalled

Pink plush infant sleeping swaddle.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of asphyxiation, overheating and death. The design of the product allows the baby to slip below the covers causing overheating and potentially airway obstruction, both of which have been associated with sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS). Improvements are also required to the product marking, warning and labelling.

Further information on safe sleeping for babies can be found on the NHS website at the following link: https://www.nhs.uk/start-for-life/baby/baby-basics/newborn-and-baby-sleeping-advice-for-parents/safe-sleep-advice-for-babies/.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

OPSS are requiring the recall of the product from end users. If you own this product, stop using it and contact the seller for a refund.

The listing has been removed by the online marketplace (Amazon).

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0066

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
