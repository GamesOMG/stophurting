Craftplay Sand Art Bottles (10) sold via Amazon Recall (August 2026)

The products present a risk to health as the sand inside the bottles may be contaminated….

WhatCraftplay Sand Art Bottles (10) sold via Amazon
BrandCraftplay
The hazardThe products present a risk to health as the sand inside the bottles may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment – Arts/Crafts/Needlework Sand Supplies
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-05 (notice 2607-0121)

What was recalled

Bottles of play sand sold in packs of 10.

Why it was recalled

The products present a risk to health as the sand inside the bottles may be contaminated with a small quantity of asbestos. Asbestos is a banned substance because it poses a risk to health even at low levels of exposure.  

The products do not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by Amazon.

We recommend owners stop using this product immediately and keep it out of the reach of children. Follow the below instructions for details on disposal and return of the product.

Disposal instructions:
  • Stop using the product immediately.    
  • If the sand is still in its packaging, place in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double tape it securely, label it clearly and store in a secure location out of the reach of children.    
  • If the sand has been used, clean up sites where the product was used using wet cloths to avoid generating dust. Wear gloves and a mask. Double bag the sand, gloves, mask and cloths.     
  • Keep children and other people away from areas where the sand has been used until the area has been cleaned.    
  • Return the product to store where specified by the retailer for a full refund.
  • If you are unable to return the product to the retailer, or the product was purchased via an online marketplace, follow the steps above and contact your local authority / access their website for guidance on safe disposal of asbestos in your area.
  • For further information, please contact the retailer or visit the GOV.UK page on asbestos in consumer products.
Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0121

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
