Qinbeer silicone pull string toys sold through Amazon Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation.

WhatQinbeer silicone pull string toys sold through Amazon
BrandQinbeer
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death from choking and asphyxiation if a child places the silicone pull strings in their mouth or if small parts detach from the toy and the child places them in their mouth.
Sold atProduct was sold via Amazon Australia
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale31 July 2025 - 1 July 2026
Recalled byShenzhen Qinbeier Trading Co., Ltd
Made inChina
ContactShenzhen Qinbeier Trading Co., Ltd Email: qinbeerrecall@outlook.com
CategoryToys for babies and toddlers
Recall date2026-09-15

What was recalled

Montessori toys for babies with food grade silicone pull string activities sold via Amazon Australia.

Why it was recalled

The product may not comply with the mandatory standard for toys for children up to and including 36 months of age. The product’s silicone pull strings are too long and can reach the back of a child's throat causing choking or asphyxiation. Small parts may detach from the product and cause a child to choke if they place them in their mouth.

What to do

  1. Stop using the toy immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Cut the strings of the toy and take a photograph of the toy before disposing of it in household waste.
  3. Email the photograph and your order details to qinbeerrecall@outlook.com who will process a refund for the original order via Amazon.
  4. For further information or assistance with this recall, please email qinbeerrecall@outlook.com.
