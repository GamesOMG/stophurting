What was recalled

A wood cutting blade marketed as suitable for use with mini grinders.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious risk of injuries as the toothed saw blade attachment increases the likelihood of kickback and loss of control of the angle grinder. If the blade snags or strikes an object during use, the tool may be suddenly forced backwards towards the operator, which could result in serious injury to the operator or bystanders.

OPSS has published a Product Safety Alert for angle grinder toothed saw blade attachments: Product Safety Alert: Angle Grinder Toothed Saw Blade Attachments (PSA5) - GOV.UK.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been withdrawn from the market and recalled by Toolpak. Affected consumers have been contacted directly.

We recommend that you stop using the product immediately and contact the retailer/supplier you purchased from to request a refund.
