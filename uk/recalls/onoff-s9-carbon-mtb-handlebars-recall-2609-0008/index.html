ONOFF S9 Carbon MTB Handlebars Recall (September 2026)

Risk of injuries as some handlebars have been produced with insufficient wall thickness,….

WhatONOFF S9 Carbon MTB Handlebars
BrandONOFF
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries as some handlebars have been produced with insufficient wall thickness, resulting in reduced structural strength.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Cycles Parts - Frames/Stems
Made inTaiwan
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-09-04 (notice 2609-0008)

What was recalled

Black carbon bicycle handlebars used to steer and control the bicycle whilst riding.

Why it was recalled

Due to a manufacturing deviation, some handlebars were produced with insufficient wall thickness, resulting in reduced structural strength. Under normal riding conditions, the handlebar may crack or fracture. The handlebar failure may lead to a sudden loss of control which can then result in falls, collisions and injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Blue Factory Team, as manufacturer of OnOff bicycle components and the Mondraker brand.

Users should verify their bike’s number using the serial number checker on the Mondraker recall page: https://mondraker.com/wo/en/recall-s9. The bike serial number can be found on the down tube or on the bottom of the bottom bracket.

If the bike is confirmed as affected, stop using it immediately and contact the place of purchase/Mondraker dealer to arrange a free replacement. A new, reinforced version of the handlebar will be fitted. Please see: https://mondraker.com/wo/en/shops for a full list of Mondraker dealers.

For further information please email: recall@mondraker.com

Additional information

PSD case number: 2609-0008

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
