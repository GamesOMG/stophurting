Arctic Zone Titan Pro Coolers Recall (June 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Choking hazard — about About 2,500 units.

WhatArctic Zone Titan Pro Coolers
How manyAbout About 2,500 units
The hazardThe recalled coolers' latch magnet can detach, posing risk of serious injury or death from a choking hazard.
Sold atCostco stores nationwide in March 2026 for about $40.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the coolers immediately and contact California Innovations for a free replacement latch, including shipping. Consumers will also receive replacement instructions and a tool to remove the existing latch and replace it with the new latch.
ContactCalifornia Innovations toll-free at 888-413-2665 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at service@ca-innovations.com, or online at https://arcticzone.com/pages/product-recall or https://arcticzone.com, and click "Recall" at the bottom of the webpage.
Recall date2026-06-11 (recall no. 26553)

What was recalled

This recall involves Arctic Zone Titan Pro 40 can-capacity, iceless coolers. The rectangular coolers were sold in black and brown and have a built?in bottle opener. The zipperless coolers are collapsible and measure about 15 inches wide, 12 inches deep and 12 inches high when open. The coolers have a hard-top lid with a front latch, a front insulated compartment with a front zippered pocket, two mesh side pockets, a shoulder strap with pad and grab handles. "Titan Pro by Arctic Zone" is printed on the front of the coolers and style number 10006348 is on a sewn-in label inside the front zippered pocket.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the coolers immediately and contact California Innovations for a free replacement latch, including shipping. Consumers will also receive replacement instructions and a tool to remove the existing latch and replace it with the new latch.. California Innovations toll-free at 888-413-2665 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at service@ca-innovations.com, or online at https://arcticzone.com/pages/product-recall or https://arcticzone.com, and click "Recall" at the bottom of the webpage.
