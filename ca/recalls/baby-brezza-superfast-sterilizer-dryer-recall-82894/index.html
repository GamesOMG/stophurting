Baby Brezza SuperFast Sterilizer Dryer Recall (November 2026)

Fire and burn hazards.

WhatBaby Brezza SuperFast Sterilizer Dryer
BrandBaby Brezza
The hazardfire and burn hazards
CategorySpecialized products
Who it affectsGeneral public Children, pregnant or breast feeding women
Recalled byImporter EI Brand Management Inc. Saint-Laurent, Quebec Canada Manufacturer Baby Brezza Enterprises LLC Newark, New Jersey United States of America
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-05

What was recalled

This recall involves the Baby Brezza SuperFast Sterilizer Dryer. The affected product sterilizes and dries baby bottles and their parts and accessories, and has model number BRZ0083 and UPC 886267008313. The model number can be found on a label on the underside of the product and the UPC can be found on the product packaging. 

Why it was recalled

The product can overheat when plugged in and in use, posing fire and burn hazards.

As of October 29, 2026, the company has received 20 reports of incidents of the product overheating and melting and no reports of injury in Canada. In the United States, the company has received 32 reports of incidents and no report of injuries.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact EI Brand Management Inc. for instructions on how to obtain a free replacement. 

For more information, consumers can contact EI Brand Management Inc. by email at service@elfe.ca or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Baby Brezza Enterprises LLC.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 6,001 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 29,000 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from September 2023 to November 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
