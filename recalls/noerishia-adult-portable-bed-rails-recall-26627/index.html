Recall

Noerishia Adult Portable Bed Rails Recall (July 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about About 504 units.

WhatNoerishia Adult Portable Bed Rails
How manyAbout About 504 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. In addition, the bed rails do not bear the required hazard warning labels.
Sold atOnline at Amazon.com from March 2026 through April 2026 for about $100.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact Hurzein for a refund. Consumers will be asked to cut off the gray foam handgrip padding and cut the securing straps in half, to render them unusable, then take a photo of the destroyed bed rail and send it to NoerishiaBedRailsRecall@outlook.com.
ContactHurzein by email at NoerishiaBedRailsRecall@outlook.com.
Recall date2026-07-16 (recall no. 26627)

What was recalled

This recall involves Noerishia adult portable bed rails. The bed rails come in black, measure 24 inches wide by 36 inches tall, are made of carbon steel, and weigh 8.4 pounds. "Model: KDB-504B" and "Batch: SO2025111104" are printed on the product packaging. The Noerishia branding "Noerishia" and "Model: KDB-504B" are printed on the instruction manual.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact Hurzein for a refund. Consumers will be asked to cut off the gray foam handgrip padding and cut the securing straps in half, to render them unusable, then take a photo of the destroyed bed rail and send it to NoerishiaBedRailsRecall@outlook.com.. Hurzein by email at NoerishiaBedRailsRecall@outlook.com.
