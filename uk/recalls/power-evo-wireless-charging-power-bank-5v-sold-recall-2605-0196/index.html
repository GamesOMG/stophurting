Power Evo Wireless Charging Power Bank 5V sold by TK Maxx Recall (June 2026)

Risk of fire as the internal wiring is insecurely attached, which may lead to overheating….

WhatPower Evo Wireless Charging Power Bank 5V sold by TK Maxx
BrandPower Evo
The hazardThe product presents a risk of fire as the internal wiring is insecurely attached, which may lead to overheating and fires.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment – Chargers
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-03 (notice 2605-0196)

What was recalled

Two models of 5-volt power bank supplied in black and white colours.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of fire as, in some units, the wiring may not be securely attached. If the wiring becomes loose or detaches during use, the product may overheat and ignite, causing a fire. 

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The products have been recalled from end users by TK Maxx. 

Owners are advised to stop using the product immediately and return it to TK Maxx for a refund or replacement.  

For further information, access the TK Maxx website at: Safety notice - Product Recall - TK Maxx UK

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2605-0196

Notifying Authority: Local Authority Trading Standards
