Flymo SimpliTrim 230 AC Trimmer and Flymo SimpliTrim 230 Plus AC Trimmer Recall (October 2026)

Risk of injuries.

WhatFlymo SimpliTrim 230 AC Trimmer and Flymo SimpliTrim 230 Plus AC Trimmer
BrandFlymo
The hazardThe product presents a risk of injuries because the spool cover may break and be ejected at a high speed during operation.
Product typeMachinery - Lawn Powers/Rakers (Powered)
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-07 (notice 2610-0026)

What was recalled

Orange and black lawn trimmer with orange power supply cord.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of injuries because the spool cover on affected units may break and be ejected at high speed during operation. This poses a risk of physical injury to the user and bystanders.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Supply of Machinery Regulations 2008.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Flymo.

Owners are advised to stop using the product, ensure it is unplugged, and return it to the retailer purchased from for a refund, or contact Flymo directly to arrange for collection and refund.

For further information, please access the Flymo website at the following link: https://www.flymo.com/uk/c/support/flymo-faqs/product-recall-information

Additional information

PSD case number: 2610-0026

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
