Recall

Squeezy Dumplings sold by Samsons Cash and Carry Ltd Recall (June 2026)

Serious chemical risk.

WhatSqueezy Dumplings sold by Samsons Cash and Carry Ltd
The hazardThe product presents a serious chemical risk because the outer layer contains an excess concentration of benzene.
Product typeToys - Dolls/Soft Toys (Non Powered)
Made inChina
Risk levelSerious
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-06-25 (notice 2606-0196)

What was recalled

Plastic ‘basket style’ pot containing a single soft plastic polymer dumpling toy.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a serious chemical risk because the outer layer contains an excess concentration of benzene (20mg/kg). If inhaled, benzene can cause irritation to the eyes, nose and throat. Exposure to large amounts may also cause a burning feeling throughout the digestive tract and skin irritation. Improvements are also required to product labelling and marking.

Further information regarding benzene can be found on Gov.uk at the following link: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/benzene-general-information-incident-management-and-toxicology/benzene-general-information

The product does not meet the requirements of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Samsons Cash and Carry Ltd.

Owners are advised to stop using the product and keep it out of reach of children. For more information, please contact samsonscashandcarry@hotmail.co.uk

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2606-0196

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
