Recall

DR Power Equipment Pilot and Premier Trimmer Mowers Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Fire and Burn hazard.

WhatDR Power Equipment Pilot and Premier Trimmer Mowers
The hazardGrass and debris can accumulate in the trimmer mowers and ignite, posing a risk of serious injury or death from fire and burn hazards.
Sold atSold At: Ace, TSC, Bomgaars, other home improvement, hardware and independent power equipment stores nationwide and online at drpower.com from November 2022 through July 2026 for between $380 to $550.
ContactDR Power Equipment toll-free at 800-854-3194 from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at recall@drpower.com or online at http://drpower.com/recalls or www.drpower.com and click “Product Recalls” at the bottom of the page for more information.
Recall date2026-10-08 (recall no. 27016)

What was recalled

This recall involves DR Power Equipment Pilot and Premier walk-behind trimmer mowers. The trimmer mowers are orange and black and have the DR Power logo on the top of the motor and on the top of the frame above the cutter head. The trimmer mowers are labeled “Premier” or “Pro” on the side of the frame. The model numbers are printed on a label located on the side of the product’s frame.

Source: the official CPSC recall notice, published 2026-10-08. Every fact on this page comes from that notice — if anything here disagrees with it, the notice wins.
