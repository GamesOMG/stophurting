Recall

True Blue robotic pool cleaner sold at Bunnings Warehouse Recall (August 2026)

Serious injury or death and property damage.

WhatTrue Blue robotic pool cleaner sold at Bunnings Warehouse
BrandTrue Blue Pool Robotic Vacuum Cleaner
The hazardRisk of serious injury or death and property damage if the cleaner ignites and causes a fire. Incidents of the cleaner catching fire while charging have occurred.
Sold atBunnings Warehouse
Where it was soldNationally
On sale1 November 2023 - 30 April 2025
Recalled byPOOL SYSTEMS PTY. LTD
Made inChina
ContactTrue Blue Customer Service Call: 1300 827 665
CategoryLithium-ion batteries · Swimming pools and spas (permanent)
Recall date2026-08-06

What was recalled

Rechargeable robotic swimming pool cleaner.

Please see attached list of affected serial numbers.

Why it was recalled

The affected product’s lithium-ion battery may overheat and ignite if charged in hot weather or direct sunlight and catch fire.

What to do

  1. Stop using the robotic pool cleaner immediately. Unplug it and place it in a cool place.
  2. Call the True Blue Customer Service number to arrange for a courier to collect the robotic pool cleaner, free of charge.
    • You will need to provide your details, contact information, including the serial number of the robotic pool cleaner (available under the hood).
    • Once the robotic pool cleaner has been collected, the courier will provide you with a receipt and you will be contacted by Bunnings, who will arrange the payment of a full refund.
