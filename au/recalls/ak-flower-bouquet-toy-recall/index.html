Recall

AK Flower Bouquet Toy Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury to face or eyes of young children during play or use.

WhatAK Flower Bouquet Toy
BrandAdairs
The hazardRisk of serious injury to face or eyes of young children during play or use if the metal rod is exposed from the toy.
Sold atAdairs
Where it was soldNationally · Online
On sale10 July 2026 - 14 September 2026
Recalled byAdairs Retail Group Pty Ltd
Made inChina
ContactADAIRS RETAIL GROUP PTY LTD Call: 1300 783 005 Email: info@adairs.com.au Visit: https://www.adairs.com.au/contact-us/
CategoryStuffed and plush toys
Recall date2026-09-18

What was recalled

Plush colourful fabric in the shape of 3 stemmed flowers with playful faces secured by a large bow. Used in play or as a decorative item for children.

Why it was recalled

The internal metal rod that supports the flower stem may penetrate the material of the flower or the stem and be exposed.

What to do

  1. Stop using the toy immediately and place out of reach of any children.
  2. Return the toy to an Adairs Retail Store for a full refund.
  3. Alternatively, contact Adairs Customer Service for a pre-paid label to post the toy back to Adairs, who will then issue a full refund.
