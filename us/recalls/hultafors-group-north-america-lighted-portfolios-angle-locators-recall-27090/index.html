Recall

Hultafors Group North America Lighted Portfolios, Angle Locators, Calipers and Calculators Recall (November 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Battery Ingestion — about 229,430 units.

WhatHultafors Group North America Lighted Portfolios, Angle Locators, Calipers and Calculators
How manyAbout 229,430 units
The hazardThe various trade tools violate the mandatory safety standard for consumer products with button cell and coin batteries because the button batteries can be accessed easily by children, posing an ingestion hazard. Additionally, the packaging and product do not have the warnings required under Reese’s Law. When button cell or coin batteries are swallowed, the ingested batteries can cause serious injuries, internal chemical burns and death.
Sold atSold At: The Johnson Level Digital Angle Locator, Digital Caliper and Building Calculator were sold at Lowe’s Home Improvement, Menards, W.W. Grainger, True Value Hardware and other hardware stores nationwide and online at Amazon.com from February 2023 through November 2026 between $64 and $400. The CLC Lighted Portfolio and Johnson Level 7” Digital Angle Locator and Ruler were sold at Lowe’s Home Improvement, Menards, W.W. Grainger, True Value Hardware and other hardware stores nationwide and online at Amazon.com from March 2024 through November 2026 between $45 and $55.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled trade tools immediately, place them in an area inaccessible to children and contact Hultafors Group North America. To obtain a refund, consumers will be asked to upload a photograph of the product at www.HG2026.expertinquiry.com and certify that the product has been disposed of and will no longer be used. Upon verification, consumers will receive the retail price, as shown in table below in the form of a virtual Visa gift card. The virtual Visa gift card may be redeemed at any establishment that accepts Visa. Note: Button cell batteries are hazardous. Batteries should be disposed of or recycled by following local hazardous waste procedures.
ContactHultafors Group North America toll-free at 877-722-7026 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, email at HultaforsGroup7307@sedgwick.com or online at www.HG2026.expertinquiry.com.
Recall date2026-11-19 (recall no. 27090)
