Aussie Clean Drain Cleaner 4pk Recall (July 2026)

Serious illness or death.

WhatAussie Clean Drain Cleaner 4pk
BrandAussie Clean
The hazardRisk of serious illness or death if a child swallows the contents of the product. Risk of serious injury if the product comes into contact with mucous membranes (eg, mouth, nose or eyes) or skin. An incident of a child ingesting the product has occurred.
Sold atPlease see attached list of traders.
Where it was soldNationally
On sale12 December 2025 - 24 June 2026
Recalled byMNB Variety Imports
Made inChina
ContactMNB Variety Imports Call: 02 9690 1622 Email: Info@mnb.com.au
CategoryCleaning products
Recall date2026-07-30

What was recalled

Drain cleaning powder used to help clear blocked household drains and pipes, supplied in 4 single-use foil packs for domestic use.

Why it was recalled

The product is not packed and labelled as legally required. The packaging does not contain the required safety information to ensure consumers know how to handle the product safely.

What to do

  1. Stop using the product immediately and place out of reach of children.
  2. Return the product to the place of purchase for a full refund.
