4-in-1 Handheld Cordless Vacuum sold via eBay Recall (October 2026)

The product was supplied without the required manufacturer and importer identification de….

What4-in-1 Handheld Cordless Vacuum sold via eBay
The hazardThe product was supplied without the required manufacturer and importer identification details.
Product typeElectrical appliances and equipment – Household Vacuum Cleaners
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-01 (notice 2609-0144)

What was recalled

Cordless handheld/stick vacuum cleaner marketed as a “four in one” product. The product is supplied with a floor brush, crevice tool, brush tool, telescopic extension tube and washable HEPA filter.

Why it was recalled

The product was supplied without the required manufacturer and importer identification details. The product did not include the manufacturer’s postal address, nor the UK importer’s name, registered trade name or trademark, or postal address. In addition, the CE marking displayed on the product was below the minimum prescribed size requirements.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by the eBay seller – Storm.Trading (JD Europe). Affected customers have been contacted directly by the supplier. If you own this product, stop using it and contact the eBay seller for a refund, repair or replacement.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2609-0144

Notified by: Office for Product Safety and Standards
