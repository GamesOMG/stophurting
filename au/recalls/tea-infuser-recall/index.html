Tea infuser Recall (August 2026)

Injury from cuts or scratches by the loose or raised section of the tea infuser when used.

WhatTea infuser
BrandFrank Green
The hazardRisk of injury from cuts or scratches by the loose or raised section of the tea infuser when used. An incident of a consumer being scratched has occurred.
Sold atPlease see attached list of retailers.
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale17 March 2025 - 3 August 2026
Recalled byTHINKONE PTY LTD trading as Frank Green
Made inChina
ContactFrank Green Email: hello@frankgreen.com.au or use the Contact form Customer service team available between 9:00am and 5:00pm, Monday to Friday
CategoryKitchenware and containers
Recall date2026-08-21

What was recalled

Tea infuser designed for use with Frank Green ceramic reusable cups and bottles 295ml and up.

Product number: D21S30

Why it was recalled

On affected tea infusers, sections of the metal finish on the top metal rim may be loose or raised.

What to do

  1. Stop using the product immediately and inspect the product for loose or raised metal materials on the tea infuser.
  2. If your product is affected, contact Frank Green to arrange for a full refund and dispose of the product in accordance with local waste disposal requirements.
  3. If you require further information or assistance with this recall, contact Frank Green.
