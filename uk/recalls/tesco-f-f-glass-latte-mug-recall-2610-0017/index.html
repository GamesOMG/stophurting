Tesco F&F Glass Latte Mug Recall (October 2026)

Risk of burns and injuries.

WhatTesco F&F Glass Latte Mug
BrandTesco F&F
The hazardThe product presents a risk of burns and injuries as it may shatter during use.
Product typeKitchen/cooking accessories – Mugs/Cups
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-10-05 (notice 2610-0017)

What was recalled

Tall glass latte mug with curved handle.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a risk of burns and injuries as it may shatter during use. If the glass mug breaks while containing a hot beverage, consumers could be burned by the hot liquid or injured by sharp glass fragments.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Tesco.

If you have purchased the product, stop using it immediately and return it to any Tesco store for a full refund. No receipt is required.

For more information, please access the Tesco website at: Tesco recall or contact Tesco Customer Services on 0800 248 123

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2610-0017

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
