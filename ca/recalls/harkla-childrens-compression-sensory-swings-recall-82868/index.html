Harkla Children’s Compression Sensory Swings Recall (October 2026)

Fall hazard.

WhatHarkla Children’s Compression Sensory Swings
BrandHarkla
The hazardfall hazard
CategoryHousehold items
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byImporter Base Products LLC, dba Harkla Boise, Idaho United States of America
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-10-29

What was recalled

This recall involves Harkla Children’s Compression Sensory Swings that have a blue fabric seat and come with a four-hole ceiling hook mounting plate for suspension, with a drop length of 18 to 24 inches. The SKUs that are included in the recall are in the table below. The SKU number is printed on the side of the product packaging.

Why it was recalled

The swing’s ceiling hook can separate from its mounting plate, posing a risk of injury from a fall hazard.

As of October 23, 2026, the company has received 4 reports of the hook separating from the mounting plate and 1 report of injury in Canada. In the United States, the company has received 29 reports of the hook separating from the mounting plate and 17 reports of injuries.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact Harkla to obtain a repair kit. The repair kit consists of a replacement ceiling hook mounting plate, a safety strap and updated swing installation instructions. Consumers will be asked to go to https://harkla.co/pages/ceiling-hook-recall-2026 to register. Consumers should then dispose of the hooks according to municipal waste guidelines.

For more information, consumers can contact Harkla by telephone at 844-442-7552 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. PT Monday through Friday, by email at recall@harkla.co or visit the company website at https://harkla.co/pages/ceiling-hook-recall-2026 or https://harkla.co and click “Recall” at the top of the page. 

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Harkla.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 5,534 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 25,541 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from March 2020 to April 2025.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
