Love to Dream Sleep Machine Recall (September 2026)

Serious injury, death and/or property damage from fire.

WhatLove to Dream Sleep Machine
BrandLove to Dream
The hazardRisk of serious injury, death and/or property damage from fire if the battery overheats and starts a fire. Incidents have occurred with 1 incident causing property damage.
Sold atSee traders list
Where it was soldNationally · International · Online
On sale1 November 2024 - 3 September 2026
Recalled byLove to Dream
Made inChina
ContactLove to Dream Call: 02 5839 5858 Email: recall@lovetodream.com Visit: www.lovetodream.com.au/recall
CategoryLithium-ion batteries
Recall date2026-09-08

What was recalled

Portable rechargeable white noise machine with night light. Includes USB charging cable.

Date codes: 7T12TN and 8E05TN.  

  • Date codes not listed are not affected and not part of this recall. The date code can be found on the side of the Sleep Machine or on the back of the box.

Why it was recalled

The product’s lithium-ion battery can overheat while charging.

What to do

  1. Stop using the affected product immediately.
  2. Disconnect the product from any charger and place in a safe location, away from anything flammable or anything that could damage the product.
  3. Visit the Love to Dream recall page for information on how to dispose of the product and claim your refund or replacement.
  4. Dispose of the product only in accordance with local requirements for lithium-ion products.  Do not place the product in household rubbish or recycling bin.
  5. Contact Love to Dream if you require further information or assistance with this recall.
