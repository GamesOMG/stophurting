Ororo PD10K-Pro Rechargeable Lithium-ion Battery Recall (November 2026)

Fire hazard.

WhatOroro PD10K-Pro Rechargeable Lithium-ion Battery
BrandOroro
The hazardfire hazard
CategoryElectronics
Who it affectsGeneral public
Recalled byManufacturer Dongguan OPAQI Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. Guangdong, China Importer SainStore Technology Co., Ltd Guangdong, China Retailer Langis LLC dba ORORO Las Vegas, Nevada United States
Issued byHealth Canada — Consumer Product Safety
Recall date2026-11-19

What was recalled

This recall involves the Ororo PD10K-Pro Rechargeable Lithium-ion Battery. The product is black in colour. The product displays the model number PD10K-Pro, and the serial numbers 67UXXXX, 685XXXX, 686XXXX or 68AXXXX, on the back of the battery. The products UPC is 840482678300.

Why it was recalled

A potential manufacturing defect may cause the battery to rapidly lose charge and overheat, posing a fire hazard.

As of November 9, 2026, the company has received no reports of incidents or injuries in Canada or the United States.

What to do

Consumers should immediately stop using or charging the recalled product, and safely dispose of the items in such a way that they cannot be used again. Contact the company for a refund.

Contact your municipality for instructions on how to safely transport and dispose of lithium-ion batteries. Batteries are considered hazardous waste. Do not place them in household garbage.

For more information, consumers can contact Langis LLC by telephone at 1-702-751-7639, from 9 am to 6 pm Eastern Time, Monday to Friday, or visit the company website.

Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Langis LLC.

Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.

Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.

This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.

Background

Number Sold

The company reported that 24 units of the affected products were sold in Canada and 269 were sold in the United States.

Time Period Sold

The affected products were sold from September 2026 to October 2026.

Place of Origin

Manufactured in China
