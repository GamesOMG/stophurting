Recall

usOpal Xalirium Portable Bed Rails Recall (October 2026)

Serious Injury or Death from Entrapment and Asphyxiation — about 200 units.

WhatusOpal Xalirium Portable Bed Rails
How manyAbout 200 units
The hazardThe recalled bed rails violate the mandatory standard for adult portable bed rails because when the bed rails are attached to a bed, users can become entrapped within the bed rail or between the bed rail and the side of the mattress, posing a serious entrapment hazard and risk of death by asphyxiation. The bed rails do not meet structural stability or retention strap requirements, posing a fall hazard. In addition, the bed rails do not bear the required hazard warning labels.
Sold atSold Online At: Amazon.com from February 2026 through August 2026 for about $60.
RemedyConsumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact usOpal for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the black storage bag and straps and to cut the foam padding on the bed rails, write “RECALLED” on the upper and lower bed rails, and send a photo of the marked rails to xaliriumofficial@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.
ContactusOpal by email at xaliriumofficial@outlook.com
Recall date2026-10-15 (recall no. 27029)

What was recalled

This recall involves Xalirium-branded adult portable bed rails. They have a white frame with black foam rubber grips and a black fabric pouch. The bed rails measure about 17.15 inches high, 4.33 inches wide and 1.99 inches long. "Xalirium" is printed on the label of the product packaging. Only bed rails without vertical bars inside the opening are included in this recall.

What to do

Stop using the product. The listed remedy is: Consumers should stop using the recalled adult portable bed rails immediately and contact usOpal for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to cut the black storage bag and straps and to cut the foam padding on the bed rails, write “RECALLED” on the upper and lower bed rails, and send a photo of the marked rails to xaliriumofficial@outlook.com. Consumers should then dispose of the recalled product.. usOpal by email at xaliriumofficial@outlook.com
