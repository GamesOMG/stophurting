Recall

TJ Morris Toy Story Water Bottle sold by Home Bargains Recall (August 2026)

Choking hazard.

WhatTJ Morris Toy Story Water Bottle sold by Home Bargains
BrandTJ Morris
The hazardThe product presents a choking hazard because the spout can detach from the lid, creating a small part which may obstruct a child’s airway.
Product typeHobby/sports equipment - Drinking Bottles
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-08-03 (notice 2607-0240)

What was recalled

Blue water bottle with red lid, decorated with characters from the film Toy Story. Only the batch numbers above are in scope of the current recall.

Why it was recalled

The product presents a choking hazard because the spout can detach from the lid, creating a small part. If this occurs during use, the spout may block a child’s airway and cause them to choke.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

The product has been recalled from end users by Home Bargains.

We recommend that you stop using the product immediately and keep it out of reach of young children. Return it to your nearest Home Bargains for a refund. For questions or more information, please access: TJ Morris Toy Story Water Bottle - sold at Home Bargains – Home Bargains Help Centre

Additional information

PSD case number: 2607-0240

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
