Britax Römer Baby Safe Pro Infant Carrier Recall (November 2026)

Affected batches of the product present a risk of injuries.

WhatBritax Römer Baby Safe Pro Infant Carrier
BrandBritax Römer
The hazardAffected batches of the product present a risk of injuries because the harness buckle may release or break when installed on the base in the reclined position.
Product typeChildcare articles and children’s equipment – Baby Car/Booster Seats
Made inChina
Issued byOffice for Product Safety and Standards
Recall date2026-11-19 (notice 2611-0130)

What was recalled

Infant carriers sold on their own or as a set together with the VARIO BASE 5Z ISOFIX base. Only specified batches of the product are affected. Access Annex A in the attached PDF for instructions on identifying if you have an affected product.

Why it was recalled

Affected batches of the product present a risk of injuries because the harness buckle may release or break when installed on the base in the reclined position. This means the child may not be properly restrained and may slip from the carrier and receive injuries.

The product does not meet the requirements of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

What to do

Affected batches of the product have been recalled from end users by Britax Römer. Owners of a Baby-safe Pro manufactured between 26 December 2025 and 30 September 2026 are advised to visit www.baby-safe-check.com to check if their seat is affected. For support on identifying the manufacturing date, please see Annex A overleaf.

For questions or concerns, please contact Britax at hello@britax.com.

Additional information

PSD notification number: 2611-0130

Notified by: Local Authority Trading Standards
